1.1.3 for Minecraft 26.3

## Changes

- Earning a mod advancement now writes it in chat: who made the advancement, reached the goal, or completed the challenge, and the reward under that line (the items and the experience).
- The advancements tab is always named Evolving Threats, in every language.
- A regular spider bites for 2 hearts. A matriarch bites for 4. Neither bite grows with the day.
- Hostile mobs no longer pile up beside the one that just spawned. From day 40 the night can hold more of them at once, and the game places them in its own dark spots across the spawn area. That night is still almost the usual number. By day 100 a full moon is about twice as many (about 140 instead of 70 around one player); a new moon is about 1.7 times.


1.1.2 for Minecraft 26.3

## Changes

- Sleep is judged on the floor where the bed stands. A mine, a cellar, and the fences around the bottom of a tower do not change a bed upstairs. Those fences count again when the bed is on their floor.
- An upper floor is not a safe room. The night outside its window or wall sets the chance, even when there is no stair down to the ground.
- Every 2 points halves the chance of being woken: 0 is 100%, 2 is 50%, 4 is 25%, 6 is 12%, 8 is 6%. A wooden door with night outside is about 50%. One stone wall is about 25%.


1.0.2 for Minecraft 26.3

## Changes

- Vindicators no longer lose a target that hides in a wooden house or behind glass. They chop through the wood and the glass until they can see it again.
- Ravagers no longer lose a target hidden behind a wall they can break. They keep headbutting through the pause between hits until they can see it again. Obsidian and bedrock still make them give up.
- Ravager headbutt cooldown is 10–15 seconds, down from 20–25.
- Sleeping no longer skips every night. Open ground always wakes you. Torches, fences, doors, and walls lower the chance. A lit, fenced base inside 60 blocks rarely does. A failed attempt does not skip the night, and that same night cannot be tried again. The mobs appear beside the bed, and the door or two blocks of the inner wall break.


1.0.1 for Minecraft 26.3

## Changes

- Super-fast creepers appear only from day 40. The chance is unchanged, 3%.
- Creepers that walk slowly, then sprint at 6–7 blocks, also appear only from day 40. The chance is unchanged, 0.3%.
- Floating weapons appear less often at night in the Overworld: 2% instead of 5%. The Nether chance is still 5%.


1.0.0 for Minecraft 26.3


First release of **Evolving Threats** for Minecraft 26.3.

Hostile mobs grow more dangerous as the world ages, across about 100 in-game days. The full list of changes is in [Features](https://github.com/megaskumbria/Evolving-Threats-Mod/blob/v1.0.0/FEATURES.md).

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- Fabric API
- Java 25

## Install

Place `evolving-threats-1.0.0.jar` in the `mods` folder.
