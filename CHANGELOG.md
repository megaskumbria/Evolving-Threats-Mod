1.0.2 for Minecraft 26.3

## Changes

- Vindicators no longer lose a target that hides in a wooden house or behind glass. They chop through the wood and the glass until they can see it again.
- Ravagers no longer lose a target hidden behind a wall they can break. They keep headbutting through the pause between hits until they can see it again. Obsidian and bedrock still make them give up.
- Ravager headbutt cooldown is 10–15 seconds, down from 20–25.


1.0.1 for Minecraft 26.3

## Changes

- Super-fast creepers appear only from day 40. The chance is unchanged, 3%.
- Creepers that walk slowly, then sprint at 6–7 blocks, also appear only from day 40. The chance is unchanged, 0.3%.
- Floating weapons appear less often at night in the Overworld: 2% instead of 5%. The Nether chance is still 5%.


1.0.0 for Minecraft 26.3


First release of **Evolving Threats** for Minecraft 26.3.

Hostile mobs grow more dangerous as the world ages, across about 100 in-game days. The full list of changes is in [Features](https://github.com/megaskumbria/Evolving-Threats-Mod/blob/v1.0.0/FEATURES.md).

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- Fabric API
- Java 25

## Install

Place `evolving-threats-1.0.0.jar` in the `mods` folder.
