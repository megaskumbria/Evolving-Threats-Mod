## Mob changes and abilities

### Phantoms

- Phantoms can spawn in significantly larger numbers.
- They deal damage comparable to the damage they inflicted in the early versions following their introduction to the game.
- Rare Phantom variants can inflict **Weakness** or **Blindness** when attacking players.

### Blazes

- A special Blaze projectile can remove the **Fire Resistance** effect from players.

### Zombies

- Each Zombie has a random amount of health, from **20 to 40 HP**.
- They can spawn equipped with various weapons, armor, shields, and even Totems of Undying.
- Some Zombies can spawn with positive potion effects.
- They can break blocks that obstruct their path.
- They can detect nearby light sources, move towards them, and destroy them.
- Zombies wearing pumpkins on their heads attract nearby Zombies of the same type.
- Some Zombies spawn holding TNT, which they use to destroy obstacles.
- Rare Zombies can spawn with a spawner on their heads. Every second hit they receive summons two additional minions.
- Some Zombies can steal items directly from the player's hands.

**Rare variants**

- **Zombie Sorcerer** — launches players into the air, sets them on fire, and pulls them towards itself.
- **Rotten Overseer** — a mini-boss that relentlessly pursues the player regardless of their location. It can summon reinforcements and deals devastating damage using two axes.
- **Raid Anchor** — a mini-boss accompanied by a squad of **12–15** well-equipped minions. As long as the Anchor remains alive, its minions can detect the player's location.
- **Excalibur** — a rare Zombie wielding an incredibly sharp sword.
- **Mjolnir** — a rare Zombie wielding a mace with extremely powerful knockback.

### Skeletons

- Each Skeleton has a random amount of health, from **20 to 40 HP**.
- They can spawn equipped with various weapons, armor, shields, and even Totems of Undying.
- Some Skeletons can spawn with positive potion effects.
- They can spawn riding horses.
- They use arrows with various harmful effects.
- They can wield both melee weapons and bows simultaneously, choosing the appropriate weapon depending on the distance to the player.
- Some Skeletons spawn wearing pumpkins on their heads and attack players with flaming arrows.
- Skeletons with dispensers on their heads can unleash a barrage of arrows at players.
- Baby Skeletons can spawn alongside regular ones.
- Some Skeletons spawn with a spawner on their heads. Every second hit they receive summons two additional minions.
- Some Skeletons possess enchantments with levels exceeding standard vanilla limits. These enchantments cannot be obtained as loot.
- When hit by an arrow, there is a chance that the item currently held by the player will be knocked out of their hand.
- Some Skeletons use arrows that each inflict a random effect.

**Rare variants**

- **Skeleton Captain** — spawns accompanied by its own bodyguards.
- **Tactical Skeleton** — charges directly at the player after hitting them with an arrow carrying a harmful effect.
- **Basilisk** — equipped with an incredibly powerful bow and sturdy armor.
- **Sniper Skeleton** — attacks players from extremely long distances.

### Wither Skeletons

- They can spawn with positive potion effects.
- Some Wither Skeletons spawn with a spawner on their heads. Every second hit they receive summons two additional minions.
- They can wield both stone and Netherite swords.
- Their swords have a significantly higher chance of dropping with powerful enchantments.

### Creepers

- Each Creeper has a random amount of health, from **20 to 30 HP**.
- They can explode even when the player attempts to hide behind blocks.
- Some Creepers throw blocks of TNT at players.
- A single Creeper explosion can completely disable a player's shield.
- They can spawn in a charged state.
- There is a chance for an extremely fast Creeper to spawn.
- Some Creepers can suddenly accelerate, triggering an almost instantaneous explosion.

**Rare variants**

- **Magnet Creeper** — pulls players towards itself.

### Spiders

- Each Spider has a random amount of health, from **16 to 30 HP**.
- Some Spiders can place cobwebs directly underneath the player's feet.
- They can spawn in large groups and attack players in swarms.

**Rare variants**

- **Spider Queen** — increased health, accompanied by a group of Cave Spiders.

### Slimes

- Slimes can poison players upon contact or attack. The duration and severity of the poison depend on the Slime's size.

### Witches

- A rare Witch variant can throw a special white potion that completely removes all effects from the player.
- If the player is too far away, a Witch may drink an **Invisibility** potion.
- Witches have access to a significantly expanded arsenal of potions, allowing them to use a much wider variety of effects against players.
- They can throw Lingering Potions.

### Vindicators

- They can spawn wielding diamond axes.
- They can quickly destroy wooden blocks and fences to clear a path towards the player.
- Some Vindicators can spawn wearing armor.

### Vexes

- Their attack damage is increased.
- They can inflict **Weakness** upon hitting players.

### Drowned

- They can spawn wielding tridents enchanted with **Channeling**. Every successful hit on a player is guaranteed to summon a lightning strike, even during clear weather.
- Some Drowned wear turtle shell helmets that provide additional damage resistance.
- While underwater, they regenerate **2 HP** every **3 seconds**.
- They can spawn with fishing rods and use them to hook players, pulling them closer before attacking.

### Ravagers

- They can break through even sturdy walls and complex structures, destroying obstacles in their path.

### Shulkers

- Rare Shulker variants fire projectiles that inflict **Slowness**.
- Some Shulkers can fire two projectiles in quick succession.
- They can teleport to adjacent blocks significantly more frequently than usual.
- Some of their projectiles inflict **Levitation** for an extended duration.
- A rare Shulker variant can teleport players a short distance when its projectile hits them.
- When striking a Shulker's shell, the player's currently equipped tool or weapon has a chance to lose up to **50%** of its durability.

### Pillagers

- They can load their crossbows with fireworks instead of regular bolts.
- Some Pillagers have crossbows enchanted with high-level enchantments.
- They can spawn wearing armor.

### Piglin Brutes

- Health is increased to **60 HP** (vanilla is 50).
- A single hit can destroy **50%** of the durability of any armor piece, regardless of its enchantments. The attack also inflicts **Nausea** for a short duration.

### Magma Cubes

- They can set players on fire when attacking.
- They leave blocks of scorching magma beneath themselves, creating dangerous areas.
- They have increased health and deal more damage.
- **Overheat** — the cube enters an aggressive phase: it moves significantly faster and deals considerably more damage.

### Endermen

- During combat, they can instantly swap positions with the player, unexpectedly changing the positions of both combatants.

### Cave Spiders

- Cave Spider bites inflict **Poison** for a significantly longer duration. The effect can kill players, just as it did in earlier versions of the game.

## Other features and mechanics

- Many mob variants can spawn with positive potion effects, significantly enhancing their combat capabilities.
- Hostile flying tools and weapons can appear throughout the world and attack players on their own.
- Hostile variants of Iron Golems can spawn alongside regular ones.
- Vindicators, Pillagers, and even Ravagers can appear near the player's base, creating an additional threat to their settlement.
- Special ritual groups can appear throughout the world, each possessing unique abilities and behavioral characteristics.
- Player bases can be subjected to full-scale sieges involving waves of Zombies and Skeletons. Players cannot sleep during these nights.
- A bed skips the night only when the place is hard for the night to reach. Open ground always wakes the player. Torches, fences, doors, and thicker walls lower the chance. One or two zombies or skeletons appear beside the bed and one of them hits. A door or two blocks of the inner wall break. The night keeps going, and that night cannot be slept through again.
- Mobs have an increased detection range, allowing them to spot players from much greater distances.
- Hitting a Zombie's or Skeleton's shield with an axe temporarily disables it, following the same mechanics used against Vindicators.
- When sleeping outdoors, mobs can wake you up. The chance of being woken up depends on the light level within a 60-block radius, wall thickness, and the presence of fences.

**Named threats and traits**

- **Bastion** — a rare and extremely dangerous enemy with an enormous amount of health and complete immunity to knockback.
- **Mirror** — any attack against a creature with this trait reflects the damage it receives back onto the attacking player.
- **Spiky** — incredibly durable armor with thorns. Attacking such a creature causes the player to receive significant reflected damage.
