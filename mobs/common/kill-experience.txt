KILL EXPERIENCE
================

Type: any hostile mob. The game calls them monsters:
     zombies, skeletons, creepers, spiders, endermen, witches, brutes, slimes,
     magma cubes, phantoms, blazes, pillagers, and the rest of this list.
     Cows, pigs, villagers, and iron golem are not included.
     Floating tool and damaged golem are included: the mod registered them as monsters.
Code: MobExperience.java (multiplier),
     mixin/MobExperienceMixin.java (on death).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. XP is MULTIPLIER times what vanilla would give. Currently 2.
   FIXED: does not depend on day, threat scale, or random health.
2. Bare mob gives 10 instead of usual 5.
   If it has weapon or armor, vanilla adds 1–3 XP per such item.
   That bonus is doubled too. Zombie with one sword gives 12–16.
   Overseer and raid anchor too: was 5, becomes 10, plus bonus for axes and armor.
3. Baby zombie already gives more in vanilla: 5 becomes 12, then mod doubles to 24.
   Extra gear counts the same.
4. XP drops only if a player killed the mob. That is vanilla behavior.
5. Custom numbers are unchanged; multiplier does not touch them:
   Excalibur — 30, Mjölnir — 20, sorcerer — 15, ritual band — 50, Basilisk — 100, Bastion — 200,
   “700 IQ” skeleton — 30, wither skeleton — 25.
6. Floating tool is coded as 5; after multiplier drops 10.
   Damaged golem is coded as 10; after multiplier drops 20.


SETTINGS
---------
  MobExperience.java
    ENABLED ...... true — off returns vanilla XP
    MULTIPLIER ... 2 — how many times. 1 = no bonus, 3 = bare mob gives 15
