Siege
=============

Type: a rare night-event in the upper world. Not a separate mob and not a "Raid Anchor".
Code: siege/SiegeNight.java (schedule, bans, texts, sounds),
     siege/SiegeComposition.java (who will come: factions, species, random fractions),
     siege/SiegeWaves.java (waves and a hike to the player),
     siege/SiegeData.java (what the world remembers), siege/SiegeEvents.java (connection).
Links to the game: mixin/SiegeMobMixin.java (goals and mark of the siege in mobs),
     mixin/EntityPortalSiegeMixin.java, spawn/NaturalMonsterSwap.java.
     Settings — in the "SETTINGS" blocks.


ESSENCE
----
Once every 10–15 game days (by chance) comes the night of the siege: until dawn in the upper world
only mobs of the same faction wander, go in waves from afar and hunt the player wherever he is.

Two fractions, in one siege there is only one, they do not mix:
  • UNDEAD and CREATURES: zombies (all species), skeletons (all species), desiccants, creepers,
    spiders (and cavemen), witches, phantoms.
    There are no drowned people or slugs under siege.
  • ILLAGERS: robbers, champions, callers, ruiners.
Illager siege is possible from the 40th day of the scale (ThreatTable.SIEGE_ILLAGER_DAY), 25% chance
(SiegeComposition.ILLAGER_SIEGE_CHANCE) Until the 40th day, all sieges are made of undead and critters.

Dryers (skeletons-dryers) fall into a random composition only if someone in this world
i've already been to Hell. One visit of any player is enough: the world remembers it and does not forget it.
While no one has been to Hell, other types of undead and creatures are chosen instead of dryers.
Check at the time of warning. Go to Hell after it — this siege will not receive desiccants,
the next one will.
The old campaign also counts: the game has an achievement for entering Hell.
Disable check: SiegeComposition.WITHER_SKELETONS_REQUIRE_NETHER = false.
The siege start wither_skeleton command does not check so that the view can be viewed in advance.

The composition is selected once when the warning begins, and lasts until dawn:
  1. How many species: one (15%), two (50%) or three (35%).
  2. Which species are from the faction: by chance, each species has its own weight (more often zombies and skeletons, less often phantoms).
  3. The shares of the selected species are random, in the amount of 100%. For example: 90% of crippers and 10% of zombies;
     45% spiders, 35% cripplers, 20% skeletons; 100% zombies.
The composition is visible in /evolvingthreats siege status.


STAGES
-----
1. On the morning of the day before the siege (optional) — a vague hint in the chat, with no exact date.
2. 2 minutes before the night — a scary chat message, a bell and moans in the distance.
   From now on, you can not sleep and enter the portals to Hell and End.
3. Night (from 13,000 to 23,000 ticks) — waves are coming.
4. Dawn — a chat message, everything comes back.

No headers, rows above the panel, boss bars and sounds when waves appear.


PROHIBITIONS
-------
Sleep
  Prohibited from warning (2 minutes before the night) until dawn: otherwise you can sleep
  in the evening and skip the siege. Anyone who has already been lying in bed is lifted out of it.
  Trying to lie down is a scary chat message.
Portals
  The player in the upper world cannot enter the portal in Hell or End. A player who is already in Hell or End,
  may return. Message no more than once every 10 seconds.


WHAT MOBS
----------
• Vanilla night spawn: any monster is replaced with a mob from the siege, in the same proportions.
  So an ordinary spawn does not dilute the siege by strangers. Cadavra, Wanderers, Swamp & Desert Skeletons
  are picked by biome. Phantoms from vanilla night spawn (if the player has not slept) are untouched;
  in the siege, they go as a mob of the composition only if there are phantoms in it.
• Mobs from the squad, which appeared themselves from the warning until dawn, also go to the player.
• Go with a separate goal: the game itself forgets the player if he is not visible from afar, and rubs the way by wandering.
  Phantoms are guided only by the target (they fly to it themselves), the rest are also segments of the path.
• Everything else from mobs.txt works for these mobs (armor, weapons, dynamite, head spavner,
  charged and fast creepers, spider uterus…). Helpers, whom the mob calls itself, do not go
  to the player specifically (for example, swarm cave spiders).
• No minibosses: supervisor, basilisk, skeleton captain, bastion, ritual packs.
  There are also no robber patrols, floating tools, damaged golems.
  The "road anchor" does not appear on the night of the siege (only on command).


Waves
-----
• The group appears on the surface in 50–70 blocks from the player (wave without sound).
  Phantoms appear in the air at a height of 10–18 blocks.
• Size 8–15 in the "price" of mobs: a regular mob costs 1, a caller 2, a ruiner 3
  (SiegeComposition.Kind) At the beginning of the night, the waves are weaker (×0.6), at midnight they come from both sides,
  before dawn one large final wave (×1.5).
• Pause between waves 60–90 seconds. Before the first wave, 30 seconds after the start of the night.
• No more than 80 live mobs of siege around one player (MAX_ALIVE_PER_PLAYER).
• Mobs receive the player with a target and a large viewing radius; if the path is too long, they go in segments
  24 blocks for each player. While walking from afar, looking at the player and holding the battle rack
  (the zombies have their hands up, the illagers have weapons in their hands), not a walk. They hit and shoot only in the vicinity.
• Zombies and skeletons in the wave are taken from the list of biome monsters (censers in the desert, wanderers in the snow,
  swamp skeletons in the swamp). If there are no such in the biome — ordinary.
• Mob tag — evolvingthreats_siege tag. Outside the siege, the tagged mob behaves as normal.


IN-GAME TEST
-----------
Commands (cheats / OP):
  /evolvingthreats siege status    — when is the next, at what stage and what is the composition of mobs
  /evolvingthreats siege warn      — Warning now; night starts in 2 minutes
  /evolvingthreats siege start     — the night of the siege immediately (during the day, mobs in the sun burn: this is normal)
  /evolvingthreats siege warn|start undead   — the same, but always undead and creatures
  /evolvingthreats siege warn|start illagers — the same, but necessarily illagers (day 40 is not checked)
  /evolvingthreats siege start is a 100% siege from one species to test it separately.
    Species: zombie, skeleton, wither_skeleton, creeper, spider, witch, phantom,
          pillager, vindicator, evoker, ravager.
    wither_skeleton doesn't check if someone has been to Hell.
    warn <kind> — same, but warning first (night in 2 minutes).
    Witches, Summoners and Ruiners have a limit (6 / 3 / 3). When it's full,
    the remaining wave mobs are replaced with a zombie or a champion.
  /evolvingthreats siege stop      — stop the siege, the next one is reassigned
  /evolvingthreats siege in — assign a siege after so many days (0 = on the night of today)

Fast way to complete a run: /evolvingthreats siege in 0, then /time set 10000
(up to 10600 — warning, up to 13000 — waiting for the night). Time can be moved further with the/time command.


WHAT TO CHANGE
----------
SiegeNight.java:
  Days_MIN / days_MAX ............ how rare (10–15 days)
  NIGHT_START_TICK / DAWN_TICK ... when the night starts and ends
  WARNING_BEFORE_NIGHT_TICKS ..... how much warning (2400 = 2 minutes)
  OMEN_ENABLED, OMEN_DAYS_BEFORE . vague morning hint
  BELL_*, moan_*, SOUND_* ........ bell and moans
  *_MESSAGES and *_MESSAGES_EN .... texts in the chat: Russian and English.
                                 The language is taken from the player's game settings (ru_… — Russian, the rest is English).
SiegeComposition.java:
  Illager_SIEGE_CHANCE ........... chance of Illager siege (0.25 = 25%)
  WITHER_SKELETONS_REQUIRE_netter  true — dryers in a random siege only after Hell.
                                   false — dryers can fall out without Hell.
  ONE_KIND_CHANCE, TWO_KINDS_CHANCE  chance of one and two species (otherwise three)
  SHARE_FLOOR .................... how even the shares are (0 = completely random)
  Kind (list of species at the top) ..... weight (how often the species drops out), price in the wave and max for each species.
                                   max = how many mobs of the species can be alive around one player.
                                   Now: summoners 3, ruiners 3, witches 6, others unlimited (0).
                                   When the limit is exhausted, another species from the lineup is taken instead
                                   (if there are no others — a champion or a zombie).
  CAVE_SPIDER_FRACTION, PHANTOM_HEIGHT_* .. the proportion of cave spiders, the height of the appearance of phantoms
ThreatTable.java:
  SIEGE_illager_DAY .............. from which day the Illager siege is possible (40)
SiegeWaves.java:
  WAVE_SIZE_*, WAVE_PAUSE_*, MAX_ALIVE_PER_PLAYER, SPAWN_DISTANCE_*, EARLY_*, TWO_SIDES_FROM,
  FINAL_WAVE_*, MARCH_*, FOLLOW_RANGE
  Include_CREATIVE_PLAYERS ....... true — the player in the creative mode is also the target of the siege (convenient for verification);
                                   false — it is not there for the siege: there will be no waves next to it, the mobs are standing


Known Limitations
---------------------
• Mobs are conducted only in loaded chunks (approximately 8–10 chunks around the player).
• Siege mobs do not receive a constant “do not disappear” label: far from the player, they can disappear themselves.
• A player who has spent all night in Hell will not receive a siege: portals are closed only at the entrance.
• Dawn after a real siege night grants «До рассвета» to players alive in the overworld.
  An illager mix also grants «Чужая осада». /siege stop does not grant either.
  Code: advancement/ModAdvancements.java
