SHIELDS ON ZOMBIES AND SKELETONS
=======================

Type: shared mechanic for normal zombies, skeletons, and strays.
Code: MobShields.java (settings, pattern, block chance),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java and mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (grant on spawn),
     mixin/LivingEntityMobShieldMixin.java (hit moment).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn chance SPAWN_CHANCE (25%) to get shield in off hand.
2. If off hand is taken (skeleton sword beside bow) — no shield, weapon is not removed.
3. Random base color and 6 flag pattern layers on shield. Many patterns, 16 colors each,
   six layers — millions of shields. Pattern visible in hand and kept if shield drops.
4. Melee or projectile hit on such mob has BLOCK_CHANCE (10%) to be fully negated.
   Shield block sound plays. Fall, lava, and void are not blocked.
5. Drowned, piglins, overseer, and raid mobs do not get shield this way.
6. Shield drops at DROP_CHANCE (~8.5%), like armor.


SETTINGS (MobShields.java)
---------------------------
  ENABLED ............. true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ........ 0.25 — shield chance (25%)
  BLOCK_CHANCE ........ 0.10 — negate hit chance (10%)
  PATTERN_LAYERS ...... 6 — pattern layers. 0 = color only
  DROP_CHANCE ......... 0.085 — drop chance
