SPIKY
=====

Type: a rare normal zombie or normal skeleton. Not a new mob in the menu.
Code: spiky/Spiky.java (chance, hearts, armor piece),
     mixin/SpikyMixin.java (remembers Spiky),
     mixin/SpikyHitMixin.java (suppresses vanilla Thorns, counter-stab),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java and mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (chance on spawn).

Bastion, overseer, anchor, sorcerer, Excalibur, Mjölnir, dynamite, ritual,
Basilisk, captain, and “700 IQ” do not become Spiky. Small skeleton — no.
Bastion and Spiky never on one mob.


HOW IT LOOKS
------------
Name above head: Spiky. Visible only when crosshair is on it and within ~64 blocks.
One random armor piece always has Thorns. Level on item — THORNS_LEVEL (currently III).
Level does not affect stab strength: hearts are set separately.
Other three slots — like normal zombie or skeleton: empty, armor, or special head.
If no armor at all, one piece is still equipped.


HOW IT ACTS
-------------
1. Player hit, arrow, or trident that lands — one counter-stab, not per piece.
   Removes 7 or 8 hearts. Armor, Protection, and raised shield do not reduce it.
   Same ring as vanilla Thorns damage. No ring if stab did not apply.
2. Golden apple absorption hearts go first, then red.
   Resistance potion still weakens the hit.
3. Fire, explosion, and foreign Thorns do not trigger stab.
4. Vanilla Thorns from its armor are not added: otherwise hit would stack twice.
5. If you take that armor and wear it, it pokes weakly like normal Thorns.
6. Name does not prevent despawn by itself.
7. Chance 0.2% from day 40. FIXED: moon and later days do not change it.
   None before day 40.


WHO IS EXCLUDED
--------
Baby, husk, drowned, zombie villager, stray, swamp and desert skeleton.
Does not appear on siege night. Summoned reinforcements and /summon skip chance.
Spawner rolls.


COMMAND
-------
  /etspiky                 — zombie
  /etspiky skeleton        — skeleton
  /evolvingthreats spawn spiky
  /evolvingthreats spawn spiky skeleton
Day and chance not checked. Spawns 6 blocks along look.
Operator rights required.


WHAT TO TUNE
----------
Spiky.java:
  ENABLED .......... false — no new spawns, command silent too
  SPAWN_CHANCE ..... 0.002 = 0.2%. 0 = never natural. 0.01 = 1%
  HEARTS_MIN ....... 7 — fewer hearts per stab. Both ends inclusive
  HEARTS_MAX ....... 8 — more hearts per stab
  THORNS_LEVEL ..... 3 — text on piece. 0 = no enchant text, still stabs
ThreatTable.java:
  SPIKY_DAY ........ 40 — first day for natural spawn
