RANDOM HEALTH
==================

Type: zombie, husk, zombie villager, drowned, skeleton, stray,
     spider, cave spider, creeper.
     Enderman excluded: always 40 like vanilla.
Code: MobHealth.java (ranges),
     mixin/MobPotionBuffExtraMixin.java (all except cave spider),
     mixin/MobPotionBuffMixin.java (cave spider).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn health is a random integer from min to max inclusive.
   Endpoints count: 20 and 40 can roll.
2. Vanilla without mod: zombie and skeleton 20, spider 16, cave spider 12, creeper 20, enderman 40.
   Cave spider also gets 16–30, so never weaker than normal spider.
3. Not re-rolled when the world loads.
4. Unchanged: enderman (always 40), zombified piglin, wither skeleton, swamp and desert skeletons.
   Rotten overseer stays 100, spider matriarch 40, raid anchor 100, Basilisk 100.
   Bastion stays 1000.
   Small skeleton first gets 20–40, then its own 10–16 (SmallSkeleton).
   “700 IQ” skeleton first gets 20–40, then its own 20–35 (TacticianSkeleton).
5. Kill XP for any hostile mob is doubled. Details: kill-experience.txt.

    CREEPER_MIN ................ 20
    CREEPER_MAX ................ 30
