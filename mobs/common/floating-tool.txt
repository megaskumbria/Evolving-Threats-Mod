Floating Instrument
==================

Type: separate flying mob. It looks like a sword, axe, shovel, pickaxe or hoe.
Code: tool/FloatingTool.java (settings, flight, impact, command)
mixin/NaturalSpawnerFloatingToolMixin.java
entity/ai/FloatingToolAttackGoal.java (pause between beats)
client/tool/FloatingToolRenderer.java (picture: spins at rest, looks at the target in attack).
Charm: MobEnchantments.java. Potion: MobPotionBuffs.java.
What item in the ordinary world: RandomWeapons.java (without neserite).
In hell, the subject is chosen by FloatingTool itself: Neserite.


How it works
------------
1. In the ordinary world, he appears in the same place where zombies and skeletons: dark, no closer than 24 blocks to the player.
Out of every 100 such appearances, approximately 2 are this instrument (NATURAL SPAWN CHANCE = 0.02).
The shot is common to a damaged golem: the next 0.2% is a golem, not this instrument.
He doesn't appear in the End. He sees a player, he goes to him.
2. He is in hell at any time, not just at night. FIXED, 5% hostile appearances
(NETHER SPAWN CHANCE) Instead of gast, magma cube and other monsters.
The spawners of the fortress and the team do not make this throw. /ettool in hell does not give a chance
But it's still nonseritic, because it came from hell.
3. In the ordinary world, the object is random: wood, stone, gold, copper, iron or diamond.
The kind of instrument is also random. No Neserite.
In hell it always does not matter: sword, axe, shovel, pickaxe or hoe, equally.
All at Fire III Conspiracy. Hellish has no other charms.
4. He flies to the player and hits hard. No way through walls.
While chasing and hitting, the picture does not rotate around its axis - it looks at the target.
When there is no goal, it spins again like an abandoned thing.
5. Damage is taken from the object: the hoe is weak, the diamond sword is strong.
Plus a small "fist" BASE ATTACK DAMAGE.
6. Health depends on the material: wood and gold are weak, diamond is tenacious, it does not hold even stronger.
7. In an ordinary world with the chance of ENCHANT CHANCE, the subject has the same charm as a zombie weapon.
The sharpness, throwback and fiery aspect work in impact.
Efficiency, luck, silk touch only on the fallen object.
In hell, there is no enchantment from this list: only Conspiracy of Fire III.
8. Potions are the same as zombies (MobPotionBuffs): 0 to 5 effects while the mob is alive.
Strength hurts, regeneration heals, fire resistance extinguishes fire damage.
There is no invisibility; it would hide the instrument itself. Particles of another potion give it away.
Speed and jump almost do not change flight: in the air it flies at its own speed.
9. The tool doesn't break in battle. Otherwise, the mob would be without a picture.
10. After death, an object from the ordinary world falls with the chance of DROP CHANCE, along with the spell.
Hellish Neserite never falls. Extraction does not bypass: it adds nothing to zero chance.
11. In a peaceful regime disappears like other hostile mobs.
