MIRROR
================

Type: a rare tag on a normal mob. Not a new mob in the menu.
Code: mirror/Mirror.java (chance, name, hit return),
     mixin/MirrorMixin.java (remembers tag),
     mixin/MirrorHitMixin.java (moment when hit has landed),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java, mixin/SkeletonMixin.java, mixin/SpiderRoleMixin.java,
     mixin/PiglinBruteHealthMixin.java, mixin/MobPotionBuffExtraMixin.java (chance on spawn).


WHO
---
Normal zombie, skeleton, wither skeleton, blaze, spider, piglin, brutal piglin,
enderman, silverfish, creeper, witch.
Not husk, drowned, stray, cave spider.
If the mob already has a custom name (Bastion, Spiky, Basilisk, captain, overseer, etc.),
this chance is not rolled.


HOW IT ACTS
-------------
1. Name above head: Mirror. Visible only when crosshair is on it and within ~64 blocks.
2. Melee hit (hand, sword, axe, etc.) returns to the player.
   Same number vanilla computed before mob armor: Sharpness, Strength, crit already inside.
   Then player armor and Protection reduce it. Mirror’s armor does not affect the return.
3. Arrow, explosion, fire, and thorns are not returned. Thorns on mob armor still poke weakly separately.
4. Name does not prevent despawn by itself.
5. Chance 0.3% from day one. FIXED: moon and scale do not change it.
   Can appear on siege night too: not a mini-boss, a rare trait on these mobs.


COMMAND
-------
  /etmirror
  /etmirror skeleton | wither | blaze | spider | piglin | brute | enderman | silverfish | creeper | witch
  /evolvingthreats spawn mirror
  /evolvingthreats spawn mirror <same word>
No word — zombie. Spawns 6 blocks along look. Does not roll chance.
Operator rights required.


SETTINGS (Mirror.java)
-----------------------
  ENABLED .......... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ..... 0.003 — 0.3%
  NAME ............. Mirror
