Magic on Weapons, Instruments and Mob Armour
==========================================

Type: General mechanics for zombies, skeletons, wanderers, robbers and thugs.
Weapons and tools: sword, axe, pickax, shovel, hoe, bow, crossbow.
Armor: helmet, breastplate, leggings, boots of zombies, skeletons, wanderers, pillagers, and vindicators.
   On an illager each piece is rolled alone, so the set is not enchanted all at once.
Code: MobEnchantments.java (settings and all logic)
RandomArmor.java (isModArmor: what is considered fashion armor)
mixin/ZombieMixin.java, mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (a challenge for zombies and skeletons)
mixin/IllagerWeaponEnchantMixin.java (robber with crossbow and thug with axe),
mixin/PillagerRaidEnchantMixin.java (the crossbow of the robber is already in the raid).


How it works
------------
1. A mob appears and receives weapons and armor (as before, see RandomWeapons.java and RandomArmor.java).
2. Every item has a chance to be charmed: the weapon defaults 30%, each piece of armor 25%.
3. If it falls out - from 1 to 5 chars (weapons) or from 1 to 4 (armor), most often 1-2.
Only suitable for this subject: the sword and the axe - sharpness, a fiery conspiracy ...; pickaxe - efficiency, luck ... ;
bow - force, flame ...; crossbow - fast recharging, multi-charged ... ;
helmet - underwater breathing, submariner; shoes - weightlessness, underwater walking; knife - agility, etc.
4. Incompatible charms do not come together (as in ordinary Minecraft): sharpness / heavenly punishment / scourge - only one;
Protection/protection from fire/explosion/projectiles is only one; silk touch and luck are one of the two, etc.
5. Charm level is from I to maximum, but high levels are rare: sharpness V occurs on a wooden sword.
protection IV on a leather helmet, but rarely fall out (about 2-3% of items with this charm).
Most often there will be protection I or II. One "protection IV" on a piece of armor is a possible option.
6. The robber in the raid gets the same throw on the crossbow, after the spell of the game itself.
If the throw does not fall, there is a quick reload of the raid (I or II, in late waves).
The thug in the raid and the rotten overseer is still with his spell of play.
7. Special "heads" (spawner block, beacon pumpkin, distributor) are not fascinated.
8. Such an object falls off the mob with the usual chance (about 8.5%), the charm is preserved.
Exception: A bow with a folding V or VI never falls. See mobs/skeletons/punch-bow.txt.

What works for the mob in combat:
- weapons: sharpness, fiery plot, discard, force, flame, rapid reload, etc.
Armor: all types of protection (mob harder to kill), thorns (beat the player back), weightlessness,
underwater walking and the speed of the soul.
Heavenly punishment and the scourge of arthropod damage to the player do not increase - they are just a beautiful loot.
Extraction, efficiency, luck, silk touch, strength, repair, curse, underwater breathing, submariner,
Agility: the mob is not affected, needed as a valuable lute for the player.
The curse of removability on armor: a player wearing such armor from a mob cannot remove it without death.

The icebreaker is off by default (weight 0): otherwise the mob would freeze the water underfoot.


SETTINGS (MobEnchantments.java)
---------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
ZOMBIE ENCHANT CHANCE - 0.30 - chance to enchant zombie weapons (30%)
SKELETON ENCHANT CHANCE - 0.30 - the same for the skeleton / wanderer (bow and sword are thrown separately)
ILLAGER ENCHANT CHANCE....... 0.30 – robber (crossbow, including in the raid), thug (axe, not in the raid)
ZOMBIE ARMOR ENCHANT CHANCE 0.25 – a chance to enchant EVERY piece of zombie armor
ILLAGER_ARMOR_ENCHANT_CHANCE .. 0.65 on day 100 per piece (helmet, chest, legs, boots roll separately). Day 0 is 1%
SKELETON ARMOR ENCHANT CHANCE 0.25 - Same for skeleton/wanderer armor
COUNT WEIGHTS . . . {55, 28, 12, 4, 1} - how many spells on a weapon
ARMOR COUNT WEIGHTS {60, 28, 10, 2} - how many spells on a piece of armor
LEVEL BIAS . . . 2.0 – The more, the less high the levels
Candidates ........................... list of charms and their weights; weight 0 = charm off

A game check.
---------------
Put a 1.0f chance on ZOMBIE ENCHANT CHANCE, ZOMBIE ARMOR ENCHANT CHANCE, RandomWeapons. ZOMBIE WEAPON CHANCE
and RandomArmor.ZOMBIE ARMOR PIECE CHANCE, restart the game, call the zombies:
Enchanted objects on it shimmer.
To see the spell itself, pick up the object from the fallen mob (do not forget to return 0.30, 0.25, 0.40 and 0.50).
