BASTION
=======

Type: a rare ordinary zombie or ordinary skeleton. Not a new mob in the menu.
Code: bastion/Bastion.java (chance, health, knockback immunity),
     mixin/BastionMixin.java (remembers it is a Bastion, skips Speed potion),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java and mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (chance on spawn).

Overseer, raid anchor, sorcerer, Excalibur, Mjölnir, dynamite, ritual,
Basilisk, captain, Spiky, and “700 IQ” do not become Bastions. Small skeleton — no.


HOW IT LOOKS
------------
Name above head: Bastion. Visible only when crosshair is on it and it is within ~64 blocks.
Health 1000 (500 hearts). Random 20–40 does not apply.
Armor, enchants, weapon, shield, potions, and head — like a normal zombie or skeleton.
Walk speed like adult vanilla zombie or skeleton. No separate speed boost.


HOW IT ACTS
-----------
1. Hits, Punch arrows, and explosions do not move it.
2. Speed potion is not applied: not on spawn, not from bottles, not from jack-o’-lantern beacon.
   Slowness, Strength, and Jump Boost still work.
3. Zombie breaks blocks like a normal zombie (from day 40, same rule).
4. The name does not prevent despawn by itself.
5. Kill XP — 200. Extra armor/weapon does not add; global ×2 multiplier does not apply to this number.


WHO IS EXCLUDED
---------------
Baby, husk, drowned, zombie villager, stray, swamp and desert skeleton.
Does not appear on siege night. Summoned reinforcements and /summon do not roll chance.
Spawners do roll.


COMMAND
-------
  /etbastion                 — zombie
  /etbastion skeleton        — skeleton
  /evolvingthreats spawn bastion
  /evolvingthreats spawn bastion skeleton
Spawns 6 blocks along look direction. Does not roll spawn chance.
Operator rights required.


SETTINGS (Bastion.java)
------------------------
  ENABLED ..................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................ 0.002 — 0.2% for normal zombie and separately for skeleton
  NAME ........................ Bastion
  MAX_HEALTH .................. 1000
  XP_REWARD ................... 200 — XP on kill. 0 = no XP
  KNOCKBACK_RESISTANCE ........ 1.0 — immovable. 0 = normal
  Day ........................ ThreatTable.BASTION_DAY = 60. Chance does not grow with day.
