GLASS SIGHT
===========

Type: zombies, skeletons, creepers, and spiders. Not a separate mob.
Code: sight/GlassSight.java (the roll and which blocks),
     mixin/LivingEntityGlassSightMixin.java (the look after a lucky roll),
     mixin/MobSightMixin.java (asks each mob to roll).

Scale: FIXED. 5% on each roll, from day 0. The moon does not change it.


WHAT HAPPENS
------------
Any of these mobs can notice the player through glass. It is not decided at spawn.
Every 30 seconds that mob rolls a number from 1 to 100.
If it is 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5, he notices — that is 5%.
The roll only matters when glass is all that stands between you.
An open view does not roll: he notices the usual way.
A stone wall, leaves, or water: no roll, he does not see you.

Glass means glass, stained glass, tinted glass, and the panes of each.
Distance is his normal sight (a zombie about 35, a skeleton or creeper 32, a spider 32).
After a lucky roll he comes as if he had seen you in the open. A skeleton can also shoot
through that window. He keeps you while you stay in that sight and the look is still
only glass or air. Step behind stone and he loses you.
A zombie who noticed you still remembers you for the usual 32 blocks,
so stepping back from the window does not make that zombie forget.

Each mob rolls on his own timer, so a crowd does not all look on the same tick.
A miss just waits another 30 seconds.

Not these: zombified piglin, wither skeleton, White Eyes.
Yes: husk, zombie villager, drowned, stray, cave spider, and the other ordinary kinds.


SETTINGS (GlassSight.java)
--------------------------
  ENABLED ................ false — nobody notices through glass
  ROLL_EVERY_TICKS ....... 600 — how often he rolls. 20 ticks = 1 second, so 600 = 30 seconds
  ROLL_OUT_OF ............ 100 — the roll is 1 through this
  NOTICE_ON_OR_BELOW ..... 5 — notice on 1–5, which is 5%. 100 = always, on the half-minute
  MAX_GLASS_BLOCKS ....... 8 — how many glass blocks one look may cross


TEST
----
1. Build a glass box and stand inside. Summon a few zombies just outside, where they would
   see you if the wall were air. Wait 30 seconds. About 1 in 20 rolls should start one walking.
   To force it, set NOTICE_ON_OR_BELOW to 100 and restart.
2. Replace one glass block with stone in front of a mob who was coming. He should lose you,
   unless he is a zombie still inside his 32-block memory.
3. Same wait with a skeleton (he may shoot), a creeper, and a spider.
