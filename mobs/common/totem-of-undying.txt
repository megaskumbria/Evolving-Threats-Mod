TOTEM OF UNDYING
=================

Type: normal zombie, husk, zombie villager, skeleton, and stray.
Code: MobTotem.java (chance and drop),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java and mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (grant before shield and arrow).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (4%). Totem sits in off hand.
2. Lethal hit does not kill: half a heart left, totem burns.
   Mob then gets regeneration, absorption, and fire resistance like a player.
   Void and kill command are not stopped by totem.
3. Off hand occupied. Shield, harmful arrow, and second sword are not granted.
   Skeleton keeps bow in main hand. Zombie main-hand weapon as usual.
4. If totem did not trigger, it drops ~8.5% chance. After trigger nothing to drop.
5. No totem: drowned, zombified piglin, rotten overseer,
   raid zombies and skeletons, wither skeleton, swamp and desert skeletons,
   small skeleton, “700 IQ” skeleton.


SETTINGS
---------
  MobTotem.java
    ENABLED .................... true
    SPAWN_CHANCE ............... 0.04 — 4%
    DROP_CHANCE ................ 0.085 — drop if totem did not burn
