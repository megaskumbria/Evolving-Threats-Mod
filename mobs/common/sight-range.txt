SIGHT RANGE
=================

Type: all hostile mobs that notice the player at close range in vanilla.
Code: sight/MobSight.java (numbers),
     mixin/MobSightMixin.java (on spawn).

Cows, pigs, and villagers unchanged.
Sight does not pass through walls: mob targets only if it sees the player.
From day one, does not grow with threat scale (FIXED).
Already living mobs keep old range until they respawn.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. Who noticed player within 16 blocks now notices from 32.
   Skeleton, creeper, spider, witch, phantom, slime, shulker, wither skeleton.
   Vindicator and brute saw from 12 — also become 32.
2. Who already saw farther stays the same:
   zombie 35, blaze 48, enderman 64, ghast 100.
   Mod specials too: overseer and captain 112, Basilisk 64, siege 96,
   fast creeper 48, long bow 36, ritual band 52.
3. Floating tool (24) and damaged golem (24) already beyond 16, untouched.


SETTINGS (MobSight.java)
-------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false restores vanilla range for new mobs
  RAISE_IF_AT_MOST ............. 16 — who gets raised. Above this left alone
  SEE_BLOCKS ................... 32 — new range. 24 = bit farther, 48 = almost blaze


TEST
----
1. Summon skeleton and step ~25 blocks away so it sees you. It should approach.
     Before it would not notice from that distance.
2. Stand behind a wall at same distance. No target until you peek.
3. Zombie from 30 blocks walks before and now. Its range did not change.
