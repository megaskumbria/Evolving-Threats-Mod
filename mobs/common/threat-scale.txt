THREAT SCALE (world difficulty over time)
==========================================

Type: global system, not a separate mob. One scale per world, shared by all players.
Code: threat/ThreatScale.java (formula and settings), threat/ThreatTable.java (day thresholds and start values),
     threat/ThreatData.java + ThreatEvents.java (remembers when the mod was first loaded),
     client/threat/ThreatHud.java (on-screen bar).
FIXED / GROWING labels and “from day N” come from mobs.txt.


SUMMARY
----
• A “young” world → weak mobs. Over 100 in-game days they smoothly become more dangerous:
  better armor and weapons, stronger enchantments, more potions, more special abilities.
• Some mobs and events do not appear until a certain day (steps).
• The moon tweaks danger at night: full moon stronger, new moon weaker.


HOW DAYS ARE COUNTED
--------------------
• Days count from when the mod FIRST loaded this world. If you add the mod to a world
  that already had 24 days, the scale still starts at zero.
• Sleep speeds the count: skip a night and the scale advances — the world “ages”.
• 1 day = 24000 ticks = 20 minutes real time. 100 days ≈ 33 hours without sleeping.
• The start moment is stored in the world folder and survives game restarts.


HOW GROWING THREAT WORKS
------------------------
Step 1. Progress: t = day / 100 (capped at 1).
Step 2. Curve: Θ = t^1.5. Almost flat early, faster near the end.
Step 3. Moon adjusts Θ:
        Θ_eff = Θ × (0.7 … 1.2 by moon phase) + a small full-moon bonus early in the game.
        Θ_eff ranges from 0 (day 0) to 1 (“maximum”).
Step 4. Each GROWING mechanic interpolates between “start” and “end”:
        value = start + (end − start) × Θ_eff.
        “End” is the number in the mob class (unchanged except enchant/armor scaling, see below).

Rough Θ_eff (full moon / new moon):
  day 10  — 0.09 / 0.02       day 60  — 0.58 / 0.33
  day 20  — 0.15 / 0.06       day 80  — 0.87 / 0.50
  day 40  — 0.34 / 0.18       day 100 — 1.00 / 0.70
  Each full moon is higher than the last; new moon rolls back slightly.
  After day 100, full moon gives 1.0, new moon 0.7 (scale stops growing).

Moon uses the world’s day (same as the sky), whole day from dawn to dawn.
Phases: 0 full moon, 4 new moon (same as HUD icons: moon_0 … moon_7).


WHAT TURNS ON FROM WHICH DAY (FIXED + day; moon does not matter)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Day 20 ... skeletons with Punch bow enchantments V–VI (stronger than vanilla)
  Day 25 ... first siege; then every 10–15 days, more often on nights near full moon,
             no sieges on new moon.
             No drowned or slimes in sieges.
             Wither skeletons in sieges — not by day, but after someone has visited the Nether
  Day 40 ... zombies break blocks, run to light and break it, zombie sorcerer appears;
             Excalibur zombie (Sharpness 20 sword, 1% chance);
             illager siege possible (ravagers, pillagers, evokers, vindicators), 25% chance;
             Spiky (zombie or skeleton, 0.2% chance): one armor piece deals 7–8 hearts through armor
  Day 60 ... Rotten overseer, skeleton captain, Bastion (zombie or skeleton, 0.2% chance)
  Day 80 ... Basilisk skeleton, Raid anchor (natural spawn, 0.1% on surface zombie at night,
             not more than once per 5 days)
These mechanics’ chances do not grow with day: from the threshold they use the class constants.
All day numbers live in threat/ThreatTable.java.


WHAT IS GROWING (start day 0 → end day 100)
-------------------------------------------
Zombie and skeleton health ........ max 20 → 40 (minimum always 20)
One armor piece (each slot) ....... 8% → 75%
Armor material weights (leather/gold/chain/copper/iron/diamond):
    60 / 20 / 9 / 6 / 4.7 / 0.3  →  10 / 5 / 10 / 8 / 32 / 35  (diamond 35% of armor rolls; no netherite)
Weapon in zombie hands ............ 10% → 40%  (start = 25% of max; same pattern for all weapon chances)
Weapon material weights (wood/stone/gold/copper/iron/diamond):
    50 / 30 / 10 / 6 / 3.7 / 0.3  →  30 / 25 / 10 / 10 / 15 / 10
Shield ............................ 2.5% → 25%
Totem of undying .................. 0.2% → 4%
Weapon enchantments ............... 1% → 70%
Armor enchantments ................ 1% → 65%
Enchant count on weapons .......... mostly 1 → up to 5 (weights 85/11/3/1/0 → 55/28/12/4/1)
Enchant level strength ............ mostly low → at max, high levels more common than low (3.0 → -0.3, Sharpness V ≈ 26%)
Extra night spawns ................ 0–3 extra zombies/skeletons on night spawn:
    chance 0% → 40% (ThreatPack.java; not in siege, not daytime)
Spawn potion buffs ................ almost none (96/3/1/0/0/0) → 50/24/13/7/4/2
Special abilities (start = 10% of max):
    TNT zombie 4%, jack-o-lantern beacon 1%, spawner on head 0.5% (and wither skeletons 0.5%),
    harmful arrows 20%, fast bow 15%, tactician skeleton 5%,
    dispenser on skeleton head 2%, Punch 12% (from day 20),
    netherite wither skeleton sword in Nether 15% (stone for the rest; netherite does not drop)
    — numbers here are “end”; on day 0 each is 10× lower.
Wither skeleton sword enchants ..... same as “weapon enchants” above: 1% → 70%, strength grows too.
                                   Stone and netherite swords. Outside Nether and spawner-head carriers: no mod enchants.

Exceptions: floating tools, captain, raid servants, and ritual packs
use FIXED enchants and armor (not scaled by the threat table).


WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE (FIXED)
----------------------------
Phantoms, creepers, spiders, slimes, witches, vindicators, pillagers, drowned, ravagers,
shulkers, piglin brute, magma cube, enderman, patrols, hostile golems, floating tools,
ritual groups. They work from day 0 as before.
Mirror: 0.3% from day 0, moon ignored. See mirror.txt.
Random-arrow skeleton: 1% from day 0, moon ignored. See mobs/skeletons/skeleton.txt, section “RANDOM-ARROW SKELETON”.
Sniper skeleton: 1 in 500 from day 0, moon ignored. See mobs/skeletons/skeleton.txt, section “SNIPER SKELETON”.
Sight range: mobs that noticed the player only within 16 blocks now notice at 32. Does not grow with days.
See sight-range.txt.
Feint creeper: 0.3% from day 0, moon ignored. See mobs/creepers/creeper.txt, section “FEINT CREEPER”.
Cave spider poison can kill from day 0, no chance roll. Witch and slime poison stops at half a heart.
See mobs/spiders/spider.txt, section “CAVE SPIDER: LETHAL POISON AGAIN”.


ON-SCREEN BAR
-------------
Top left: today’s moon icon → “day / 100” bar → skull on the right.
Skull: days 0–24 sleeping zombie, 25–59 helmet zombie, 60–79 red-eyed skull, 80+ crowned.
Size, padding, toggles — top of client/threat/ThreatHud.java.
Hidden while the debug screen (F3) is open.


HOW TO TUNE
-----------
Overall harder / easier:
  threat/ThreatScale.java → DAYS_TO_MAX (days until max scale, now 100),
  CURVE_POWER (1.5; higher = slower start), MOON_NEW_FACTOR / MOON_FULL_FACTOR (moon influence).
Disable the scale entirely: ThreatScale.ENABLED = false (all chances at max, all days unlocked).
Move when a mechanic unlocks: threat/ThreatTable.java (all day thresholds in one place).
Start values (where GROWING begins): also ThreatTable.java.
Maximums (“end of scale”): in each mechanic’s class (RandomArmor, RandomWeapons, MobEnchantments, etc.).
Natural raid anchor: raid/RaidAnchorNatural.java (SPAWN_CHANCE, MIN_DAYS_BETWEEN, ENABLED).
Sieges: siege/SiegeNight.java.
Spiky: spiky/Spiky.java (pierce hearts — HEARTS_MIN / HEARTS_MAX).


IN-GAME TEST
------------
Commands (cheats / OP):
  /evolvingthreats day              — show scale day, world day, moon phase, progress and Θ
  /evolvingthreats day set <N>      — pretend N scale days passed (world time and moon unchanged)
  /evolvingthreats day set 0        — restart the scale
  /time set night                   — night for faster spawn checks
  /evolvingthreats siege in <N>     — siege in N days
  /etraid                           — raid anchor now
To test moon phases, use /time add (phase follows world day).
Compare before/after: day set 0 and day set 100, then spawn a dozen zombies and skeletons.
