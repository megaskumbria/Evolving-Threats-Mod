Sleep
=====

Type: a rule for beds in the Overworld. Not a mob.
Code: sleep/SleepShelterMath.java (the price of a path, and the numbers to edit),
     sleep/SleepShelter.java (the roll, the wake, the one or two mobs),
     sleep/SleepShelterData.java (a failed night is remembered),
     sleep/SleepShelterEvents.java (connection).
     The command /evolvingthreats sleep prints the chance for the block under your feet.
Threat scale: FIXED from the first night. The chance does not grow with the day.


ESSENCE
----
Lying down rolls one number. If the night can reach the bed easily, you wake up and the clock
does not jump. The moon keeps moving from the moment you closed your eyes.
If the roll says the bed is safe, the night skips as in the normal game.

The price is the easiest way in. Every 2 points cuts the chance in half:
  0 points = 100%, 2 = 50%, 4 = 25%, 6 = 12%, 8 = 6%.
  A closed wooden door by itself is about 50%. One stone wall is about 25%.

What adds points (SleepShelterMath):
  • 8 blocks of walking = 1 point.
  • One fence, wall, pane, or closed gate = 2 (about 50%). A second ring = 2 more.
  • Closed wooden door = 2 (about 50%). Closed iron door = 4 (25%).
  • One earth, wood, sand, or glass block that must be broken = 3 (about 35%).
  • One stone or brick block = 4 (25%).
  • One obsidian or bedrock block = 8 (about 6%).
  • Stairs and ladders on the bed's own floor are a way through the room. They add the same as walking.
  • The night just outside a window counts, on the ground floor and on a high floor.
    A house in the air and a roofed platform count the same way. No stair down is required.
  • The floor under the bed cuts off every lower floor. A mine, a cellar, and the fences
    around the bottom of a tower do not change a bed upstairs. Those fences count again
    when the bed is on their floor.
  • No roof over the bed, or the bed itself dimmer than a torch = 0 points. Always.

A block counts as night when its light is 7 or less.
Open air at night counts as dark for the whole time a bed can be used, not only at midnight.
A torch still lights the blocks next to it (about 7 blocks). The search looks 16 blocks
past a window, so one torch beside a high window does not turn the room into a sealed box.
The search looks 60 blocks out along the bed's floor, and 12 blocks up through a hole to the sky.
It does not look under the floor. It does not load new chunks.
If the whole circle is lit, the night just past the circle is what remains, so the chance stays small
and does not become a special "never".

One forgotten dark corner on the bed's own floor matters more than a thick wall on the other side.
The search uses that corner and ignores the thicker side.


WHAT YOU SEE
----
• The roll is made when you lie down. You stay down about 1.5 seconds (30 ticks).
• Failed: 1 or 2 zombies or skeletons appear on the open floor beside the bed.
  A low ceiling still counts. One of them hits as you get up. They are ordinary mobs of this mod, not a named boss.
• If the way in was a door, that door is gone (both halves, wood or iron).
  Glass and a fence lose the block they crossed. A stone or wood wall loses two blocks
  on the inside: the first block and the one above it, a gap about as tall as a player.
  A fully sealed room does this too. The mobs still appear in the room, beside the bed.
  A thicker wall is not dug all the way through. Obsidian and bedrock stay.
  The broken blocks drop, so the hole can be closed again.
  Open ground and a dark room break nothing.
• A monster already in the same room, within 16 blocks, wakes you with no new mob.
  One that is chasing you inside that room counts out to 60 blocks.
  A monster outside the walls does not count, even if it is just on the other side.
• After a failure, that night cannot be slept through again, even if you kill them.
  The next day can.
• A failed sleep does not count as having slept. Phantoms keep growing.
• The night of a siege still cannot be slept at all. That ban is unchanged.


HOW TO TUNE
----
Chances, radius, light, doors, walls: the SETTINGS block at the top of SleepShelterMath.java.
How long you lie there, how close a monster must be, how many appear, how many blocks break: SleepShelter.java.
Turn the whole rule off: SleepShelterMath.ENABLED = false.


CHECK
----
1. Stand in the open at night and use the bed. You are hit, and the time does not jump to morning.
2. Try the same bed again the same night. You are refused.
3. Sleep in a closed lit room with torches, a fence, and stone walls out to 60 blocks.
   /evolvingthreats sleep while standing there should show a small percent.
   Most nights the morning comes. A rare night does not.
4. Stand on the bed block and run /evolvingthreats sleep to read the percent without lying down.
   The command uses the block under your feet. If a door or a wall is the way in, it also says the way in would break.
5. Sleep in a closed wooden house with night outside the door.
   On a failed night the door is gone and the mobs are beside the bed. One hits.
   A stone wall loses two blocks on the inside, one above the other. The rest of a thick wall stays.
