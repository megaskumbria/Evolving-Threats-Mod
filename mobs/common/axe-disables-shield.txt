AXE DISABLES SHIELD
==================

Type: hit by a zombie, a skeleton and a brutal piglin. Not a new mob.
Code: axe/AxeShieldDisable.java (who, what axes, seconds),
     mixin/AxeShieldDisableMixin.java (the moment when the blow hit the shield).

The champion is not included here: he already knocks down a shield with an iron axe.
At the brute, the gold axe also knocked down the shield if it was in the main hand.
Now the axe in the second hand is considered the same. Wooden — no.
It does not depend on days (FIXED).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. The blow hit the raised shield, and the axe in either hand was not made of wood.
   The shield cannot be raised for 5 seconds. The same sound is heard from the axe of the defender.
   Stone, copper, gold, iron, diamond and Netherite — everything is like that.
2. The wooden axe does not knock down the shield, even if it is in the main hand.
   If the axe is stronger in the other hand, the shield still gets knocked down.
3. The sword, bow, pickaxe and empty hands do not knock down the shield.
4. This is any zombie and any skeleton that has such an axe:
   a supervisor with diamond axes, too. A drowned man — only if he has an axe in his hand.
5. A blow that the shield did not catch does not give a cooldown.


SETTINGS (AxeShieldDisable.java)
---------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false, and only the game itself knocks down the shield again
  COOLDOWN_SECONDS ............. 5 — seconds like a champion. 2 = shorter. 0 = do not shoot down


TEST
----
1. Give the zombie an iron axe and cover yourself with a shield. After hitting, the shield does not rise for about 5 seconds.
2. The same with the axe in the second hand and the sword in the first.
3. Wooden axe shield does not knock down.
4. The brute with the gold axe still knocks down the shield for the same 5 seconds.
