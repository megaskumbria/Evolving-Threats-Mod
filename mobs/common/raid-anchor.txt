Raid ANCHOR
============================

Type: Raid event (1 anchor + 12–15 servants). Not a separate type of Minecraft entity.
Code: raid/RaidAnchor.java, raid/RaidEliteLoadout.java - settings in the "SETTINGS" blocks.


ESSENCE
----
• Anchor — a zombie with a golden hoe and 100 health. He himself beats weakly.
• While the anchor is alive, the servants “hear” the player: they go to the fixed target
  within a radius of ~128 blocks (as a foreman's hunt, but for the whole wave).
• Domestic workers — zombies and skeletons with a separate armor/weapon random
  (RaidEliteLoadout): more often iron/diamond, more often enchantment; not loot of the ordinary world.
• Killed the anchor → of all the servants of this Slowness II raid for 30 seconds,
  GPS is turned off, the target is reset (then only normal aggression).


Anchor by default.
-------------------
  Health .............. 100 HP
  Weapon ................ golden hoe (weak, symbolic)
  The name is overhead ....... Raid Anchor. Visible only when the sight is on it
                          and it's no further than about 64 blocks.


DOMESTIC WORKERS
--------
  Quantity ............ 12–15 (random)
  Composition ................ ~60% zombies, ~40% skeletons
  Spawn ................. ring of 6–14 blocks from anchor
  Armor ................. its random (see RaidEliteLoadout.java)
  Skeletons ............... bow; no distributor on the head


IN-GAME TEST
-----------
Commands (cheats / OP, gamaster rights level):

  /etraid
  /evolvingthreats spawn raid
  /evolvingthreats spawn raid — raid target

Anchor appears ~8 blocks in front of you; servants around.


NATURAL APPEARANCE
----------------------
Code: raid/RaidAnchorNatural.java (settings at the top), raid/RaidAnchorData.java (world memory).
  From which day ........... 80th (ThreatTable.RAID_ANCHOR_DAY)
  The chance of................. 0.1% for each zombie (SPAWN_CHANCE) that appears does not increase from day to day
  Where and when ............ the upper world, the night, under the open sky, an ordinary zombie (not a child),
                           only natural spawn; not on the night of the siege
  Pause .................. no more than one anchor per world in 5 game days (MIN_days_BETWEEN)
  As the................... appeared zombie itself becomes an anchor, the servants stand around on the surface.
The /etraid command works as before (without conditions).
