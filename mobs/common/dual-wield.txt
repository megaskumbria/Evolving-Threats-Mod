TWO WEAPONS IN HANDS
===================

Type: normal zombie, husk, zombie villager, skeleton, and stray.
Code: RandomWeapons.java (chance and off-hand damage),
     mixin/MobDualMeleeMixin.java (bonus at hit moment),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java and mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (grant on spawn).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance 20% (ZOMBIE_DUAL_MELEE_CHANCE and SKELETON_DUAL_MELEE_CHANCE).
   Each hand gets its own random weapon: sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel, or hoe.
   They may match (sword and sword) or differ (axe and pickaxe).
2. Hit = mob strength + main-hand weapon damage + off-hand weapon damage.
   Off hand alone does not deal damage in vanilla, so it is added separately.
   Sharpness from both hands counts. Fire Aspect from off hand also ignites.
   Off-hand weapon wears from hits.
3. If two weapons rolled, shield, totem, and harmful arrow are not granted: hands are full.
   Skeleton drops bow; fights only in melee.
4. Do not get random dual weapons: drowned, raid zombie, rotten overseer,
   wither skeleton, swamp and desert skeletons, small skeleton, “700 IQ” skeleton.
   Overseer has its own two diamond axes; off hand counts in the hit too.


SETTINGS
---------
  RandomWeapons.java
    ZOMBIE_DUAL_MELEE_CHANCE ...... 0.20 — 20% for zombies
    SKELETON_DUAL_MELEE_CHANCE .... 0.20 — 20% for skeleton and stray


BOW AND ONE MELEE WEAPON
========================

Type: normal skeleton and stray. Not the captain, sniper, or “700 IQ” tactician.
Code: RandomWeapons.java (chance, which hand holds what),
     mixin/AbstractSkeletonTwoLeadMixin.java (mark on the skeleton),
     mixin/TwoLeadHandsSaveMixin.java (mark survives a reload),
     mixin/AbstractSkeletonDualWieldMixin.java (melee goal stays available),
     mixin/RangedBowAttackGoalMixin.java (no shot at point-blank; no hand swap for this mark),
     mixin/SkeletonMeleeGoalSwapMixin.java (captain and sniper still swap; this mark does not),
     client mixin/TwoLeadHandsRendererMixin.java (bow draw from the left hand; sword arm up close).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SKELETON_DUAL_WIELD_CHANCE, 0.35 = 35% on day 100. Grows with the threat scale, same as before.
2. Right hand: sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel, or hoe. Left hand: his bow.
   Up close he raises the right hand and hits at once. Far away he shoots the bow from the left hand.
   The two items never trade places, so he does not stand still swapping weapons.
3. Skeletons already in the world before this change have no mark. They still swap hands.
   New spawns get the mark.
4. Does not apply when the right hand has no melee weapon: bow only, shield, totem, or harmful arrow.
   Captain and sniper keep the bow in the right hand and still swap.
   Tactician still switches the item on his back. Two melee weapons and no bow are unchanged.


SETTINGS
--------
  RandomWeapons.java
    SKELETON_DUAL_WIELD_CHANCE .... 0.35 — 35% on day 100 for skeleton and stray
