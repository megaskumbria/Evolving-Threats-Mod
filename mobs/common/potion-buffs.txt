POTION BUFFS ON MOBS
====================

Type: zombie, husk, zombie villager, drowned, skeleton, stray, swamp skeleton,
     desert skeleton, spider, cave spider, witch, creeper, enderman, pillager,
     vindicator, evoker, ravager, blaze, phantom, slime, magma cube, guardian,
     elder guardian, shulker, piglin, brute, hoglin, zoglin, floating tool.
Code: MobPotionBuffs.java (effect list, how many at once, level-II chance),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java, mixin/SkeletonMixin.java, mixin/SpiderRoleMixin.java,
     mixin/MobPotionBuffMixin.java (witch, pillager, vindicator, evoker, ravager,
     cave spider),
     mixin/MobPotionBuffExtraMixin.java (creeper, enderman, blaze, phantom, slime,
     magma cube, guardian, elder guardian, shulker, piglin, brute, hoglin, zoglin),
     mixin/CreeperPotionCloudMixin.java (cloud after creeper explosion).
     Floating tool calls tryApply itself from tool/FloatingTool.java.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn, a mob may get 0 to 5 different helpful effects.
   More effects = rarer. All five at once is about 2%.
2. Effects never expire. Particles show like a player who drank a potion.
   Several potions blend particle colors into one.
3. Of 25 helpful potions in the game, this uses 10 effects. Long and regular versions
   behave the same at infinite duration.
   Strong potions give level II (jump, speed, regeneration, strength), about 33% chance.
4. No healing. Instant healing cannot be infinite — the mob would heal every tick.
5. Invisibility hides the body. Armor and held items stay visible.
   If another effect is active, its particles can still reveal the mob.
6. Water breathing stops zombies from drowning and turning into drowned.
7. Night vision and luck barely change combat. They are in the list as helpful potions.
8. An exploding creeper still leaves a cloud as before. The cloud effect on players
   lasts 45 seconds, not forever. The cloud itself fades in 15 seconds.
   On a living creeper, the spawn potion buff does not expire.
9. No buff: zombified piglin, wither skeleton, rotten overseer,
   mod-raid zombies and skeletons, illusioner.
   Witch, pillager, vindicator, evoker, and ravager get buffs even during raids.
   Special creepers (sapper, dynamite thrower, shield breaker) can still have potions.
   Floating tool gets the same potions when summoned by command.
   Invisibility is never rolled for creepers or floating tool: creepers are hard to spot anyway,
   and invisibility would hide the entire floating tool. Other potions still apply.


SETTINGS
---------
  MobPotionBuffs.java
    ENABLED .................... true
    COUNT_WEIGHTS .............. {50, 24, 13, 7, 4, 2} — 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 effects
    STRONG_LEVEL_CHANCE ........ 0.33 — level-II chance where supported
    CLOUD_EFFECT_SECONDS ....... 45 — seconds the creeper cloud effect lasts on the player
