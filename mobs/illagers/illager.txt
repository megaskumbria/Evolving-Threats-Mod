PATROL AT THE BASE
===============

Type: squad of vanilla pillagers, vindicators, and ravager (not a new mob in the menu).
Code: illager/IllagerPatrol.java (composition, distance, how often),
     illager/IllagerPatrolEvents.java (check every world tick),
     entity/ai/IllagerPatrolMarchGoal.java (march to where the player stood),
     mixin/RaiderPatrolMixin.java (remember the point and save in the world).

Vanilla patrol is not disabled: a rare group of pillagers alone can still appear far from a village.
This squad spawns separately and also near the base.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. Random pause: 8 to 14 in-game days (one day = 20 minutes).
   Each time the number is new. Spawns only in daytime and not on Peaceful difficulty.
   If the timer expires at night, the squad comes the next morning, not two weeks later.
   In Creative and Spectator the squad does not spawn.
2. Location — 64–96 blocks from the player, on the surface, not in water.
   If a marching squad is already nearby (140 blocks), a new one does not spawn.
3. The squad has 2–3 pillagers, 2–3 vindicators, and 1 ravager.
   One pillager is the captain with a banner. An ominous bottle drops from them.
4. They remember where the player stood at spawn and march there.
   If the player left, they still reach the base and stop within 12 blocks of the point.
   They do not chase the player across the map. If they see the player nearby on the way — fight like normal mobs,
   lose sight — march to the same point again.
5. This squad does not join someone else’s village raid.


SETTINGS (IllagerPatrol.java)
------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — turn spawning on/off
  DAYS_MIN / DAYS_MAX .......... 8 / 14 — random pause, in in-game days
  TEST_ATTEMPT_TICKS ........... 0 — if greater than 0, days are ignored, pause in ticks
  RETRY_TICKS .................. 1200 — how often to retry if it is night or no valid spot
  DAYTIME_ONLY ................. true — daytime only
  SPAWN_DISTANCE_MIN / MAX ..... 64 / 96 — how far from the player they appear, in blocks
  ARRIVE_BLOCKS ................ 12 — how close to the point to stop
  MARCH_SPEED .................. 0.6 — march speed toward the point
  PILLAGER_MIN / MAX ........... 2 / 3 — pillagers
  VINDICATOR_MIN / MAX ......... 2 / 3 — vindicators
  RAVAGER_COUNT ................ 1 — ravagers. 0 = none
  NEARBY_BLOCKS ................ 140 — do not spawn a second squad while the first is still marching


TEST
----
Command (cheats required): /etpatrol
The squad spawns immediately 64–96 blocks away and marches to where you stand.
Works in Creative and at night. The normal 8–14 day timer is not reset by this.

PILLAGER: FIREWORK FROM CROSSBOW
==============================

Type: vanilla pillager (not a new mob). Same in patrol, raid, and mansion.
Code: illager/PillagerFirework.java (chance, stars, and aim),
     mixin/PillagerFireworkMixin.java (start of crossbow charge),
     mixin/PillagerFireworkAimMixin.java (rocket flies at the chest, no arrow arc).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. About every fifth shot is a rocket. It is on the crossbow from the first moment of charging:
   a firework is visible on the crossbow, no arrow in that shot. Other shots are normal.
2. The rocket flies straight at the chest. The game aimed it like an arrow, with lift, and it passed over the head.
3. The rocket has one star. With no armor on Normal difficulty that is 7 health (3.5 hearts).
   Weaker on Easy, stronger on Hard. Armor reduces the hit.
4. The blast hits everyone in a radius of about five blocks: player, villager, golem,
   other pillagers, and the shooter if too close.
5. Piglins with crossbows and players do not use this swap.


SETTINGS (PillagerFirework.java)
---------------------------------
ENABLED — false means arrow every time again.
SHOT_CHANCE — 0.20 = every fifth shot. 0 = never. 1 = every shot is a rocket.
STAR_COUNT — stars in the rocket. 1 = 3.5 hearts. 0 = always arrow. 7 = max, 9.5 hearts.
AIM_BODY — height on the target. 0.45 = chest. 1.0 = top of head.

VINDICATOR: DIAMOND AXE
=========================

Type: vanilla vindicator (not a new mob). Iron axe stays on the rest.
Code: illager/VindicatorDiamondAxe.java (chance),
     mixin/VindicatorDiamondAxeMixin.java (moment when the axe is already in hand).

Where the chance rolls: mansion, home patrol, raid, spawn egg, spawn command.
Same chance everywhere.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. About every fourth vindicator gets a diamond axe instead of iron.
2. Enchantments from the iron axe carry over to the diamond one.
   Outside raids the game applies them by difficulty, and separately the mod with 30% chance
   (see mobs/common/enchantments-on-gear.txt, ILLAGER_ENCHANT_CHANCE).
   In raids enchantments are raid-specific: if the raid decided to enchant the axe, the diamond one stays enchanted.
   Not every axe has enchantments. Enchant chance is the same as for iron.
3. The axe drops from the vindicator with the same chance as iron: about 8.5%.
   Enchantments on the dropped axe are preserved.


SETTINGS (VindicatorDiamondAxe.java)
-------------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false means all axes iron again
  CHANCE ....................... 0.25 — share of diamond axes (25%)


TEST
----
1. To see it immediately, temporarily set CHANCE = 1.0. After testing set back to 0.25.
2. Summon a vindicator or call patrol /etpatrol. Diamond axe in hand.
   Some shimmer — that is enchantments.
3. Start a raid with vindicators. Some have diamond axes too, including raid-enchanted ones.
4. Mansion: vindicators there use the same chance.

VINDICATOR CHOPS WOOD
=====================

Type: vanilla vindicator. Does not break stone or dirt.
Code: illager/VindicatorWoodBreak.java (what counts as wood and speed),
     entity/ai/VindicatorChopGoal.java (when to chop),
     mixin/VindicatorChopMixin.java (hook up the goal).


WHEN IT CHOPS
-----------
Has a target (player, villager, iron golem) within 32 blocks, no normal path,
and a wooden block with collision directly in front of the vindicator.
If the target is already in melee range, it attacks instead of chopping.
If a path opens — drops the block and moves.
The axe stays in hand while chopping, hits with it, not fists.
As soon as the path is clear, attacks the player again with the same axe.
Crack progress is not reset for 5 seconds if distracted and returning to the same block.


WHAT IT BREAKS
----------
Everything wooden that blocks the way:
  fence, gate, door, trapdoor, planks, logs, stairs, slabs,
  button, pressure plate, shelf, sign, bamboo.
Nether and any non-wood fence is left alone.
Chest and barrel are not broken: items are inside.
Dirt, stone, cobblestone fence — no.


SPEED
--------
20% of how fast the same axe chops for the player.
Oak fence: iron axe about 2.5 sec, diamond about 1.9 sec.
Efficiency on the axe speeds it up too. Sharpness does not affect chop speed.
Items from the block do not drop.


SETTINGS (VindicatorWoodBreak.java)
------------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPEED_MULTIPLIER ............. 0.2 — fraction of player speed
  DROP_ITEMS ................... false
  MAX_DISTANCE_TO_TARGET ....... 32
  CHECK_INTERVAL_TICKS ......... 10
  KEEP_PROGRESS_TICKS .......... 100
  REACH ........................ 3.5
  RESPECT_MOB_GRIEFING ......... true


TEST
----
1. Put a wooden fence between yourself and a vindicator. It should chop the fence with crack animation.
2. Iron axe is slower than diamond on the same fence.
3. Stone fence and dirt are not broken: vindicator stands and looks for a path.
4. /gamerule mobGriefing false — chopping stops.

RAVAGER: BREAKS THROUGH WALLS
=============================

Type: vanilla ravager (not a new mob). Same in patrol and raid.
Code: illager/RavagerWallBreak.java (cooldown, hole size, what counts as stone),
     entity/ai/RavagerHeadbuttGoal.java (when to strike),
     mixin/RavagerHeadbuttMixin.java (goal in behavior list).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. If there is no path to the target, the ravager headbutts the wall in front.
   Target is close and path is clear — normal bite, does not touch the wall.
2. One strike removes a hole 3 blocks wide and 3 high, one layer deep.
   A thick wall gives way layer by layer: the next layer only after a cooldown.
3. Dirt, wood, glass, wool, netherrack drop on the first hit.
   Stone, cobblestone, deepslate, bricks, ores, and metal blocks
   crack on the first hit and drop on the second.
4. Obsidian, crying obsidian, and bedrock do not break.
   Barrier, portal frame, and reinforced deepslate too: they cannot be broken.
5. Chest and barrel drop on the first hit, furnace on the second like stone.
   The block itself does not drop as an item. Contents spill on the ground and can be picked up.
   A wall of planks and stone also leaves no block drops.
6. Cooldown between hits is random, 20–25 seconds.
   While stunned by a shield or roaring, it does not strike.


SETTINGS (RavagerWallBreak.java)
---------------------------------
ENABLED — false disables wall breaking.
COOLDOWN_MIN_SECONDS / COOLDOWN_MAX_SECONDS — pause in seconds. 20 and 25 = twenty to twenty-five.
STONE_HITS — hits on stone. 2 = two hits. 1 = stone drops immediately.
HOLE_WIDTH — hole width. 3 = center, left, and right.
HOLE_HEIGHT — height from feet. 3 = feet, body, and head.
REACH — how close the wall must be to strike. 2 = flush or one block away.
DROP_ITEMS — true if blocks should drop as items.
RESPECT_MOB_GRIEFING — true: with mobGriefing false walls are not broken.

VEX: +1 HEART PER HIT
==============================

Type: vanilla vex (not a new mob). Still summons ravager as before.
Code: vex/VexDamage.java (how many hearts to add),
     mixin/VexDamageMixin.java (moment of hit).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
Each hit removes one heart more than in vanilla. With no armor.
Sword, Sharpness, and difficulty stay as they were — one extra heart on top.

On Normal difficulty iron sword hit was 4.5 hearts, now 5.5.
On Easy was 2.75 hearts, now 3.75.
On Hard was 6.75 hearts, now 7.75.

Armor still reduces damage. Peaceful still zeroes damage as before.

About every fifth hit that removed health applies Weakness I for 10 seconds.
Player only. Villager and golem do not get weakness.
A shield block and brief post-hit invulnerability do not count.
Milk removes the effect immediately. Another such hit refreshes the duration.
A swarm of several vexes applies weakness more often: chance rolls separately for each.


SETTINGS (VexDamage.java)
--------------------------
ENABLED — false disables the extra damage. Weakness still applies.
EXTRA_HEARTS — extra hearts per hit. 1 = one heart. 0 = vanilla.
WEAKNESS_ENABLED — false removes weakness. Extra heart remains.
WEAKNESS_CHANCE — 0.20 = every fifth hit that landed. 0.25 = every fourth. 0 = never.
WEAKNESS_SECONDS — how long weakness lasts. 10 = ten seconds. 0 = no effect.
WEAKNESS_AMPLIFIER — 0 = Weakness I. 1 = Weakness II, sword barely hits.
