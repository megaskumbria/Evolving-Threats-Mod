PILLAGER: FIREWORK FROM CROSSBOW
==============================

Type: vanilla pillager (not a new mob). Same in patrol, raid, and mansion.
Code: illager/PillagerFirework.java (chance, stars, and aim),
     mixin/PillagerFireworkMixin.java (start of crossbow charge),
     mixin/PillagerFireworkAimMixin.java (rocket flies at the chest, no arrow arc).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. About every fifth shot is a rocket. It is on the crossbow from the first moment of charging:
   a firework is visible on the crossbow, no arrow in that shot. Other shots are normal.
2. The rocket flies straight at the chest. The game aimed it like an arrow, with lift, and it passed over the head.
3. The rocket has one star. With no armor on Normal difficulty that is 7 health (3.5 hearts).
   Weaker on Easy, stronger on Hard. Armor reduces the hit.
4. The blast hits everyone in a radius of about five blocks: player, villager, golem,
   other pillagers, and the shooter if too close.
5. Piglins with crossbows and players do not use this swap.


SETTINGS (PillagerFirework.java)
---------------------------------
ENABLED — false means arrow every time again.
SHOT_CHANCE — 0.20 = every fifth shot. 0 = never. 1 = every shot is a rocket.
STAR_COUNT — stars in the rocket. 1 = 3.5 hearts. 0 = always arrow. 7 = max, 9.5 hearts.
AIM_BODY — height on the target. 0.45 = chest. 1.0 = top of head.
