VEX: +1 HEART PER HIT
==============================

Type: vanilla vex (not a new mob). Still summons ravager as before.
Code: vex/VexDamage.java (how many hearts to add),
     mixin/VexDamageMixin.java (moment of hit).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
Each hit removes one heart more than in vanilla. With no armor.
Sword, Sharpness, and difficulty stay as they were — one extra heart on top.

On Normal difficulty iron sword hit was 4.5 hearts, now 5.5.
On Easy was 2.75 hearts, now 3.75.
On Hard was 6.75 hearts, now 7.75.

Armor still reduces damage. Peaceful still zeroes damage as before.

About every fifth hit that removed health applies Weakness I for 10 seconds.
Player only. Villager and golem do not get weakness.
A shield block and brief post-hit invulnerability do not count.
Milk removes the effect immediately. Another such hit refreshes the duration.
A swarm of several vexes applies weakness more often: chance rolls separately for each.


SETTINGS (VexDamage.java)
--------------------------
ENABLED — false disables the extra damage. Weakness still applies.
EXTRA_HEARTS — extra hearts per hit. 1 = one heart. 0 = vanilla.
WEAKNESS_ENABLED — false removes weakness. Extra heart remains.
WEAKNESS_CHANCE — 0.20 = every fifth hit that landed. 0.25 = every fourth. 0 = never.
WEAKNESS_SECONDS — how long weakness lasts. 10 = ten seconds. 0 = no effect.
WEAKNESS_AMPLIFIER — 0 = Weakness I. 1 = Weakness II, sword barely hits.
