RAVAGER: BREAKS THROUGH WALLS
=============================

Type: vanilla ravager (not a new mob). Same in patrol and raid.
Code: illager/RavagerWallBreak.java (cooldown, hole size, what counts as stone),
     entity/ai/RavagerHeadbuttGoal.java (when to strike),
     mixin/RavagerHeadbuttMixin.java (goal in behavior list),
     mixin/RavagerWallTargetMixin.java (keep the target behind a wall).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. If there is no path to the target, the ravager headbutts the wall in front.
   Target is close and path is clear — normal bite, does not touch the wall.
2. One strike removes a hole 3 blocks wide and 3 high, one layer deep.
   A thick wall gives way layer by layer: the next layer only after a cooldown.
3. Dirt, wood, glass, wool, netherrack drop on the first hit.
   Stone, cobblestone, deepslate, bricks, ores, and metal blocks
   crack on the first hit and drop on the second.
4. Obsidian, crying obsidian, and bedrock do not break.
   Barrier, portal frame, and reinforced deepslate too: they cannot be broken.
5. Chest and barrel drop on the first hit, furnace on the second like stone.
   The block itself does not drop as an item. Contents spill on the ground and can be picked up.
   A wall of planks and stone also leaves no block drops.
6. Cooldown between hits is random, 10–15 seconds.
   While stunned by a shield or roaring, it does not strike.
7. Hiding behind a wall he can break (dirt, wood, stone, glass) does not make him
   forget the target. He keeps coming and headbutts through the cooldown,
   until he can see the target again.
   Obsidian and bedrock do not count: he forgets those as in the normal game.
   After he sees the target again, such a hide drops the target in 3 seconds.


SETTINGS (RavagerWallBreak.java)
---------------------------------
ENABLED — false disables wall breaking.
COOLDOWN_MIN_SECONDS / COOLDOWN_MAX_SECONDS — pause in seconds. 10 and 15 = ten to fifteen.
STONE_HITS — hits on stone. 2 = two hits. 1 = stone drops immediately.
HOLE_WIDTH — hole width. 3 = center, left, and right.
HOLE_HEIGHT — height from feet. 3 = feet, body, and head.
REACH — how close the wall must be to strike. 2 = flush or one block away.
DROP_ITEMS — true if blocks should drop as items.
RESPECT_MOB_GRIEFING — true: with mobGriefing false walls are not broken.
KEEP_TARGET_BEHIND_WALL — true: a target behind a breakable wall is not forgotten.
UNSEEN_FORGET_TICKS — 60: after he can see again, a hide he cannot break lasts 3 seconds.
