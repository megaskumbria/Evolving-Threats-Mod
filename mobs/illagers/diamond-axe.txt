VINDICATOR: DIAMOND AXE
=========================

Type: vanilla vindicator (not a new mob). Iron axe stays on the rest.
Code: illager/VindicatorDiamondAxe.java (chance),
     mixin/VindicatorDiamondAxeMixin.java (moment when the axe is already in hand).

Where the chance rolls: mansion, home patrol, raid, spawn egg, spawn command.
Same chance everywhere.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. About every fourth vindicator gets a diamond axe instead of iron.
2. Enchantments from the iron axe carry over to the diamond one.
   Outside raids the game applies them by difficulty, and separately the mod with 30% chance
   (see mobs/common/enchantments-on-gear.txt, ILLAGER_ENCHANT_CHANCE).
   In raids enchantments are raid-specific: if the raid decided to enchant the axe, the diamond one stays enchanted.
   Not every axe has enchantments. Enchant chance is the same as for iron.
3. The axe drops from the vindicator with the same chance as iron: about 8.5%.
   Enchantments on the dropped axe are preserved.


SETTINGS (VindicatorDiamondAxe.java)
-------------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false means all axes iron again
  CHANCE ....................... 0.25 — share of diamond axes (25%)


TEST
----
1. To see it immediately, temporarily set CHANCE = 1.0. After testing set back to 0.25.
2. Summon a vindicator or call patrol /etpatrol. Diamond axe in hand.
   Some shimmer — that is enchantments.
3. Start a raid with vindicators. Some have diamond axes too, including raid-enchanted ones.
4. Mansion: vindicators there use the same chance.
