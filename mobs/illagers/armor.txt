PILLAGER AND VINDICATOR ARMOR
==============================

Type: vanilla pillager and vindicator. Patrol, raid, mansion, and summon.
Code: illager/PillagerArmor.java (which set, dye, drop),
     mixin/PillagerArmorMixin.java (on spawn),
     client illager/PillagerArmorLayer.java (how the plates sit),
     client illager/IllagerArmorFit.java (hides the robe while armor is worn; the nose stays).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. A new pillager or vindicator might wear a full set. GROWING: 12% on day 0, 75% at day 100
   on a full moon. A new moon is lower (about 56% at day 100). Already spawned ones keep what they wear.
   If the roll hits, the set is one material: leather 34%, iron 28%, chain 22%, copper 13%, diamond 3%.
   Those shares do not change with the day. No gold, no netherite.
2. Leather is dyed one color for the whole set, so it is not plain brown.
3. The set is real armor: it blocks damage, and each piece can drop (8.5%).
   Enchantments are rolled per piece, same growth as zombie armor (1% on day 0, 65% per piece
   at day 100, full moon). One piece, several, or the whole set — each roll is separate.
   A captain banner is not enchanted. See MobEnchantments.ILLAGER_ARMOR_ENCHANT_CHANCE.
4. A slot that already has an item is left alone. A captain banner on the head stays;
   that pillager still gets chest, legs, and boots.
5. The illager model is not a player's, so the game does not draw armor by itself.
   The mod glues the player armor mesh onto the head, body, arms, and legs.
   The nose stays, including in front of a helmet.
   While chest, legs, or boots are worn, the robe is hidden so it does not cut through the plates.
   A chestplate also unfolds the crossed arms, so the sleeves have a real arm to follow.
   Idle pillagers in a chestplate stand with their arms down. Evokers are not changed.
   If a plate sits wrong, change the numbers in PillagerArmorLayer and restart.


SETTINGS (PillagerArmor.java)
-----------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  WEAR_CHANCE_END .............. 0.75 = 75% wear a set at day 100, full moon. Day 0 is ThreatTable 0.12
  LEATHER_DYE_CHANCE ........... 1.0 = every leather set is dyed. 0 = brown
  LEATHER_USE_DYE_COLORS ....... true = 16 bright dyes. false = any RGB
  DROP_CHANCE .................. 0.085 = 8.5% per piece

SETTINGS (PillagerArmorLayer.java)
----------------------------------
  HELMET_LIFT_Y ................ -1 sits the helmet on the 10-pixel head. -2 lifts it off the chin. 0 leaves the crown bare
  CHEST_DROP_Y ................. 2 lowers the chestplate under the mouth. 0 leaves the collar across the chin
  CHEST_DEPTH_SCALE ............ 1.08 thickens the plate and the sleeves together, so the shoulder stays closed
  LEGS_DEPTH_SCALE ............. 1.24 wraps the waist and still sits under the chestplate
  SHOW_REAL_ARMS_WITH_CHESTPLATE  true = arms down so sleeves fit. false = keep the crossed robe (it cuts the plate)
