WITCH: NEW POTIONS AND FOG
============================

Type: vanilla witch throws at the player. No separate mob type spawns.
Code: witch/WitchThrownPotions.java (list, chances, seconds),
     mixin/WitchThrownPotionMixin.java (swap bottle at throw time).

Allies in a raid still get healing and regeneration.
The nullifier white potion stays splash and still clears effects.
While the witch is drinking, she throws nothing.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Roughly every other throw at the player comes from the new list, equal weight:
   Slowness IV for 15–20 seconds,
   Blindness for 5–10 seconds,
   Wither I for 10 seconds,
   Hunger I for 20–30 seconds,
   Nausea for 8–12 seconds,
   Mining Fatigue I for 15–20 seconds,
   Levitation for 4–6 seconds.
2. Other throws work as before: Slowness at range, poison if you are healthy,
   Weakness sometimes up close, otherwise instant damage.
3. About one third of throws at the player are fog bottles. On impact, a cloud
   lasts about 30 seconds, like a normal lingering potion. While you stand in it,
   the effect is reapplied.
   For poison, slowness, weakness, and damage, fog lasts one quarter of the bottle time.
   For new effects, seconds match the settings.
4. Splash at point-blank lasts almost the full configured duration.
   At the edge of the puddle, duration is shorter — same as any splash bottle.
   Splashes and fog affect everyone nearby, not only the player.
5. To disable one potion from the new list, set its maximum seconds to 0.


SETTINGS (WitchThrownPotions.java)
-----------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPECIAL_CHANCE ............... 0.50 — share of new effects (50%)
  LINGERING_CHANCE ............. 0.35 — share of fog bottles (35%)
  SLOWNESS_MIN_SECONDS ......... 15
  SLOWNESS_MAX_SECONDS ......... 20
  SLOWNESS_AMPLIFIER ........... 3 — Slowness IV
  BLINDNESS_MIN_SECONDS ........ 5
  BLINDNESS_MAX_SECONDS ........ 10
  WITHER_SECONDS ............... 10 — Wither I, about 2 hearts
  WITHER_AMPLIFIER ............. 0
  HUNGER_MIN_SECONDS ........... 20
  HUNGER_MAX_SECONDS ........... 30
  NAUSEA_MIN_SECONDS ........... 8
  NAUSEA_MAX_SECONDS ........... 12
  FATIGUE_MIN_SECONDS .......... 15
  FATIGUE_MAX_SECONDS .......... 20
  FATIGUE_AMPLIFIER ............ 0 — Mining Fatigue I. Level 1 nearly stops mining
  LEVITATION_MIN_SECONDS ....... 4
  LEVITATION_MAX_SECONDS ....... 6
  VANILLA_LINGERING_SCALE ...... 0.25 — fog for vanilla potions, quarter duration


TEST
----
1. Set SPECIAL_CHANCE = 1.0 and LINGERING_CHANCE = 0. Summon a witch and back away
   so she throws. Bottles are splash; effects come from the new list.
2. Set LINGERING_CHANCE = 1.0. After a hit, colored fog remains on the ground.
3. Restore 0.50 and 0.35. Some throws are poison, slowness, weakness, or damage again.
4. /etwitch — white bottle is still splash and still clears effects.

WITCH: INVISIBILITY POTION
==========================

Type: normal witch, including nullifier witch. No separate mob type spawns.
Code: witch/WitchInvisibility.java (chance, pause, duration),
     mixin/WitchInvisibilityMixin.java (moment when the witch decides to drink).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. While the witch has a target, every 15 seconds there is a 10% chance to drink invisibility.
   First check happens as soon as she picks someone.
   Without a target, the timer does not run: alone in her hut she does not vanish.
2. Sip works like vanilla witch potions: bottle at mouth, she slows down, about one and a half seconds.
   While drinking, she throws nothing.
3. After the sip she is invisible for 20 seconds. Throws and sounds remain:
   you see the bottle in flight, hear steps and voice.
   No particles around her, or the cloud would give away her position.
4. If she is already drinking water, fire resistance, or healing, she finishes that sip.
   Invisibility starts right after, with no new chance roll.
   If she was drinking speed, the bottle may switch to invisibility.
5. While invisibility is active, she does not drink another. When it ends, the 15-second countdown restarts.
6. If she already spawned invisible from general spawn potions, she will not drink:
   that effect does not expire.


SETTINGS (WitchInvisibility.java)
----------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false disables new sips
  DRINK_CHANCE ................. 0.10 — chance per check (10%). Was 35%
  CHECK_SECONDS ................ 15 — how often to check in combat. Was 6 seconds
  INVISIBILITY_SECONDS ......... 20 — how long she stays invisible. 180 = full bottle
  SHOW_PARTICLES ............... false — true enables particles and makes her easier to find


TEST
----
1. To see it immediately, temporarily set DRINK_CHANCE = 1.0. After testing, restore 0.10.
2. Summon a witch and hit her while standing close. She should raise a bottle, drink, and vanish.
3. Wait: she still throws potions while invisible. After 20 seconds she is visible again.
4. Set ENABLED = false. New sips stop; normal witch potions remain.

NULLIFIER WITCH
===============

Type: rare variant of a normal witch. Looks like vanilla.
Code: witch/WitchNullifier.java (settings, effect removal, command),
     mixin/WitchNullifierMixin.java (flag and pause between white potions),
     mixin/WitchNullifierMobMixin.java (spawn chance and persistence),
     mixin/ThrownSplashPotionNullifyMixin.java (swap bottle to white),
     mixin/AbstractThrownPotionNullifyMixin.java (hit clears effects).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn, SPAWN_CHANCE (15%). Witch hut, raid, and spawner all use this chance.
2. The witch still drinks potions for herself and throws poison, slowness, and damage.
   If the cooldown has passed, the next throw at the player is a white splash potion with no effect of its own.
   The first throw at the player does not wait for cooldown.
3. A player in the splash loses all effect icons at once: potions, golden apple, beacon beam.
   Poison, wither, and weakness drop too — the potion clears everything, not only buffs.
   If you stand in a beacon beam, the beacon reapplies the effect on its own.
4. Other mobs in the splash do not lose their effects.
   Allies in a raid still get healing throws, not white potions.
5. Shield does not block the white potion: splash potions are not blocked by shield in the game.
6. The next white potion is not sooner than 15 seconds later.
   Normal throws during that pause work like a regular witch.
   While the witch is drinking, she throws nothing — step back and wait a couple of seconds.


COMMAND
-------
  /etwitch
  /evolvingthreats spawn witch
The witch spawns 8 blocks in front of where you look and immediately faces you.
Requires operator permissions, like other mod commands.


SETTINGS (WitchNullifier.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false disables both spawn and effect removal
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.15 — world spawn chance (15%). Command does not roll chance
  THROW_COOLDOWN_SECONDS ....... 15 — pause between white potions
  POTION_COLOR ................. 0xF4F4F4 — splash color, almost white
  SPLASH_RADIUS_BLOCKS ......... 4 — horizontal splash, same as normal potion
  SPLASH_VERTICAL_BLOCKS ....... 2 — up and down, same as normal potion


TEST
----
1. Drink strength, speed, or fire resistance. You can apply poison too — it will also clear.
2. Run /etwitch and step back several blocks so she starts throwing.
3. The first white bottle clears all icons. Gulp sound means effects really dropped.
4. For about 15 seconds the next throws are normal (poison, slowness, damage). Then white again.
