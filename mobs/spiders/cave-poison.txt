CAVE SPIDER: POISON CAN KILL AGAIN
===================================

Type: regular cave spider bite, not a separate mob.
Code: spider/CaveSpiderPoison.java (toggle),
     mixin/CaveSpiderPoisonMixin.java (remembers poison is from cave spider),
     mixin/PoisonTickMixin.java (final tick can kill).

When: from day one, always. Not a chance and not tied to the threat scale.
Moon does not matter. Siege night does not disable this poison.


SAME AS VANILLA
----------------------
The mod does not change the seconds. The game sets them:
- Easy difficulty — bite does not apply poison;
- Normal — Poison I for 7 seconds;
- Hard — Poison I for 15 seconds.

Every 1.25 seconds half a heart is removed. Armor does not reduce this damage.
The mod still raises cave spider health to 16–30. That is separate.


WHAT CHANGED
--------------
In vanilla, poison stops at half a heart and cannot kill.
Cave spider poison can now remove that last half heart.

Does not finish off: witch poison, slime poison, drunk poison potion.
If a longer witch or slime poison is applied on top of the spider bite,
there is nothing left to finish — their poison is what is active.
Milk removes poison along with this special behavior.

Disable the finishing blow: ENABLED = false at the top of CaveSpiderPoison.java.
Bite duration in seconds does not get shorter because of this.
