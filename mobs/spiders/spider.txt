SPIDERS: WEAVER AND MATRIARCH
===================

Type: rare variants of the regular spider (not separate mobs in the menu).
Code: spider/SpiderThreats.java (all settings and rules),
     entity/ai/SpiderWebWeaveGoal.java (weaver behavior),
     mixin/SpiderRoleMixin.java (chance on spawn + goal hookup),
     mixin/SpiderRoleSaveMixin.java (role persists in the world).

Who participates: regular spider only.
Who does not: cave spiders, spider jockeys (skeleton on top).
One roll on spawn: matriarch → weaver → swarm (see "SPIDERS: SWARM" below) → regular.
A summoned spider (swarm helper) skips this roll and stays regular.


WEAVER (WEAVER_SPAWN_CHANCE = 10%)
-------------------------------
Looks the same as a regular spider.
While it sees the player (up to 12 blocks, line of sight) — every 5–9 sec it places cobweb
in the cell under the player's feet. Cobweb stays FOREVER (does not despawn on its own) —
remove with sword, shears, or by breaking the block.
If there is nowhere to place cobweb (cell occupied / water) — retry in 1 sec.
Does not work if /gamerule mobGriefing is off (RESPECT_MOB_GRIEFING).


MATRIARCH (MATRIARCH_SPAWN_CHANCE = 4%)
-----------------------------------
Spider with 40 HP (regular has 16), size x1.3 — to tell them apart.
On spawn, 1–4 cave spiders appear nearby (within 2.5 blocks).
Cave spiders are regular — not matriarchs or weavers.
Matriarchs do not appear during chunk generation (there is no real world there for reinforcements).


SETTINGS (SpiderThreats.java)
------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — enable/disable all
  WEAVER_SPAWN_CHANCE .......... 0.10 — weaver chance
  MATRIARCH_SPAWN_CHANCE ....... 0.04 — matriarch chance
  WEB_COOLDOWN_MIN/MAX_SECONDS . 5 / 9 — pause between cobwebs
  WEB_MAX_DISTANCE ............. 12 — range for placing web
  MATRIARCH_MAX_HEALTH ......... 40 — matriarch health
  MATRIARCH_CAVE_SPIDERS_MIN/MAX 1 / 4 — how many cave spiders
  MATRIARCH_SCALE .............. 1.3 — matriarch size (1.0 = regular)


TEST
----
1. Temporarily set WEAVER_SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0 (and MATRIARCH = 0.0) — approach a spider at night:
   cobweb should appear under your feet.
2. For matriarch: MATRIARCH_SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0 — spider is bigger, 1–4 small ones nearby.
3. Cobweb remains until you break it.

SPIDERS: SWARM
============

Type: rare pack of regular spiders (not a separate mob in the menu).
Code: spider/SpiderSwarm.java (pack size, chance, where to place),
     spider/SpiderThreats.java (shared roll: matriarch, then weaver, then swarm),
     mixin/SpiderRoleMixin.java (called when a spider appears).

Who starts a swarm: regular spider only, and only if it did not roll matriarch or weaver.
Who does not: cave spiders, spider jockeys (skeleton on top), spiders already
summoned by another mob. Swarm helpers do not start a new swarm themselves.

During new chunk generation no swarm forms: the spider there stays alone.
Otherwise there would be nowhere to place the pack.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (2%). Decided once, together with matriarch and weaver.
2. Pack has PACK_MIN to PACK_MAX spiders (6–10), including the one that started the swarm.
3. The rest spawn nearby on free floor: block under feet, body not in wall,
   not in water or lava. They do not suffocate in blocks.
4. If there is no spot for someone (narrow tunnel), that spider does not appear. Pack will be smaller.
5. Everyone in the pack is regular: no weaver cobweb and no matriarch entourage.


SETTINGS (SpiderSwarm.java)
----------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — enable/disable swarm
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.02 — swarm chance (2%). 0.0 = never, 1.0 = almost every
                                   spider that did not roll matriarch or weaver
  PACK_MIN / PACK_MAX .......... 6 / 10 — total spider count
  RADIUS_MIN / RADIUS_MAX ...... 2 / 6 — how far from the first to search for a spot, in blocks
  SPOT_TRIES ................... 16 — how many points to try per spider
  VERTICAL_STEPS ............... 3 — how many blocks up and down to still search for floor


TEST
----
1. In SpiderThreats set MATRIARCH_SPAWN_CHANCE = 0 and WEAVER_SPAWN_CHANCE = 0.
2. In SpiderSwarm set SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0.
3. Find a spider at night: several more should be nearby, all on the floor, not in walls.
4. Restore chances afterward, or almost every spider will be a swarm.

CAVE SPIDER: POISON CAN KILL AGAIN
===================================

Type: regular cave spider bite, not a separate mob.
Code: spider/CaveSpiderPoison.java (toggle),
     mixin/CaveSpiderPoisonMixin.java (remembers poison is from cave spider),
     mixin/PoisonTickMixin.java (final tick can kill).

When: from day one, always. Not a chance and not tied to the threat scale.
Moon does not matter. Siege night does not disable this poison.


SAME AS VANILLA
----------------------
The mod does not change the seconds. The game sets them:
- Easy difficulty — bite does not apply poison;
- Normal — Poison I for 7 seconds;
- Hard — Poison I for 15 seconds.

Every 1.25 seconds half a heart is removed. Armor does not reduce this damage.
The mod still raises cave spider health to 16–30. That is separate.


WHAT CHANGED
--------------
In vanilla, poison stops at half a heart and cannot kill.
Cave spider poison can now remove that last half heart.

Does not finish off: witch poison, slime poison, drunk poison potion.
If a longer witch or slime poison is applied on top of the spider bite,
there is nothing left to finish — their poison is what is active.
Milk removes poison along with this special behavior.

Disable the finishing blow: ENABLED = false at the top of CaveSpiderPoison.java.
Bite duration in seconds does not get shorter because of this.
