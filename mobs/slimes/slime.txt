SLIME POISON
=============

Type: hit from a normal slime. Magma cube does not apply poison.
Code: slime/SlimePoison.java (chances and effect strength),
     mixin/SlimePoisonMixin.java (moment of the hit).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
The chance is rolled when a slime hit actually removed health.
A shield that blocked the hit, and brief invulnerability right after a hit, do not roll again.

Large (size 4, and any size greater than 2):
  30% — Poison II for 15 seconds.
Medium (size 2):
  20% — Poison I for 15 seconds.
Small (size 1):
  no poison. It barely deals damage anyway.

If the player already has poison that is stronger or lasts longer, a weaker hit does not overwrite it.


SETTINGS (SlimePoison.java)
-----------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  LARGE_CHANCE ................. 0.30 — large slime chance (30%)
  MEDIUM_CHANCE ................ 0.20 — medium slime chance (20%)
  POISON_SECONDS ............... 15 — how many seconds poison lasts
  MEDIUM_SIZE .................. 2
  LARGE_MIN_SIZE ............... 3 — slime counts as large from this size up
  MEDIUM_AMPLIFIER ............. 0 — Poison I
  LARGE_AMPLIFIER .............. 1 — Poison II


TEST
----
1. Summon a large slime and let it hit you several times. About every third hit — Poison II.
2. Kill it, wait for mediums. Their hits sometimes give Poison I.
3. Small slimes do not apply poison.
4. Magma cube does not apply poison.

MAGMA CUBE
==========

Type: normal magma cube. Does not change the slime; slime poison still does not apply.
Code: magma/MagmaCubeThreats.java (all numbers),
     mixin/MagmaCubeThreatMixin.java (health by size and jumps while overheated),
     mixin/MagmaCubeTickMixin.java (overheat and magma each tick),
     mixin/SlimePoisonMixin.java (on hit: harder hits and sets fire; slime poison lives here too).


WHAT HAPPENS
------------
Twice as much health as in vanilla.
  Large: 32 (16 hearts) instead of 16.
  Medium: 8 instead of 4.
  Small: 2 instead of 1.
Hits deal one and a half times normal damage.
  Large: 9 instead of 6. Medium: 6 instead of 4.
  Small does not hit with melee under vanilla rules.

Each hit that lands sets the player on fire for 4 seconds.
A shield that blocked the hit does not set fire.

While standing on the ground, about every 4 seconds with a 45% chance
it replaces the block under itself with heated magma.
The magma stays. Works on netherrack, nylium, soul sand, blackstone, basalt,
dirt, grass block, sand, gravel, stone, deepslate, and snow block.
Does not touch chests, wood, obsidian, or ores.
If the world has “mob griefing” turned off, it does not place magma.

Overheat. Medium and large cubes, after chasing the target for 6 seconds, have a 50% chance
to enter a stage for 8 seconds. Lava flash particles show; sound like a blaze shooting.
In that stage it is 70% faster, jumps twice as often, and hits deal double damage:
large hits for 18, medium for 12. Then 16 seconds of cooldown.
Small cubes do not overheat.


SETTINGS (MagmaCubeThreats.java)
---------------------------------
ENABLED — false restores the vanilla cube.
HEALTH_MULTIPLIER — 2.0, health multiplier vs vanilla.
DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER — 1.5, normal hit damage multiplier.
FIRE_CHANCE — 1.0, chance to ignite (100%).
FIRE_SECONDS — 4.
PLACE_MAGMA — false removes magma under feet.
PLACE_CHANCE — 0.45.
PLACE_INTERVAL_SECONDS — 4.
RESPECT_MOB_GRIEFING — true respects the world game rule.
OVERHEAT_ENABLED — false removes only the overheat stage.
OVERHEAT_MIN_SIZE — 2.
OVERHEAT_WARMUP_SECONDS — 6.
OVERHEAT_CHANCE — 0.50.
OVERHEAT_SECONDS — 8.
OVERHEAT_COOLDOWN_SECONDS — 16.
OVERHEAT_SPEED_MULTIPLIER — 1.7.
OVERHEAT_JUMP_DIVISOR — 2.
OVERHEAT_DAMAGE_MULTIPLIER — 2.0.


TEST
----
1. Summon a large magma cube. Health bar is longer than usual.
2. Let it hit you: the player catches fire, damage is noticeably higher.
3. Stand on netherrack for several seconds. Magma sometimes appears under the cube.
4. Let it chase you for about ten seconds. About half the time it flares up and gets faster and deadlier.
