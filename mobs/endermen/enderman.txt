Endermen changing places
==========================

Type: A normal enderman when he is already angry with the player.
Code: enderman/EndermanSwap.java (pause, chance, range)
mixin/EndermanSwapMixin.java (the moment an enderman thinks on a server).


What's going on?
--------------
The usual teleport of the Enderman has not gone anywhere: from the impact, from the look at point-blank range and from the water, he jumps as before.

In addition, while the goal is the player:
1. The first 8 seconds of anger does nothing.
2. Then, with a 45% chance, the player changes places if the player is less than 24 blocks.
3. The player is where the Enderman was. Enderman is where the player was.
4. Both places are checked: air for the whole height and solid ground under the feet.
If the player is tight (low ceiling for the enderman) or someone would be in the block – no exchange.

If the player is in the water, there is no exchange: the enderman is not put in the water.
We are looking for land 8-24 blocks from the player, no closer than 3 blocks from the water.
You need two blocks of air and ground under your feet. It doesn't teleport inside the stone.
If there is no such point, the player remains in the water.

After luck, pause 14 seconds. After the failure – 4 seconds and a new attempt.


SETTINGS (EndermanSwap.java)
-----------------------------
ENABLED - False only turns off this technique.
FIRST_WAIT_SECONDS — 8.
COOLDOWN_SECONDS — 14.
RETRY_SECONDS — 4.
SWAP_CHANCE — 0.45 (45%).
MAX_RANGE_BLOCKS — 24.
DRY_MIN_BLOCKS — 8.
DRY_MAX_BLOCKS — 24.
DRY PADDING - 3, water is no closer than that.
SEARCH UP - 32, how far up to look for the surface.
SEARCH_DOWN — 6.
RESCUE_TRIES — 24.


TEST
----
1. Look Enderman in the eye and fight for ten seconds. He can swap seats with you, with the sound of the teleport.
2. Go into the water near the evil Enderman. He should throw you out on land, not in the wall or on the edge.
3. Stand in a hole two blocks high. The exchange should not be: the enderman does not fit in there.

Enderman takes and puts blocks
==============================

Type: ordinary enderman. Anger is not necessary: drags blocks and calmly.
Code: enderman/EndermanBlocks.java (how often)
mixin/EndermanTakeBlockMixin.java (attempt to take)
mixin/EndermanPlaceBlockMixin.java (attempt to put).


How it works
------------
1. FIXED: From day one, the threat scale does not increase.
2. Takes the block next to you more often. Try on average once every quarter of a second
(In a normal game, once in half a second).
Watches one random block in a couple of steps. If there is air or something that cannot be carried,
The attempt is empty and he tries again.
3. Put a block that already carries, tries on average every 2 seconds
(In a normal game, once every 50 seconds, so the block hangs in your hands for a long time).
The place is also random: you need air over a solid block.
4. The list of blocks is the same as in the game: earth, sand, gravel and other things that the enderman can carry.
He doesn't take stone, boards and chests.
5. If “mobs break blocks” is turned off in the world, the enderman does not touch the blocks.


SETTINGS
---------
  EndermanBlocks.java
ENABLED...............false again as in a regular game
TAKE INTERVAL .... 5 - pause between attempts to take, in ticks. 20 ticks = 1 second.
Less is more often. 1 = every tick.
PLACE INTERVAL - 40 - pause between attempts to bet. Less is faster than that.
