SKELETON WITH A FAST LONG-RANGE BOW
===================================

Type: regular skeleton and stray that still have a bow.
Code: skeleton/FastBowSkeleton.java (chance, range, pause, arrow speed),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (roll at spawn),
     mixin/AbstractSkeletonFastBowMixin.java (range and arrow speed),
     mixin/RangedBowAttackGoalMixin.java (short draw time).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (15%), and only if it holds a bow.
   Two swords take the bow away — such a skeleton does not get this chance.
2. Shoots from 30 blocks instead of 15. Notices the player from 36 blocks instead of 16.
3. Draws the bow for half a second instead of a full second. The arrow is still full-strength, not weak.
4. The pause after a shot is half as long: on hard difficulty half a second instead of a second,
   on normal — a second instead of two. In total it shoots roughly twice as often.
5. The arrow flies faster (2.4 instead of 1.6) so it reaches the new range.
   The aim is lower than a regular skeleton's: a fast arrow drops less,
   and with the old aim it flew over the head. How much to raise the aim is calculated
   automatically from the speed, there is no separate number.
6. Does not occur on a wither skeleton, bogged or parched skeletons, raid skeletons,
   the small skeleton and the "700 IQ" skeleton.


SETTINGS
---------
  FastBowSkeleton.java
    ENABLED .................... true
    SPAWN_CHANCE ............... 0.15 — 15%
    ATTACK_RANGE_BLOCKS ........ 30 — distance it shoots from
    FOLLOW_RANGE ............... 36 — distance it sees from
    DRAW_TICKS ................. 10 — bow draw time, 20 ticks = 1 second
    HARD_PAUSE_TICKS ........... 10 — pause on hard difficulty
    OTHER_PAUSE_TICKS .......... 20 — pause on easy and normal
    ARROW_SPEED ................ 2.4 — arrow speed, 1.6 for a regular one

SKELETON WITH A HARMFUL ARROW
=============================

Type: variant of the regular skeleton and stray.
Code: skeleton/HarmfulArrowSkeleton.java (chance, arrow list, duration),
     skeleton/SpectralArrowHunt.java (hunt after a spectral arrow),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (given at spawn, before the sword and shield),
     mixin/SpectralArrowHuntMixin.java (spectral arrow hit),
     mixin/MobSpectralHuntMixin.java (mobs do not forget the target).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (20%). One harmful arrow in the off-hand, a bow in the main hand.
2. The skeleton shoots only that arrow. It does not take a regular arrow.
   The arrow never runs out. No sword in the off-hand and no shield are given to this skeleton.
3. Which arrow it gets is random and equal. The list: slowness, instant damage, poison, weakness
   (regular, long and strong, where they exist), wind charge, weaving, oozing, infestation, spectral,
   blindness, wither, hunger, nausea and levitation. Helpful potions and turtle master are not used.
   Blindness is tinted with the weakness colour (dark gray), wither — with the weaving colour (brown).
   Hunger is olive, nausea is purple, levitation is pale blue. These are their own colours.
   These effects have no arrows of their own in the game.
   Blindness about 8 sec, wither I about 7 sec.
   Hunger and nausea last a random 1 to 10 minutes, level I.
   Levitation 8 seconds: a rise of about 8 blocks, then a fall.
4. Effects last twice as long as a vanilla arrow (EFFECT_DURATION_SCALE 0.25 instead of 0.125).
   Poison II — about 5.4 sec. Instant damage does not depend on duration.
5. A skeleton's spectral arrow that hits the player: all hostile mobs within 80 blocks
   go for them for 30 seconds, even if the player is behind a wall. Peaceful animals do not react.
   The glow from the spectral arrow stays vanilla (about 10 seconds).
6. Does not occur on the "700 IQ" skeleton: it has its own arrow.
   Does not occur on the random-arrow skeleton: it does not hold an arrow in its hand.
7. A regular harmful arrow drops with a chance of about 8.5%.
   Blindness, wither, hunger, nausea and levitation arrows never drop, even with Looting.


SETTINGS
---------
  HarmfulArrowSkeleton.java
    ENABLED .................... true
    SPAWN_CHANCE ............... 0.20 — 20%
    EFFECT_DURATION_SCALE ...... 0.25 — duration of the potion on the arrow. Higher = longer
    DROP_CHANCE ................ 0.085 — does not apply to arrows without a vanilla potion
    BLINDNESS_SECONDS .......... 8 — seconds of blindness
    WITHER_SECONDS ............. 7 — seconds of wither I
    HUNGER_MIN_MINUTES ......... 1 — hunger, minimum minutes
    HUNGER_MAX_MINUTES ......... 10 — hunger, maximum minutes
    NAUSEA_MIN_MINUTES ......... 1 — nausea, minimum minutes
    NAUSEA_MAX_MINUTES ......... 10 — nausea, maximum minutes
    LEVITATION_SECONDS ......... 8 — seconds of levitation

  SpectralArrowHunt.java
    AGGRO_RADIUS ............... 80 — blocks
    HUNT_SECONDS ............... 30 — how long mobs go for the player

SKELETON WITH RANDOM ARROWS
===========================

Type: regular skeleton and stray. A separate variant, not a mix with "700 IQ".
Code: skeleton/RandomArrowSkeleton.java (chance, effect cycle, shot, command),
     mixin/RandomArrowSkeletonDataMixin.java (queue memory),
     mixin/RandomArrowSkeletonSaveMixin.java (saving to the world),
     mixin/RandomArrowSkeletonAttackMixin.java (shot instead of a regular arrow),
     mixin/RandomArrowHitMixin.java (burn duration),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (chance at spawn, no arrow in hand).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (1%). FIXED: from day one, does not grow with the threat scale.
   Only the regular skeleton and stray, and only if the bow was not taken by two swords.
   Cadaver, bogged, parched, wither and raid skeletons do not roll this chance.
2. A bow in hand, no effect arrow there. The effect is chosen at the moment of the shot.
   Armor, enchantments on the bow, shield, second weapon, fast bow and special heads —
   as on a regular skeleton. A harmful arrow is not given to its off-hand.
3. A cycle of ten, in random order: fire, blindness, poison, hunger,
   slowness, weakness, wither, nausea, levitation, instant damage.
   The same effect does not come up twice until all the others have.
   A new cycle does not start with the effect that was just shot.
4. Fire 5 seconds. Blindness 8, poison 5, hunger 30, slowness 15, weakness 15,
   wither 7, nausea 8, levitation 8. All of these are level I.
   Instant damage — level I, three hearts, no duration.
   The player does not get the arrow.
5. The fast bow can drop separately, with its usual chance.
   Then this skeleton shoots the same cycle, only more often and from farther away.
   The small skeleton does not get a fast bow, like all other small ones.
6. A dispenser on the head, if it dropped, shoots a regular volley. The cycle is for the bow only.


COMMAND
-------
  /etarrows
  /evolvingthreats spawn random_arrow
Appears 4 blocks away in the direction you look, with a bow right away. The chance is not rolled.
Operator permissions required.


SETTINGS (RandomArrowSkeleton.java)
------------------------------------

  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.01 — 1%, one in a hundred. FIXED from day one. 0 = command only
  FIRE_SECONDS ................. 5 — fire, seconds
  BLINDNESS_SECONDS ............ 8 — blindness, seconds
  POISON_SECONDS ............... 5 — poison I, seconds
  HUNGER_SECONDS ............... 30 — hunger I, seconds
  SLOWNESS_SECONDS ............. 15 — slowness I, seconds
  WEAKNESS_SECONDS ............. 15 — weakness I, seconds
  WITHER_SECONDS ............... 7 — wither I, seconds
  NAUSEA_SECONDS ............... 8 — nausea I, seconds
  LEVITATION_SECONDS ........... 8 — levitation I, seconds
  HARM_LEVEL ................... 0 — instant damage I (three hearts). 1 = II, six hearts

  Fast bow — its own numbers are in FastBowSkeleton.java, this skeleton does not change them.


TEST
----
1. /etarrows. A bow in the main hand, no effect arrow in the off-hand.
   Armor and bow enchantments may be present, as on a regular skeleton.
2. Ten hits in a row — ten different effects. The eleventh does not repeat the tenth.
3. If a fast bow dropped together with it, the pause between shots is shorter.

SNIPER SKELETON
===============

Type: regular skeleton and stray. It always has a bow.
Code: skeleton/SniperSkeleton.java (chance, range, arrow speed, command),
     mixin/SniperSkeletonDataMixin.java (marker),
     mixin/SniperSkeletonSaveMixin.java (saving to the world),
     mixin/AbstractSkeletonFastBowMixin.java (speed, aim, spread, range),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (spawn: bow is mandatory, weapon in the off-hand).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (0.2%, one in five hundred). FIXED: from day one, does not grow with the threat scale.
   Only the regular skeleton and stray.
   Cadaver, bogged, parched, wither, raid, "700 IQ" and small skeletons do not become snipers.
2. A bow is always in the main hand. Two swords are not given to it: otherwise it would not be a sniper.
   The off-hand holds a random weapon, like on the skeleton with a bow and a sword.
   Armor and enchantments on the bow and on this weapon — as on a regular skeleton.
   A shield, totem and harmful arrow do not occupy the off-hand.
3. Shoots from 128 blocks. Notices the player from 140.
   The arrow flies at speed 10 (1.6 for a regular one), otherwise it would hit the ground sooner.
   The aim is calculated for every shot and targets the middle of the body.
   A single fixed lift value at long range sent the arrow over the head.
   The spread is small: the regular spread at 128 blocks misses by several blocks.
   Damage does not grow with speed: 6 hearts before armor.
   Power on the bow adds damage on top, like on any arrow.
4. At close range it takes the weapon from the off-hand, like the skeleton with a bow and a sword.
5. The fast bow can drop separately, with its usual chance.
   Then the sniper draws the bow more often. The range and arrow speed stay sniper-level,
   the fast bow does not cut them down to its own 30 blocks.
6. If in the world mobs fall asleep (despawn/freeze) closer than eight chunks, it will not shoot from 128 blocks
   until you come closer. A chunk is 16 blocks. This is a world setting, not a sniper number.


COMMAND
-------
  /etsniper
  /evolvingthreats spawn sniper
Appears 4 blocks away in the direction you look, with a bow and a weapon in the off-hand right away.
The chance is not rolled. Operator permissions required.
To test the range, walk away yourself: it also shoots up close, but the point is the distance.


SETTINGS (SniperSkeleton.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false turns off snipers and the command
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.002 — one in five hundred. FIXED from day one. 0 = command only
  ATTACK_RANGE_BLOCKS .......... 128 — distance it shoots from
  FOLLOW_RANGE ................. 140 — distance it sees from
  ARROW_SPEED .................. 10 — arrow speed. The aim adjusts itself
  AIM_HEIGHT ................... 0.5 — where on the body height. 0 = feet, 1 = top of the head, 0.5 = middle
  INACCURACY ................... 0.15 — spread. 0 = pinpoint. 2 = like hard difficulty, a miss at 128
  DAMAGE_HEARTS ................ 6 — hearts before armor, if the bow has no Power. Flight speed does not increase this

  Fast bow — its own numbers are in FastBowSkeleton.java. On the sniper it only changes how often
  the bow is drawn.


TEST
----
1. /etsniper. A bow in the main hand, a sword or axe in the off-hand, not an arrow and not a shield.
   Armor and enchantments may be present.
2. Walk far away, but with no hill between you. The arrow should hit the body, not pass over the head.
3. Come up close: it takes the weapon from the off-hand.

SKELETON: ARROW KNOCKS ITEMS OUT OF HANDS
=========================================

Type: regular skeleton, stray and bogged (not a new mob).
Code: skeleton/SkeletonDisarm.java (chance),
     mixin/SkeletonDisarmMixin.java (moment the arrow hits).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. An arrow that has already removed health has a 5% chance to knock items out of the hands.
   That is roughly every twentieth such arrow.
2. One roll per hit. If both hands are occupied, both stacks fly out at once.
   An empty hand drops nothing.
3. The item flies forward, where the player is looking, as with the Q key.
   For about two seconds it cannot be picked up, then it lies on the ground.
4. Armor and the rest of the inventory stay.
5. A shield that blocked the arrow, and the short invulnerability flash after a hit, do not give the chance.
   In creative mode health is not removed, but an arrow that hits can still knock the hands out.
   Spectators are not affected. An arrow that killed the player does not knock the hands out separately:
   the items drop with death anyway.
6. The shooter is a skeleton, stray or bogged, including from a dispenser on the head.
   A player's arrow and a drowned's trident do not knock the hands out.


SETTINGS (SkeletonDisarm.java)
-------------------------------
ENABLED — false turns the knocking out off.
DISARM_CHANCE — 0.05 = 5%. 0.02 = barely noticeable. 0.15 = too often. 0 = never. 1 = every arrow.

BOW WITH PUNCH V AND VI
=======================

Type: rare enchantment on the bow of a regular skeleton and stray. Not a new mob.
Code: skeleton/SkeletonPunchBow.java (chance, level, drop ban),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (given after the regular enchantments),
     mixin/SkeletonPunchBowMixin.java (before death the drop chance of this bow = 0).

The skeleton captain is not included: it has its own bow with Punch IV, which does not drop either.
"700 IQ" and the captain's helpers do not roll this chance.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. A skeleton or stray gets the regular kit and regular enchantments (Power, Flame, Punch I–II).
2. If the bow stayed in the hand, there is a CHANCE (12%) that Punch becomes V or VI.
   Of such bows 75% get V and 25% get VI.
   Power and Flame, if already present, stay on the same bow.
3. V knocks back farther than the captain's IV. VI knocks back even farther.
4. This bow does not drop. Neither with the regular chance nor with the Looting enchantment.
   In combat the bow may move to the off-hand (sword into the main hand). The check looks at both hands at the moment of death.
   Bones and arrows from the skeleton's regular loot drop as before.
   Punch I and II can still drop.


SETTINGS (SkeletonPunchBow.java)
---------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false does not hand out new bows. Existing V/VI bows still do not drop
  CHANCE ....................... 0.12 — chance per skeleton with a bow (12%). 1.0 = every one
  PUNCH_LEVEL_V ................ 5
  PUNCH_LEVEL_VI ............... 6
  LEVEL_V_WEIGHT ............... 3 — how often V
  LEVEL_VI_WEIGHT .............. 1 — how often VI. 0 = always only V
  MIN_LEVEL_THAT_NEVER_DROPS ... 5 — from this level the bow does not drop


COMMAND
-------
  /etpunch
  /evolvingthreats spawn punch
A skeleton 6 blocks away in the direction you look, with a Punch VI bow in hand. The chance is not rolled.
  /etpunch v
  /evolvingthreats spawn punch v
The same, but Punch V. Operator permissions required. The bow still does not drop.


TEST
----
1. /etpunch — the bow has Punch VI. /etpunch v — Punch V.
2. Summon a skeleton or wait for night. The bow should have Punch V or VI.
3. Let it hit. The player is flung farther than by the captain's IV.
4. Kill the skeleton. There is no bow on the ground. Bones and arrows may be there.
5. Restore CHANCE = 0.12f.

SMALL SKELETON
==============

Type: regular skeleton and stray.
Code: skeleton/SmallSkeleton.java (chance, size, speed, health),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (roll at spawn, after the common health).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (10%). Decided once at spawn.
2. Scale 0.5 — half the size of a regular one. Both the model and the place where hits land get smaller.
3. Runs 1.5 times faster than a regular skeleton (like a baby zombie relative to an adult).
4. Health 10–16 instead of the adult's 20–40. The common health roll happens earlier
   and is overwritten here.
5. The bow is regular: shoots from the same 15 blocks and with the same pause.
   The long-range fast bow, totem and two melee weapons are not given to it.
   Harmful arrow, shield, armor, enchantments and potions — as on a regular skeleton.
6. Burns in sunlight. When the world loads it stays small: size, speed and health
   are already written into the mob itself.
7. Does not occur on a wither skeleton, bogged or parched skeletons, raid skeletons
   and the "700 IQ" skeleton.


SETTINGS
---------
  SmallSkeleton.java
    ENABLED .................... true
    SPAWN_CHANCE ............... 0.10 — 10%
    SCALE ...................... 0.5 — size, 1.0 = regular
    SPEED_MULTIPLIER ........... 1.5 — how many times faster
    HEALTH_MIN ................. 10
    HEALTH_MAX ................. 16


TEST
----
Summon several skeletons: /summon skeleton
Roughly every tenth should be noticeably shorter and faster.
Kill it: fewer hearts than an adult standing next to it.

SKELETON HORSEMAN
=================

Type: regular skeleton on a skeleton horse. Not a new mob in the menu.
Code: skeleton/SkeletonHorseman.java (chance, horse, command),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (seats the already-equipped skeleton),
     mixin/SkeletonHorseMixin.java (the horse despawns far from the player),
     mixin/SkeletonTrapRiderMixin.java (thunderstorm does not re-equip the rider).

The rider gets everything a skeleton on foot gets: shield, potions, arrows, armor and weapon.
The captain, his helpers, the wither skeleton and a raid participant do not mount a horse.


WHERE IT SPAWNS
--------------
At night in the regular world, if there is open sky above the skeleton.
Caves, spawners, the Nether and the End do not roll this chance.
Chance 5% (SPAWN_CHANCE). This is a single rider, not a squad of four horses.

The vanilla thunder trap stays. Its skeletons are also equipped by the mod.
Previously the thunder trap then put on an iron helmet and re-enchanted the bow and helmet.
Now it does not: the mod's kit stays.


HORSE
------
Tamed, so the skeleton rides and runs toward the player. It is not a trap and not lightning.
Far from the player (128 blocks) both the horse and the rider vanish.
If the rider is killed, the horse vanishes too. If it dismounted by itself — the horse stays.


COMMAND
-------
/ethorseman
or /evolvingthreats spawn horseman
The skeleton appears 8 blocks in front of you, already on the horse, and looks at you.
The command does not roll the spawn chance.

"700 IQ" SKELETON
=================

Type: regular skeleton and stray. A separate variant, not a mix of the others.
Code: skeleton/TacticianSkeleton.java (chance, sword, arrow, weapon swap, health),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (spawn, before armor and other weapons),
     mixin/TacticianSkeletonAttackMixin.java (special arrow),
     mixin/TacticianArrowMixin.java (a hit switches on the sword),
     mixin/TacticianSkeletonMobMixin.java (tick, saving, drop from the back),
     mixin/MeleeAttackGoalMixin.java (runs even if the player is around a corner),
     client/.../TacticianBackItemLayer.java (sword or bow on the back).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (5%). Decided once at spawn.
2. No armor except a helmet. The helmet drops with the same 50% chance as on a regular skeleton,
   any material from leather to diamond, no netherite. Leather may be dyed.
3. Always a bow and one sword. Sword: wood, stone, gold, copper, iron or diamond.
   No netherite. Whatever is not in the hand is visible on the back.
4. Potions on the skeleton itself occur, as on a regular one. Enchantments on the sword — with the skeleton weapon chance,
   enchantments on the helmet — with the skeleton armor chance. The bow has no enchantments.
5. Always shoots a single arrow type: weakness 20 seconds, slowness 20 seconds, blindness 5 seconds.
   Level I. The player does not get the arrow.
6. A hit — bow goes to the back, sword into the hand, runs at that player, even around a corner.
   A shield took the arrow or a miss — no effects, stays with the bow.
7. It keeps the sword while the player has at least one of the three effects. Blindness ends after 5 seconds,
   weakness and slowness after 20, so the sword is usually out for 20 seconds.
   Milk removes the effects earlier — the bow comes right back.
8. Health is always 50. It does not pick up items from the floor, so it cannot put on armor.
   Killing it drops 30 experience. The common experience multiplier does not increase this number.
9. Does not occur together with the small skeleton, long-range bow, totem, two weapons,
   harmful arrow and shield. Not on the wither, bogged, parched and raid skeletons.


SETTINGS
---------
  TacticianSkeleton.java
    ENABLED .................... true
    SPAWN_CHANCE ............... 0.05 — 5%
    HEALTH_MIN ................. 50 — always 50 if equal to HEALTH_MAX
    HEALTH_MAX ................. 50 — 25 hearts
    XP_REWARD .................. 30 — exactly this much, the experience multiplier does not touch it
    WEAKNESS_SECONDS ........... 20
    SLOWNESS_SECONDS ........... 20
    BLINDNESS_SECONDS .......... 5
    SWORD_WEIGHTS .............. wood 30, stone 25, gold 10, copper 10, iron 15, diamond 10

  The helmet and its enchantments — common skeleton numbers:
    RandomArmor.SKELETON_ARMOR_PIECE_CHANCE ... 0.50 — helmet chance
    MobEnchantments.SKELETON_ENCHANT_CHANCE ... 0.30 — chance of enchantments on the sword
    MobEnchantments.SKELETON_ARMOR_ENCHANT_CHANCE ... 0.25 — chance of enchantments on the helmet

  If the sword or bow sits crooked on the back — TacticianBackItemLayer.java:
    OFFSET_X, OFFSET_Y, OFFSET_Z — shift
    ROT_X, ROT_Y, ROT_Z — rotation
    SCALE — size


TEST
----
Command, without searching among regular skeletons: /ettactician
The same: /evolvingthreats spawn tactician
It appears a couple of blocks in front of you, with a bow in hand and a sword on the back.

Stand so that the arrow hits. Weakness, slowness and short blindness should arrive,
and the skeleton should run up with the sword. Drink milk: it should take the bow again.

SKELETON CAPTAIN
================

Type: a rare regular skeleton and two helpers. Not a new mob in the menu.
Code: skeleton/SkeletonCaptain.java (kit, squad, loot, command),
     entity/ai/SkeletonSquadLeashGoal.java (helpers do not wander far),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (chance at spawn),
     mixin/SkeletonSquadMixin.java (who is the captain and who is in the squad, saving),
     mixin/SkeletonSquadGoalMixin.java (captain's hunt and leash),
     entity/ai/CaptainHuntGoal.java (the captain walks to the player himself),
     mixin/SquadFriendlyFireMixin.java (allies do not hit allies),
     mixin/SquadHurtByMixin.java (a friendly hit does not make an enemy),
     mixin/SkeletonSquadMobMixin.java (shared target and extra loot).

The mod's potions, random weapon and a spawner on the head are not given to this squad.


HOW IT LOOKS
------------
The captain. Above his head the name Captain of the Skeletons.
  Visible only when the crosshair is on him and he is no farther than about 64 blocks.
  Diamond helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots.
  Bow: Power V and Punch IV. Punch IV cannot be obtained in regular survival.
  Diamond sword: Sharpness V, Fire Aspect II, Knockback II.
  At range he shoots, up close he hits with the sword.
The helpers, two.
  A full iron set and an iron sword in each hand. They have no bow.
The armor of the captain and helpers, and the helpers' swords, are enchanted separately.
A piece may stay without enchantments, one may be enchanted, all may be.
The enchantments on the captain's bow and sword are always the same, randomness does not replace them.


HOW IT ACTS
-------------
1. The captain walks to the nearest player himself, like the Rotten Overseer.
   Radius 128 blocks. A wall between you does not stop him from setting out.
   Beyond 128 blocks he drops that target. A spectator is not locked on.
   Creative is also pursued (HUNT_CREATIVE). Set false if creative players should be left alone.
   The helpers run after the captain and pick up the same target when they get closer than 32 blocks.
2. Allies do not fight each other. The captain's arrow hitting a guard and a guard's sword hitting the captain
   do no damage and do not make them respond. A foreign squad is still an enemy.
3. The helpers stay near the captain.
   Out of combat, farther than 6 blocks — they catch up.
   In combat they can go 14 blocks away to reach the player with a sword, and beyond that the leash pulls them back.
4. The captain does not run after the helpers: the squad forms around him, and he goes to the player.
   If the captain died, the helpers stick together and do not look for the player from afar on their own.
5. The name does not let the captain despawn on his own. The helpers do not despawn either while the squad is alive.


LOOT
----
The captain's bow never drops. With no chance at all.
From the captain:
  sword with his enchantments ........ 15%
  each piece of armor ................ 8.5%, enchantments on the piece are kept
  always ............................. 1 name tag
  also ............................... 20% one diamond
From a helper:
  each sword and each piece of iron .. 8.5%
  a helper does not drop a name tag or a diamond.
Bones and arrows are dropped by the game as from a regular skeleton, on top of this.

-------

  /evolvingthreats spawn captain
The squad appears 6 blocks away in the direction you look. The chance is not rolled.
Operator permissions required.

  HELPER_SWORD_ENCHANT_CHANCE .. 0.55 — chance of enchantments on each helper sword
  BOW_PUNCH_LEVEL .............. 4 — bow punch (IV). Vanilla cap is 2
  BOW_POWER_LEVEL .............. 5
  SWORD_SHARPNESS_LEVEL ........ 5
  SWORD_FIRE_ASPECT_LEVEL ...... 2

----
1. /etcaptain. The name above the captain, diamond, two iron skeletons with swords in both hands.
2. Move about 40 blocks away, possibly behind a wall. The captain comes to you, the helpers follow him.
3. Stand so that the captain's arrow hits a helper. The helper does not turn on the captain.
4. Kill the captain. There is no bow on the ground. There is a name tag. The sword and diamond — not every time.
5. Kill a helper. No name tag and no diamond.

BASILISK
========

Type: a rare regular skeleton. Not a new mob in the menu.
Code: skeleton/BasiliskSkeleton.java (kit, range, loot, command),
     mixin/BasiliskSkeletonMixin.java (remembers that it is a Basilisk),
     mixin/BasiliskBowMixin.java (shoots from afar),
     mixin/BasiliskLootMixin.java (experience, name tag, skull, bow and armor do not drop),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (chance at spawn),
     mixin/AbstractSkeletonFastBowMixin.java (arrow speed, so it reaches the target),
     mixin/BasiliskShieldMixin.java (arrows accumulate damage on the shield).

The captain, "700 IQ", random weapon and a spawner on the head are not given to it.


HOW IT LOOKS
------------
Above the head the name Basilisk. Visible only when the crosshair is on it and it is no farther than about 64 blocks.
Health 100 (50 hearts). The random 20–40 of a regular skeleton does not remain.
A full netherite set without enchantments. Does not burn in daylight: the helmet shields from the sun.
In the off-hand a black shield with no pattern. It does not drop.
A hit on it is fully absorbed with a 10% chance — this is the mod's common shield (MobShields), not a separate ability.
Bow: Flame, Power V, Punch XX.
Punch XX is stronger than both the captain's IV and the rare VI. It carries the player very far.


HOW IT ACTS
-------------
1. Notices the player from 64 blocks if there is no wall between you. A regular skeleton sees about 16.
2. Starts shooting from 48 blocks, does not come up close.
3. The arrow flies faster than a regular one, to reach the target. On a hit it sets on fire and knocks back hard.
4. If you cover yourself with a shield, every blocked arrow accumulates. The fourth breaks the shield outright.
   This does not stretch the shield's durability. A miss does not count. A different shield counts from scratch.
5. The name does not let it despawn on its own.


LOOT
----
The bow never drops. Netherite never drops. The shield never drops.
Always: 1 name tag with the name Basilisk.
Also: 100 experience. A regular skeleton gives 5.
Also: a skeleton skull with a 2.5% chance.
Bones and arrows are dropped by the game as from a regular skeleton, on top of this.


COMMAND
-------
  /etbasilisk
  /evolvingthreats spawn basilisk
Appears 6 blocks away in the direction you look. The chance is not rolled.
Operator permissions required.


SETTINGS (BasiliskSkeleton.java)
---------------------------------
  ENABLED ..................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................ 0.002 — chance for a regular skeleton (0.2%)
  NAME ........................ Basilisk
  MAX_HEALTH .................. 100 — health, 50 hearts
  XP_REWARD ................... 100
  NAME_TAG_COUNT .............. 1
  SKULL_DROP_CHANCE ........... 0.025 — 2.5% skeleton skull
  BOW_FLAME_LEVEL ............. 1 — Flame
  BOW_POWER_LEVEL ............. 5
  BOW_PUNCH_LEVEL ............. 20 — Punch XX
  SIGHT_BLOCKS ................ 64 — how far it sees
  ATTACK_RANGE_BLOCKS ......... 48 — distance it shoots from
  ARROW_SPEED ................. 3.2 — arrow speed. Lower — it does not reach
  SHIELD_HITS_TO_BREAK ........ 4 — this many blocked arrows break the shield. 0 = never breaks

BEACON (Jack o'Lantern on the head)
===================================

Type: rare zombie or skeleton (not a separate mob).
Code: beacon/JackLanternBeacon.java,
     beacon/JackLanternLight.java (light around the head),
     entity/ai/JackBeaconAuraGoal.java (zombie),
     entity/ai/JackLanternFireShotGoal.java (skeleton),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java, SkeletonMixin.java,
     mixin/ZombieJackBeaconGoalsMixin.java, AbstractSkeletonJackLanternGoalMixin.java.


HOW IT LOOKS
------------
A glowing pumpkin (JACK_O_LANTERN) in the HEAD slot.
The picture on the head does not light up the world by itself, so an invisible light of
level LIGHT_LEVEL (15 — like a lantern block) travels next to the head. Pumpkin removed or mob dead — the light goes out.
A regular carved pumpkin (without light) is NOT used by this mechanic.


WHO CAN GET THE HEAD
--------------------
  Yes:  zombie, husk, zombie villager, skeleton, stray.
  No:   drowned, zombified piglin, Overseer, raid.

  Only one thing per head: a spawner OR a beacon OR (on a skeleton) a dispenser.


ZOMBIE — PACK BEACON
--------------------
1. Does not go to break torches (excluded from LightSeeking / LightSeekGoal).
2. If it sees the player (line of sight) — about once per second a "pulse" within BEACON_RADIUS:
   neighboring zombies get Speed and (if enabled) a target on the same player, if they had no target.


SKELETON — FIRE SHOT
--------------------
Once every 8–14 sec (random) one fire arrow at the target, if within range and with line of sight.
In addition to the regular bow (does not replace the dispenser and is not a volley).


SETTINGS (JackLanternBeacon.java)
----------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  JACK_HEAD_DROP_CHANCE ........ 0.085 — chance the pumpkin drops
  LIGHT_LEVEL .................. 15 — brightness around the head, 0 = no light. This is in JackLanternLight.java
  CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE ......... 0.01 — chance at spawn on day 100 (1%). At the start of the game 10 times lower
  BEACON_RADIUS ................ 20 — radius of the pack speed-up
  BEACON_PULSE_INTERVAL_TICKS .. 20 — how often the pulse
  ALLY_SPEED_AMPLIFIER ......... 0 — Speed I
  SHARE_TARGET_WITH_ALLIES ..... true — pass the aggro to allies without a target
  FIRE_SHOT_COOLDOWN_* ......... 8–14 sec between fire arrows


TEST
----
1. CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0 — check the head and the behavior.
2. Zombie beacon + pack: kill the light, attack — neighboring zombies should run faster while the beacon sees you.
3. Skeleton beacon: wait for the fire arrow between regular shots.

SPAWNER CARRIER (Spawner Head)
==============================

Type: rare variant of the regular zombie or skeleton (not a separate mob in the menu).
Code: spawnerhead/SpawnerHeadMob.java,
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java, SkeletonMixin.java,
     mixin/LivingEntitySpawnerHeadMixin.java (player hits).


HOW IT LOOKS
------------
A spawner block on the head (like the skeleton with a dispenser — an item in the HEAD slot).
Overrides the helmet. On a skeleton the spawner and the dispenser on the head do not drop together:
if a spawner dropped — the dispenser is not put on.

Smoke and fire above the head (like a real spawner) — client side only,
code: client/spawnerhead/SpawnerHeadParticles.java (ENABLED, height HEAD_CENTER_ABOVE_FEET).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On a regular spawn the chance to become a carrier is CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE (0.5% on day 100).
2. Every 2 player hits (sword, bow, any damage from the player) 1–2 mobs of the same kind appear nearby
   (a zombie calls a zombie, a skeleton a skeleton, a husk a husk…). The hit counter resets after the summon.
3. The summoned reinforcements can NEVER get a spawner on the head (no "overpowered" chain).
4. Reinforcements go through a regular finalizeSpawn (armor/weapon by the mod's rules), but without the carrier role.


WHO CAN BE A CARRIER
--------------------
  Yes:  zombie, husk, zombie villager, skeleton, stray.
  No:   drowned, zombified piglin, Overseer boss, raid minions.
  The wither skeleton is separate, see the section "WITHER SKELETON WITH A SPAWNER" below.
  (always two per summon, its own chance).


SETTINGS (SpawnerHeadMob.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — on/off
  CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE ......... 0.005 — chance at spawn on day 100 (0.5%). At the start of the game 10 times lower
  HITS_TO_SUMMON ............... 2 — player hits before the summon
  SUMMON_COUNT_MIN / MAX ....... 1 / 2 — how many mobs to summon
  SUMMON_RADIUS ................ 3.0 — spawn radius (blocks)
  SPAWNER_HEAD_DROP_CHANCE ..... 0.085 — drop of the spawner from the head


TEST
----
1. Night, wait for zombies/skeletons or /summon — look at the head (very rare, 0.5% by day 100).
2. Hit the carrier 2 times — 1–2 of the same kind should appear nearby.
3. To see it more often: temporarily set CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0.

RITUAL BAND
===========

Type: a rare group, not a separate mob. 1–2 regular zombies and 1–2 regular skeletons.
Code: ritual/RitualBand.java (settings, behavior, loot, command),
     mixin/RitualZombieMixin.java and RitualSkeletonMixin.java (memory and saving to the world),
     mixin/RitualMobMixin.java (experience and extra items),
     mixin/RitualHuntMixin.java (the player head senses through walls, the piglin sun is put out),
     mixin/RitualHurtMixin.java (allies do not hit allies, piglin fire, wither),
     mixin/RitualDeathMixin.java (creeper head pop).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Chance SPAWN_CHANCE (0.2%, one in five hundred) for a regular adult zombie and a regular skeleton.
   FIXED: from day one, does not grow with the threat scale.
   Cadaver, stray, baby, Overseer and raid mobs do not become a band.
   A summoned band-mate does not roll the chance again.
2. Everyone in the band has the same head. Which one it is decides the behavior.
   Dark green skin, no helmet. In hand a red torch or a bone, both are present in the band.
   Enchantments, random weapon and the mod's potions are not given. They do not break blocks.
3. Health of each is 50. A hit from anyone in the band removes 4 hearts before armor.
   A regular zombie does one and a half, a skeleton with a bone one.
   Without armor the player survives two hits.
4. Anyone in the band notices the player from 52 blocks if it sees them.
   The player head senses through walls at the same 52 blocks.
   The head does not drop. If a player killed it — a compass drops with a 2.5% chance, like the other heads.
   Looting I/II/III raises it to 3.5% / 4.5% / 5.5%.
5. Creeper head. On death a small explosion: hurts nearby, barely breaks blocks.
   Does not hurt or fling its allies. The head drops rarely, like the skull from a wither skeleton.
   Gunpowder also drops, including from zombies.
6. Piglin head. Lava, fire and sunlight do not harm it: a zombie in the open does not catch fire.
   The head drops rarely, like the skull from a wither skeleton. One gold ingot also drops.
7. Wither skull. A hit applies wither for 4 seconds.
   While it is still active, the next hit does not refresh it.
   The skull drops rarely, like from a wither skeleton. Coal and a wither rose also drop.
8. A piece of dark green leather sometimes drops, like regular armor (about 8.5%).
   A red torch from the hand — with a 35% chance. A bone from the hand does not drop.
   Experience from each is 50. A regular zombie and skeleton give 10 in this mod, and the common multiplier does not double the band.


COMMAND
-------
  /etritual
  /etritual player
  /etritual creeper
  /etritual piglin
  /etritual wither
  /evolvingthreats spawn ritual
  /evolvingthreats spawn ritual wither
Without a word at the end the head is random. The band appears 6 blocks away in the direction you look.
Operator permissions required.


SETTINGS (RitualBand.java)
---------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false turns the band off
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.002 — 0.2%, one in five hundred. FIXED from day one. 0 = command only
  XP_REWARD .................... 50 — experience from each. Does not touch the experience multiplier. 0 = no experience
  LEATHER_COLOR ................ 0x1B4F2A — dark green leather
  LEATHER_DROP_CHANCE .......... 0.085 — chance of a piece of leather
  TORCH_DROP_CHANCE ............ 0.35 — chance of the torch from the hand
  HEAD_DROP_CHANCE ............. 0.025 — 2.5% for the head and for the compass.
                                 Looting I/II/III = 3.5% / 4.5% / 5.5%
  MAX_HEALTH ................... 50 — 25 hearts for each in the band
  ATTACK_DAMAGE ................ 8 — four hearts before armor. 2 damage = 1 heart
  GUNPOWDER_COUNT .............. 1 — gunpowder
  GOLD_INGOT_COUNT ............. 1 — gold ingot
  COAL_COUNT ................... 1 — coal
  ROSE_COUNT ................... 1 — wither rose
  SENSE_BLOCKS ................. 52 — distance from which they notice. The player head at this same distance senses through walls
  EXPLOSION_POWER .............. 1.5 — the pop, a creeper's is 3
  WITHER_SECONDS ............... 4 — duration of wither
  WITHER_REFRESH_IF_BELOW_SECONDS  1 — while the effect is longer than this, a hit does not refresh it


TEST
----
1. /etritual player — everyone has the player head. Step behind a wall closer than 52 blocks: they come to you.
   Kill them. A compass — rarely, about 2.5%. No player heads.
2. /etritual creeper — death pops. A neighbor from the band stays in place. Gunpowder drops.
   The head — rarely, about 2.5%.
3. /etritual piglin — lava does not burn. In daylight in the sun the zombie does not catch fire. An ingot drops.
   The head — rarely, about 2.5%.
4. /etritual wither — a hit applies wither and does not refresh it on every poke.
   Coal and a rose drop. The skull — rarely, about 2.5%.
   A hit from any band member removes 4 hearts before armor. Each has 50 health.

WITHER SKELETON: SWORD AND EXPERIENCE
=====================================

Type: regular wither skeleton (not a new mob).
Code: skeleton/WitherSkeletonSword.java (sword chance, enchantments, experience),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (moment of spawn),
     mixin/MobExperienceMixin.java (its own experience number).

Who gets netherite and enchantments: a wither skeleton that spawned in the Nether.
A spawner carrier on the head — no, it keeps a stone sword without the mod's enchantments.
A siege in the regular world — no either.
Experience 25 — for any wither skeleton, in the Nether and in a siege.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. In the Nether at spawn there is a chance of a netherite sword instead of a stone one.
   GROWING: at the start about 1.5%, by day 100 15%. The moon shifts the chance slightly.
   The rest keep the stone sword.
2. The mod may put enchantments on both swords. The chance and strength grow with the threat scale,
   like on the zombie's sword: at the start about 1%, by day 100 70%.
   The enchantments are the same: sharpness, knockback, fire aspect, looting and the rest from the sword.
   If the mod's enchantments rolled, they replace those the game already put on.
   If they did not — the stone sword keeps the game's own enchantments.
3. The netherite sword does not drop, even with Looting.
   The stone one drops as before, about 8.5%, and its enchantments too.
4. If a player killed it — experience 25. Before it was about 12–16.
   The common multiplier does not double this number a second time.
   If the wither skeleton burned to death by itself — no experience.


SETTINGS (WitherSkeletonSword.java)
------------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false removes netherite and the mod's enchantments. Experience stays
  NETHERITE_CHANCE ............. 0.15 — 15% on day 100. At the start about 1.5%. 0 = no netherite
  ENCHANT_CHANCE ............... 0.70 — 70% enchantments on day 100. At the start about 1%. 0 = no mod enchantments
  XP_REWARD .................... 25 — experience. -1 = as a regular mob again. 0 = no experience
  Starting share of the dangerous chance . ThreatTable.DANGEROUS_START_RATIO = 0.10
  Starting enchantment chance .. ThreatTable.WEAPON_ENCHANT_CHANCE_START = 0.01


TEST
----
1. To see netherite right away, temporarily set NETHERITE_CHANCE = 1.0.
   Summon a wither skeleton in the Nether. A netherite sword in hand.
   Kill it. The sword does not drop. Experience 25 if you killed it.
2. Set NETHERITE_CHANCE = 0 and ENCHANT_CHANCE = 1.0.
   A stone sword with enchantments in hand. It may drop.
3. Restore 0.15 and 0.70. Summon in the regular world: no netherite and no mod enchantments, experience is still 25.
   A spawner carrier in the Nether also keeps a stone sword.

WITHER SKELETON WITH A SPAWNER
==============================

Type: rare variant of the regular wither skeleton. Not a new mob.
Code: spawnerhead/SpawnerHeadMob.java (chance, how many it summons, command),
     mixin/SkeletonMixin.java (puts on the spawner at spawn),
     mixin/LivingEntitySpawnerHeadMixin.java (counts hits — shared with the zombie and skeleton).

The mod's armor, weapon and potions are not given to it. A stone sword and wither from the hit — as in the regular game.
A spawner block on the head. The smoke above it is slightly higher than the zombie's: it is taller itself.
Smoke height: client/spawnerhead/SpawnerHeadParticles.java, WITHER_HEAD_CENTER_ABOVE_FEET.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn in a fortress or in a soul sand valley the chance is WITHER_CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE (0.5% on day 100).
2. Every 2 player hits (sword, bow, any damage from the player) exactly 2
   wither skeletons appear nearby. The counter resets after the summon.
3. The summoned ones do not get a spawner on the head. There is no "spawner calls spawner" chain.
4. A hit not from a player (lava, another mob) does not increase the counter.
5. The zombie and the regular skeleton still have 1–2 helpers, not always two.
   The hit count is shared: HITS_TO_SUMMON.


COMMAND
-------
  /etwitherspawner
  /evolvingthreats spawn wither_spawner
Appears 4 blocks away in the direction you look, with a spawner right away. The chance is not rolled.
Operator permissions required.


SETTINGS (SpawnerHeadMob.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false turns off both this and the carriers in the regular world
  WITHER_CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE .. 0.005 — chance in the Nether on day 100 (0.5%). At the start of the game 10 times lower. The command does not roll the chance
  HITS_TO_SUMMON ............... 2 — player hits before the summon (shared with the zombie and skeleton)
  WITHER_SUMMON_COUNT .......... 2 — how many wither skeletons to summon at once
  SUMMON_RADIUS ................ 3.0 — spawn radius (blocks)
  SPAWNER_HEAD_DROP_CHANCE ..... 0.085 — drop of the spawner from the head


TEST
----
1. /etwitherspawner. A spawner block on the head, smoke above it.
2. Hit it twice. Two wither skeletons without a spawner on the head appear nearby.
3. Hit a summoned one. It does not call new ones.
4. Two more hits on the carrier — two again.
