COMMON VARIANTS THAT ZOMBIES CAN ALSO HAVE
==========================================

The full description of these mechanics is in separate files in mobs/common/.
Here only a short summary, so that the list of zombie variants is in one place.

Bastion. 1000 health, hits do not knock back, speed potion does not work, experience 200.
Armor, enchantments and block breaking as for a regular zombie. From day 60, the chance does not grow.
File: bastion.txt

Spiky. One armor piece stings for 7–8 hearts through armor and Protection. The rest of the armor is normal.
From day 40, chance 0.2%. File: spiky.txt

Mirror. A point-blank hit is returned to the player. Chance 0.3% from day 1. File: mirror.txt

Raid anchor. A zombie with a golden hoe, 100 health and 12–15 minions.
While the anchor is alive, the minions hear the player. From day 80. File: raid-anchor.txt

Siege — a rare night against the whole base, not a separate zombie. File: siege.txt

Random health, weapon, armor, shield, totem, potions and kill experience are also files in mobs/common/.
What makes the chances grow: threat-scale.txt
