ZOMBIE: STEALS AN ITEM FROM YOUR HANDS
======================================

Type: regular zombie, cadaver and zombie villager (not a new mob).
Code: zombie/ZombieTheft.java (chance and drop),
     mixin/ZombieTheftMixin.java (moment of the hit).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. A hit that has already removed health has a 4% chance to take one item from the player's hands.
   Main hand first (sword). If it is empty — the off-hand (shield or totem).
2. The item ends up in the zombie's main hand. It hits with it: a netherite sword hurts more than a bare hand.
3. If the zombie already has something in its main hand, it takes nothing.
   A fishing rod, trident, hoe, TNT and the mod's random weapon all count as occupying the hand.
4. The stolen item always drops when the zombie dies. Durability is not damaged.
   While the zombie holds it, it does not despawn on its own and does not pick up another item from the ground.
5. Drowned and zombified piglins do not steal.
   A hit blocked by a shield, and the invulnerability flash after a hit, do not give a chance to steal.


SETTINGS (ZombieTheft.java)
----------------------------
ENABLED — false turns stealing off.
STEAL_CHANCE — 0.04 = 4%, roughly every twenty-fifth hit that lands. 0 = never.
DROP_CHANCE — 1 = the item always drops and its durability is intact. 0 = it vanishes together with the zombie.

ZOMBIES GO TOWARD LIGHT (Light Seeking)
=======================================

Type: behavior of regular zombies (not a separate mob).
Code: light/LightSeeking.java (settings + light search),
     entity/ai/LightSeekGoal.java (walk / stand / walk on),
     mixin/ZombieLightSeekGoalsMixin.java (hooks it into the zombie).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. A zombie WITHOUT a target searches for light about every 2 seconds in an area: ±60 blocks in X/Z, ±16 in height
   (in a straight line up to ~60 blocks, diagonally up to ~85). It picks the nearest suitable block.
2. It finds the nearest bright light source (brightness 12+) that it can see in a straight line
   (not through walls) — and walks to it.
3. When it arrives (closer than ~2.5 blocks) it BREAKS it at the same speed as ordinary blocks
   (cracks grow on the block; a torch breaks at once, glowstone in a couple of seconds) (see the section "ZOMBIES BREAK BLOCKS" below). If the light cannot be broken (bedrock/obsidian)
   or breaking is turned off — it stands for ~4 seconds and leaves.
4. If the light is still there, it counts as "visited" for 2 minutes, and the zombie looks for another one or wanders as usual.
5. As soon as the zombie notices a player — it attacks the player. While it remembers the player
   (PlayerGrudge, up to 32 blocks), it does not go to light even if the player hid underground.
6. VILLAGERS MATTER MORE THAN LIGHT: if there is a villager within 40 blocks (±16 in height, through walls),
   the zombie targets it and goes to kill/infect it. This is checked both before heading to a light
   and every half second on the way / at the light — the zombie abandons the torch halfway.


WHO TAKES PART
-------------
  Yes:  regular zombie, husk, zombie villager.
  No:   drowned (in water), zombified piglin, Rotten Overseer, raid minions.


WHAT COUNTS AS LIGHT
--------------------
Any block with brightness >= MIN_LIGHT_LEVEL (default 12):
  torch, glowstone, lantern, sea lantern, campfire, fire, etc.
LAVA (and a lava cauldron) and GLOW BERRIES (cave vines) are always excluded.

Important: the game does not remember who placed a block. Village torches attract zombies too.
If that is a problem — raise MIN_LIGHT_LEVEL to 15.


SETTINGS (LightSeeking.java)
-----------------------------
  ENABLED ................... true / false — turn the whole behavior on/off
  MIN_LIGHT_LEVEL ........... 12 — minimum brightness of the light source
  SEARCH_RADIUS_HORIZONTAL .. 60 — "vision" radius around the zombie (blocks)
  SEARCH_RADIUS_VERTICAL .... 16 — up/down (blocks)
  REQUIRE_LINE_OF_SIGHT ..... true — light behind a wall is not noticed
  SCAN_INTERVAL_TICKS ....... 40 — pause between searches (20 ticks = 1 sec)
  MOVE_SPEED ................ 1.0 — walking speed toward the light
  ARRIVE_DISTANCE ........... 2.5 — counts as "arrived" when closer than this (blocks)
  WAIT_AT_LIGHT_TICKS ....... 80 — how long to stand at the light
  IGNORE_VISITED_LIGHT_TICKS  2400 — how long before returning to the same light
  MAX_WALK_TICKS ............ 600 — maximum for one trip (30 sec)
  VILLAGER_BEATS_LIGHT ...... true — a villager nearby matters more than light (false — as before)
  VILLAGER_SENSE_RADIUS ..... 40 — distance at which the zombie senses a villager (blocks)
  VILLAGER_SENSE_VERTICAL ... 16 — up/down (in vanilla only ±4)
  VILLAGER_RECHECK_TICKS .... 10 — how often to check for a villager on the way to the light


TEST IN GAME
-----------
1. Create a cheats-enabled world, night: /time set night, /gamerule doMobSpawning true (default).
2. Move ~15 blocks away from the zombie so it cannot see you (behind a hill/house).
3. Place a torch or glowstone in the open, in the zombie's field of view.
4. A zombie without a target should slowly walk up to the torch, stand, and move on.
5. Quick test: /summon minecraft:zombie ~ ~ ~ near the torch at a distance of 10 blocks.

ZOMBIES BREAK BLOCKS (Block Breaking)
=====================================

Type: zombie behavior (not a separate mob).
Code: digging/BlockBreaking.java (settings and rules),
     entity/ai/BreakToTargetGoal.java (dig toward the player),
     entity/ai/LightSeekGoal.java (break light),
     mixin/ZombieBreakGoalsMixin.java (hook).


1) BREAKING BLOCKS TO REACH THE PLAYER
------------------------------------------
When it works: the zombie has a player target (it noticed the player), the player is within 32 blocks,
there is no ordinary path to them AND there is a real wall ahead (a block at head level).
  - Player 3+ blocks lower and no more than 8 blocks away horizontally — digs DOWN under its feet.
  - Otherwise — it breaks only the wall at head level; a single block at foot level ahead is not broken (it steps over it).
  - On a flat road below it and "just ahead" it does not dig, if the pathfinder made a mistake.
  - If a path to the player is found again (checked every 0.5 sec) — it walks, and does not keep digging for nothing.
  - While it breaks a wall, a normal attack does not interrupt it; it strikes only if the player is within hit range.
  - If the zombie gets distracted and returns to the same block within 5 sec, the cracks are not reset.
  - Zombies do NOT dig UP (they cannot climb a tunnel).
Cracks are visible on the block, the zombie waves its arm and makes a hit sound. Items from blocks do NOT drop.

PLAYER MEMORY (PlayerGrudge):
  A zombie that noticed or hit a player remembers them and keeps the target up to 32 blocks
  (even without direct line of sight — underground, behind a wall). While the memory is active, it is
  not distracted by torches. Code: grudge/PlayerGrudge.java, entity/ai/ZombiePlayerGrudgeGoal.java
  (priority 0 — higher than light and digging).
  The Overseer and raid zombies hunt on their own; they do not need the memory.


2) BREAKING SPEED
-------------------
Same as the player's (block hardness + the tool in the zombie's hand), but multiplied by 0.3.
  Dirt with a bare hand ............ ~2.5 sec
  Stone with a bare hand ........... ~25 sec (very slow without a pickaxe)
  Stone with an iron pickaxe ....... ~1.5 sec
  Dirt with an iron shovel ......... almost instant
Zombies get tools from the random weapons (RandomWeapons): pickaxe/shovel/axe/sword/hoe
of all materials. Material matters: iron is faster than wood.


3) BREAKING LIGHT
--------------
A zombie that came to a light (LightSeeking) and does not see a player:
  - breaks it at the same speed as ordinary blocks (hardness + the tool in hand, cracks grow on the block):
    a torch — at once, glowstone/lantern — in a couple of seconds with a bare hand,
  - breaks any light except lava and glow berries,
  - if it notices a player or villager earlier — it drops the light and goes for the target.
Light from bedrock/obsidian is not broken — they just stand for 4 sec and leave.


4) WHAT CANNOT BE BROKEN
--------------------
  Bedrock, obsidian, air/liquids, and blocks with "infinite" hardness (barrier, command block...).
  Everything else (including chests, furnaces, glass) can be broken. Crying obsidian is currently breakable.
  To forbid more blocks — UNBREAKABLE_BLOCKS in BlockBreaking.java.


SETTINGS (BlockBreaking.java)
------------------------------
  ENABLED ........................ true — turn all breaking on/off
  BREAKER_FRACTION ............... 1.0 — share of zombies that are breakers (1.0 = all, 0.3 = 30%)
  SPEED_MULTIPLIER ............... 0.3 — speed multiplier relative to the player
  DROP_ITEMS ..................... false — whether items drop
  UNBREAKABLE_BLOCKS ............. bedrock, obsidian
  DIG_MAX_DISTANCE_TO_TARGET ..... 32 — they do not dig farther than this from the player
  STUCK_CHECK_INTERVAL_TICKS ..... 10 — how often to check "stuck?" and "did a path appear?"
  KEEP_PROGRESS_TICKS ............ 100 — how many ticks (5 sec) the cracks wait if the zombie got distracted
  DIG_DOWN_MIN_BLOCKS_BELOW .... 3 — dig down only if the player is 3+ blocks lower
  DIG_DOWN_MAX_HORIZONTAL ...... 8 — and the player is no more than 8 blocks away horizontally
  REACH .......................... 3.5 — arm reach
  RESPECT_MOB_GRIEFING ........... true — honor /gamerule mobGriefing

(LightSeeking.java)
  BREAK_LIGHT .................... true — whether to break light
  (light breaking speed has no setting of its own — SPEED_MULTIPLIER from BlockBreaking is used)


WHO BREAKS
----------
  Yes:  all Overworld zombies (regular, husk, zombie villager), the Overseer, raid zombie minions.
  No:   drowned, zombified piglin, zombie with TNT; skeletons (not zombies).
  Only regular zombies break light (the Overseer/raid have no "go to light" behavior).
  If /gamerule mobGriefing is false — blocks are not broken.


TEST
----
1) Build a glass cage or a dirt wall next to a zombie and stand behind it
   so that the zombie sees you (through the glass). It should start breaking the barrier.
2) Give the zombie a pickaxe: /summon zombie ~ ~ ~ {equipment:{mainhand:{id:"minecraft:iron_pickaxe"}}}
3) For light: a torch far from you, the zombie will come and break it (torch at once, glowstone in a couple of seconds).
4) Raid minions: /etraid, then hide in a pit underground.

BEACON (Jack o'Lantern on the head)
===================================

Type: rare zombie or skeleton (not a separate mob).
Code: beacon/JackLanternBeacon.java,
     beacon/JackLanternLight.java (light around the head),
     entity/ai/JackBeaconAuraGoal.java (zombie),
     entity/ai/JackLanternFireShotGoal.java (skeleton),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java, SkeletonMixin.java,
     mixin/ZombieJackBeaconGoalsMixin.java, AbstractSkeletonJackLanternGoalMixin.java.


WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
------------
A glowing pumpkin (JACK_O_LANTERN) in the HEAD slot.
The picture on the head does not light up the world, so an invisible light of level LIGHT_LEVEL
(15 — like a lantern block) travels next to the head. If the pumpkin is removed or the mob dies — the light goes out.
An ordinary carved pumpkin (without light) is NOT used by this mechanic.


WHO CAN GET THE HEAD
-------------------------
  Yes:  zombie, husk, zombie villager, skeleton, stray.
  No:   drowned, zombified piglin, Overseer, raid.

  Only one thing per head: spawner OR beacon OR (for a skeleton) dispenser.


ZOMBIE — PACK BEACON
-----------------
1. It does not go to break torches (excluded from LightSeeking / LightSeekGoal).
2. If it sees a player (direct line of sight) — about once per second a "pulse" within BEACON_RADIUS:
   nearby zombies get Speed and (if enabled) a target on the same player, if they had no target.


SKELETON — FIRE SHOT
--------------------------
Once every 8–14 sec (random) one fire arrow at the target, if within range and there is line of sight.
In addition to the normal bow (does not replace the dispenser and is not a volley).


SETTINGS (JackLanternBeacon.java)
----------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  JACK_HEAD_DROP_CHANCE ........ 0.085 — chance the pumpkin drops
  LIGHT_LEVEL .................. 15 — brightness around the head, 0 = no light. This is in JackLanternLight.java
  CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE ......... 0.01 — chance on spawn at day 100 (1%). Early in the game it is 10 times lower
  BEACON_RADIUS ................ 20 — pack speed-boost radius
  BEACON_PULSE_INTERVAL_TICKS .. 20 — how often the pulse happens
  ALLY_SPEED_AMPLIFIER ......... 0 — Speed I
  SHARE_TARGET_WITH_ALLIES ..... true — pass aggro to allies without a target
  FIRE_SHOT_COOLDOWN_* ......... 8–14 sec between fire arrows


TEST
----
1. CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0 — check the head and the behavior.
2. Beacon zombie + pack: kill the light, attack — nearby zombies should run faster while the beacon sees you.
3. Beacon skeleton: wait for a fire arrow between the normal shots.

SPAWNER CARRIER (Spawner Head)
==============================

Type: rare variant of a regular zombie or skeleton (not a separate mob in the menu).
Code: spawnerhead/SpawnerHeadMob.java,
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java, SkeletonMixin.java,
     mixin/LivingEntitySpawnerHeadMixin.java (player hits).


WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
------------
A spawner block on the head (like the skeleton with a dispenser — an item in the HEAD slot).
It overrides a helmet. A skeleton's spawner and dispenser on the head do not drop together:
if the spawner was rolled — the dispenser is not put on.

Smoke and fire above the head (like a real spawner) — client side only,
code: client/spawnerhead/SpawnerHeadParticles.java (ENABLED, height HEAD_CENTER_ABOVE_FEET).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On a normal spawn the chance to become a carrier is CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE (0.5% on day 100).
2. Every 2 player hits (sword, bow, any damage from the player) 1–2 mobs of the same kind appear nearby
   (a zombie calls a zombie, a skeleton — a skeleton, a husk — a husk…). The hit counter is reset after a summon.
3. The summoned help can NEVER get a spawner on its head (no overpowered chain).
4. The help uses a normal finalizeSpawn (armor/weapon by the mod's rules), but without the carrier role.


WHO CAN BE A CARRIER
------------------------
  Yes:  zombie, husk, zombie villager, skeleton, stray.
  No:   drowned, zombified piglin, boss Overseer, raid minions.
  The wither skeleton is separate: mobs/skeletons/skeleton.txt, section "WITHER SKELETON WITH SPAWNER"
  (always two per summon, its own chance).


SETTINGS (SpawnerHeadMob.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — on/off
  CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE ......... 0.005 — chance on spawn at day 100 (0.5%). Early in the game it is 10 times lower
  HITS_TO_SUMMON ............... 2 — player hits before a summon
  SUMMON_COUNT_MIN / MAX ....... 1 / 2 — how many mobs are summoned
  SUMMON_RADIUS ................ 3.0 — spawn radius (blocks)
  SPAWNER_HEAD_DROP_CHANCE ..... 0.085 — chance the spawner drops from the head


TEST
----
1. At night, wait for zombies/skeletons or /summon — look at the head (very rare, 0.5% by day 100).
2. Hit the carrier 2 times — 1–2 of the same kind should appear nearby.
3. To see it more often: temporarily set CARRIER_SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0.

ZOMBIE WITH TNT
===============

Type: rare regular zombie. Not a new mob in the menu.
Code: zombie/TntZombie.java (chance, throw, command),
     entity/ai/TntDropGoal.java (when to drop),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java (instead of a sword),
     mixin/TntZombieMixin.java (flag and pause, saving),
     mixin/TntZombieGoalMixin.java (target).

The weapon is TNT in hand. This zombie is not given a sword, shield or totem.
Armor, potions and the mod's special head item stay.
It does not break blocks: neither a wall toward the player nor a torch. If there is no path, it drops TNT.


WHEN IT DROPS
------------
An ordinary lit TNT appears under its feet. The fuse is 4 seconds, the explosion is like vanilla TNT.
It breaks blocks only if the game rule allows it (tntExplodes).
The zombie itself is not protected from the explosion.

1. Health below 3 (a heart and a half). Even if the player is nearby and the path is clear.
2. No path to the player is found, the player is no farther than 32 blocks, it cannot hit them,
   and there is a block right in front of the zombie. On an empty road, or if the player is just far away — it does not drop.
The next TNT comes no sooner than 4 seconds later.


COMMAND
-------
  /ettnt
  /evolvingthreats spawn tnt_zombie
The zombie appears 6 blocks away in the direction you look. The chance is not rolled.


SETTINGS (TntZombie.java)
--------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.04 — chance for a regular zombie (4%)
  LOW_HEALTH ................... 3 — below this health it drops TNT
  PATH_CHECK_BLOCKS ............ 32 — farther than this "no path" is not counted
  PATH_CHECK_INTERVAL_TICKS .... 10 — how often to check the path
  THROW_COOLDOWN_SECONDS ....... 4 — pause between TNTs
  FUSE_TICKS ................... 80 — fuse, 80 = 4 seconds
  TNT_DROP_CHANCE .............. 0.085 — chance that the TNT in hand drops on death


TEST
----
1. /ettnt. TNT in hand, no sword.
2. Put it behind a dirt wall and stand on the other side. After a couple of seconds TNT will start hissing under it.
3. Bring it almost to death (below a heart and a half). It drops TNT even if the path is clear.
4. It does not dig through the wall.

ZOMBIE SORCERER
===============

Type: rare variant of a regular zombie. Black leather on all four slots, a netherite hoe in hand.
Code: zombie/ZombieSorcerer.java (settings, spells, loot, command),
     mixin/ZombieSorcererMixin.java (flag, pause, experience),
     mixin/ZombieSorcererGoalMixin.java (when it casts),
     mixin/ZombieSorcererLootMixin.java (extra item),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java (chance on spawn, without enchantments and random armor).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn the chance is SPAWN_CHANCE (0.1%, one in a thousand). FIXED: from day 40, does not grow with the threat scale.
   Regular adult zombie only.
   husk, drowned, zombie villager, baby, Overseer and raid zombies do not become sorcerers.
2. The leather is dyed black, as if with dye. The hoe has no enchantments.
   Random enchantments, shield, totem, pumpkin and spawner are not given to its head.
   Potions at spawn are possible — as with a regular zombie.
   It does not break blocks and does not go to light.
3. Health is always 80 (40 hearts), not a random 20–40.
4. If it sees a player closer than 12 blocks, it casts one spell every 8 seconds.
   While the player is not visible or is farther — the pause does not tick, the first spell comes immediately on meeting.
   A wall blocks the spell. A shield does not catch it.
   Launch is the main spell, about 70%. Fire and pull — 15% each.
   - The launch is most often 25–30 blocks, equally split. Every tenth launch is 50.
     A fall from that height deals almost the whole life bar and more damage.
   - Ignite for 6 seconds. Fire resistance removes the damage, as usual.
   - Pull toward itself. If the player is already right next to it (closer than 2.5 blocks), there is no pull:
     then launch is more frequent, fire less.
5. The hoe and the worn armor do not drop.
6. If a player killed it — 15 experience (a regular zombie gives 5) and one random item:
   sugar, golden carrot, firework star, redstone torch
   or one piece of black leather. If the zombie burned on its own — no extra item.


COMMAND
-------
  /etsorcerer
  /evolvingthreats spawn sorcerer
The zombie appears 6 blocks away in the direction you look and immediately looks at you.
Operator permissions are required. For the launch, get out of flight.


SETTINGS (ZombieSorcerer.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false turns off spawning and spells
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.001 — chance in the world (0.1%, one in a thousand). FIXED from day 40. The command does not roll the chance
  HEALTH ....................... 80 — life, 40 hearts
  LAUNCH_WEIGHT ................ 70 — weight of the launch. Together with fire and pull the sum is 100 = percentages
  FIRE_WEIGHT .................. 15 — weight of the ignite
  PULL_WEIGHT .................. 15 — weight of the pull. 0 = no pull
  LAUNCH_MIN_BLOCKS ............ 25 — normal launch, lower bound
  LAUNCH_MAX_BLOCKS ............ 30 — normal launch, upper bound
  LAUNCH_RARE_BLOCKS ........... 50 — rare launch, exactly this many blocks
  LAUNCH_RARE_CHANCE ........... 0.10 — 10% of launches go 50 blocks. 0 = always 25–30
  XP_REWARD .................... 15 — experience, a regular zombie gives 5
  SPELL_RANGE_BLOCKS ........... 12 — it does not cast farther than this
  SPELL_COOLDOWN_SECONDS ....... 8 — pause between spells
  FIRE_SECONDS ................. 6 — how many seconds the player burns
  PULL_SPEED ................... 1.6 — strength of the pull
  PULL_MIN_BLOCKS .............. 2.5 — closer than this there is no pull
  BONUS_DROP_CHANCE ............ 1.0 — chance of the extra item if a player killed it


TEST
----
1. Enter /etsorcerer. It wears black leather and a netherite hoe, 40 hearts.
2. Stand a few blocks away with no wall and no flying. After a moment, most often a launch
   of 25–30 blocks. Less often fire or pull. About every tenth launch is 50.
   The sound is like the evoker's.
3. Wait 8 seconds — the next spell.
4. Kill it. No hoe. There is experience and one item from the list.

EXCALIBUR ZOMBIE
================

Type: rare variant of a regular zombie. The name Excalibur is shown above its head.
It is visible only when your crosshair is on it and it is no farther than about 64 blocks.
Code: zombie/ZombieExcalibur.java (settings, sword, loot, command),
     mixin/ZombieExcaliburMixin.java (flag and experience),
     mixin/ZombieExcaliburLootMixin.java (name tag),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java (chance on spawn; own sword, armor and shield as for a regular zombie).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn the chance is SPAWN_CHANCE (1%). FIXED: from day 40, does not grow with the threat scale.
   Before day 40 there are none. An Excalibur that is already alive stays.
   Regular adult zombie only.
   husk, drowned, zombie villager, baby, Overseer and raid zombies do not become one.
2. In hand there is always one sword, with equal chances: wood, stone, copper, iron or diamond.
   It has Sharpness 20. At the enchanting table the cap is V.
   No gold and no netherite.
3. Armor as on a regular zombie: each piece separately, sometimes a full set, enchantments on the armor.
   A shield in the second hand is possible, with the same chance as for a regular zombie.
   The sword in the first hand always stays.
   Random weapon, totem, pumpkin and spawner are not given to its head.
   Potions at spawn are possible — as with a regular zombie.
   It does not pick up other items from the ground, otherwise the sword would change.
4. A sword with this sharpness does not drop. Armor and shield drop with their usual chance.
   One name tag named Excalibur always drops.
5. Health is always 50. Excaliburs that are already alive keep their old health.
6. If a player killed it — 30 experience. The global experience multiplier does not increase this number.
   If the zombie burned on its own — no experience, the name tag still drops.


THE HIT
----
It is an ordinary sword hit, only with a high Sharpness. There is no separate crushing move.
The game adds 1 damage for the first level of Sharpness and another 0.5 for each next one.
20 gives 10.5 damage on top of the sword itself (Sharpness V gives only 3).

A naked player, normal difficulty, 10 hearts:
  wooden sword ..... about 8 hearts
  stone and copper .. about 9
  iron .............. about 9 and a quarter
  diamond ........... almost 10, a quarter of a heart is left

On hard difficulty any such sword kills a naked player in one hit.
In armor the hit is noticeably weaker: a full diamond set is not taken down in one hit.


COMMAND
-------
  /etexcalibur
  /evolvingthreats spawn excalibur
The zombie appears 6 blocks away in the direction you look. Each call rerolls the sword material.
The day is not checked: the command works before day 40 as well.
Operator permissions are required.


SETTINGS (ZombieExcalibur.java)
--------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.01 = 1%. FIXED from day 40. 0 = command only
  Activation day ............... ThreatTable.EXCALIBUR_DAY = 40
  SHARPNESS_LEVEL .............. 20. 5 = normal Sharpness V
  MAX_HEALTH ................... 50 = 25 hearts
  XP_REWARD .................... 30. 0 = no experience. Does not touch the experience multiplier
  NAME_TAG_COUNT ............... 1. 0 = do not drop a name tag
  DISPLAY_NAME ................. Excalibur

MJÖLNIR ZOMBIE
==============

Type: rare variant of a regular zombie. The name Mjölnir is shown above its head.
It is visible only when your crosshair is on it and it is no farther than about 64 blocks.
Code: zombie/ZombieMjolnir.java (settings, hammer, loot, command),
     mixin/ZombieMjolnirMixin.java (flag and experience),
     mixin/ZombieMjolnirLootMixin.java (name tag and rare hammer),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java (chance on spawn, no random weapon; armor as on a regular zombie).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn the chance is SPAWN_CHANCE (0.2%, one in five hundred). FIXED: from day 1, does not grow with the threat scale.
   Regular adult zombie only.
   husk, drowned, zombie villager, baby, Overseer, raid zombies and Excalibur do not become one.
2. In hand there is a mace — that is the hammer in the game. It has Knockback 20. At the enchanting table the cap is II.
3. Random weapon, shield, totem, pumpkin and spawner are not given to its head.
   Armor — as on a regular zombie: pieces separately, a full set rarely.
   The armor can have enchantments. A full-set helmet does not take away a pumpkin or spawner,
   because a rare head item is not given to it.
   Potions at spawn are possible — as with a regular zombie.
   It does not pick up other items from the ground, otherwise the hammer would change.
4. The hammer itself does not drop. With a separate roll HAMMER_DROP_CHANCE (0.5%, one in two hundred)
   a copy of the mace with Knockback 20 drops. Looting does not make this roll more frequent.
   One name tag named Mjölnir always drops.
5. If a player killed it — 20 experience (a regular zombie gives 5). If the zombie burned on its own — no experience,
   the name tag and the rare hammer can still drop.


THE HIT
----
It is an ordinary mace hit plus a very strong knockback.
Each level of Knockback adds 1 to the strength. Knockback II is 2. Knockback 20 is 20,
about ten times stronger: the player can be carried far away.


COMMAND
-------
/etmjolnir
or /evolvingthreats spawn mjolnir

ROTTEN OVERSEER
===============

Type: rare mini-boss (a regular zombie with a special kit and AI).
Code: ZombieOverseer.java — settings are in the "SETTINGS" block.


HOW TO ENCOUNTER IT
-------------
- Chance on natural zombie spawn (SPAWN_CHANCE, default 0.2%).
- Not a drowned and not a zombified piglin.
- On spawn it can immediately "lock on" to the nearest player within ~128 blocks.


STATS (default)
------------------------------
  Health ................. 100 HP
  Armor .................. full diamond set, Protection II
  Weapon ................. diamond axe, Sharpness III (main hand)
                          + axe, Sharpness II (off-hand, it hits too)
  Chase radius ........... ~112 blocks (FOLLOW_RANGE)


ABILITIES
-----------
1. Hunting the player — it remembers the locked-on target while you are within ~128 blocks
   and in the same dimension.

2. Hunt timeout — if you stay too far away for ~2 minutes (outside the chase zone),
   the hunt is reset.

3. Pack summon — once every ~25 s, if the target is closer than ~96 blocks: 2 regular zombies,
   no more than 5 "servants" at a time (zombies near the same target).
   It places them only on free floor nearby, not inside a wall. If there is no room —
   it waits 5 seconds and tries again. Summoned zombies do not become Overseers themselves.


DIFFERENCES FROM A REGULAR ZOMBIE
-------------------------
- Does not get random loot from RandomWeapons / RandomArmor on spawn.
- Name above the head: Rotten Overseer. Visible only when your crosshair is on it and it is no farther than about 64 blocks.


TEST IN GAME
-----------
Commands (cheats / OP required, permission level 2):
  /etoverseer
  /evolvingthreats spawn overseer
  /etoverseer_egg
  /evolvingthreats give overseer_egg
  /evolvingthreats give overseer_egg 16

Spawn egg:
  Creative → the "Spawn Eggs" tab (next to the zombie egg).
  Or /etoverseer_egg, /give @s evolving-threats:rotten_overseer_spawn_egg
  Right-click on a block or in the air — spawns an Overseer.
  Texture: assets/.../textures/item/rotten_overseer_spawn_egg.png

Random spawn: ZombieOverseer.SPAWN_CHANCE (default 0.2%).

RITUAL BAND
===========

Type: rare group, not a separate mob. 1–2 regular zombies and 1–2 regular skeletons.
Code: ritual/RitualBand.java (settings, behavior, loot, command),
     mixin/RitualZombieMixin.java and RitualSkeletonMixin.java (memory and saving to the world),
     mixin/RitualMobMixin.java (experience and extra items),
     mixin/RitualHuntMixin.java (player head senses through walls, piglin sun is extinguished),
     mixin/RitualHurtMixin.java (members do not hit their own, piglin fire, wither),
     mixin/RitualDeathMixin.java (creeper head pop).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. The chance is SPAWN_CHANCE (0.2%, one in five hundred) for a regular adult zombie and a regular skeleton.
   FIXED: from day 1, does not grow with the threat scale.
   husk, stray, baby, Overseer and raid zombies do not become a band.
   A summoned band-mate does not roll the chance again.
2. Everyone in the band has the same head. Which one it is — the behavior decides.
   Dark green leather skin, no helmet. In hand a red torch or a bone, and both are present in the band.
   The mod's enchantments, random weapons and potions are not given. They do not break blocks.
3. Each one has 50 health. A hit from anyone in the band removes 4 hearts before armor.
   A regular zombie does a heart and a half, a skeleton with a bone one.
   Without armor the player survives two hits.
4. Anyone in the band notices a player from 52 blocks if it sees them.
   The player head senses through walls at the same 52 blocks.
   The head does not drop. If a player killed it — a compass drops with a 2.5% chance, like the other heads.
   Looting I/II/III raises it to 3.5% / 4.5% / 5.5%.
5. Creeper head. On death a small explosion: it hurts nearby, and almost does not break blocks.
   It does not hurt or scatter its own. The head drops rarely, like the skull from a wither skeleton.
   Gunpowder also drops, including from zombies.
6. Piglin head. Lava, fire and sun do not hurt it: a zombie in the open does not catch fire.
   The head drops rarely, like the skull from a wither skeleton. One gold ingot also drops.
7. Wither skull. A hit applies wither for 4 seconds.
   While it is still active, the next hit does not refresh it.
   The skull drops rarely, like from a wither skeleton. Coal and a wither rose also drop.
8. A piece of dark green leather sometimes drops, like ordinary armor (about 8.5%).
   The red torch from the hand — with a 35% chance. The bone from the hand does not drop.
   Experience is 50 from each. A regular zombie and skeleton in this mod give 10, and the global multiplier does not double the band.


COMMAND
-------
  /etritual
  /etritual player
  /etritual creeper
  /etritual piglin
  /etritual wither
  /evolvingthreats spawn ritual
  /evolvingthreats spawn ritual wither
Without a word at the end the head is random. The band appears 6 blocks away in the direction you look.
Operator permissions are required.


SETTINGS (RitualBand.java)
---------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false turns the band off
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.002 — 0.2%, one in five hundred. FIXED from day 1. 0 = command only
  XP_REWARD .................... 50 — experience from each. Does not touch the experience multiplier. 0 = no experience
  LEATHER_COLOR ................ 0x1B4F2A — dark green leather
  LEATHER_DROP_CHANCE .......... 0.085 — chance of a leather piece
  TORCH_DROP_CHANCE ............ 0.35 — chance of the torch from the hand
  HEAD_DROP_CHANCE ............. 0.025 — 2.5% for the head and for the compass.
                                 Looting I/II/III = 3.5% / 4.5% / 5.5%
  MAX_HEALTH ................... 50 — 25 hearts for each in the band
  ATTACK_DAMAGE ................ 8 — four hearts before armor. 2 damage = 1 heart
  GUNPOWDER_COUNT .............. 1 — gunpowder
  GOLD_INGOT_COUNT ............. 1 — gold ingot
  COAL_COUNT ................... 1 — coal
  ROSE_COUNT ................... 1 — wither rose
  SENSE_BLOCKS ................. 52 — distance from which they notice. The player head senses through walls at this same distance
  EXPLOSION_POWER .............. 1.5 — the pop, a creeper's is 3
  WITHER_SECONDS ............... 4 — duration of wither
  WITHER_REFRESH_IF_BELOW_SECONDS  1 — while the effect is longer than this, a hit does not refresh it


TEST
----
1. /etritual player — everyone has the player head. Go behind a wall closer than 52 blocks: they come toward you.
   Kill. A compass — rarely, about 2.5%. No player heads.
2. /etritual creeper — death pops. A neighbor from the band stays in place. Gunpowder drops.
   Head — rarely, about 2.5%.
3. /etritual piglin — lava does not burn. In daytime in the sun the zombie does not catch fire. An ingot drops.
   Head — rarely, about 2.5%.
4. /etritual wither — a hit applies wither and does not refresh it on every poke.
   Coal and a rose drop. Skull — rarely, about 2.5%.
   A hit from any band removes 4 hearts before armor. Each has 50 health.

DROWNED: HELMET, WATER, THUNDERSTORM
====================================

Type: regular drowned (not a new mob).
Code: drowned/DrownedThreats.java (chances, healing, enchantments),
     mixin/DrownedThreatMixin.java (spawn),
     mixin/DrownedRegenMixin.java (healing),
     mixin/DrownedTridentMixin.java (lightning when the trident hits).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. About every tenth one spawns in a turtle shell helmet with Protection II.
   That is damage resistance. If there is already an item on the head, the helmet is not put on.
2. In water any drowned heals 2 health (one heart) every 3 seconds,
   until full. On land there is no healing.
3. The trident is given by the game itself, to roughly every sixteenth one. The mod does not create a new one and does not take the fishing rod away.
   The mod picks the enchantments on this trident and replaces those the game had put on.
   In 30% of cases it is Channeling. In the other 70% — one other enchantment, equally likely:
   Impaling, Loyalty, Riptide or Unbreaking. The level is from I up to the maximum of that enchantment.
   Whoever got a fishing rod just holds it in hand. It does not cast the hook. See the section "DROWNED WITH A FISHING ROD" below.
4. A trident with Channeling hitting a player strikes lightning even without rain and under a roof.
   A trident with another enchantment does not strike lightning.
   In a thunderstorm under open sky the game itself calls lightning, we do not strike a second time.
   Lightning does not strike other mobs. A point-blank trident stab does not call lightning.
   A shield that blocked the throw does not either.
5. The helmet drops with a chance of about 8.5%. The trident drops as in the normal game.


SETTINGS (DrownedThreats.java)
-------------------------------
ENABLED — false returns regular drowned.
HELMET_CHANCE — 0.10 = 10% with a helmet. 0 = none.
HELMET_PROTECTION — Protection level. 2 = Protection II. 0 = helmet without enchantments. 4 = maximum.
CHANNELING_CHANCE — 0.30 = Channeling on 30% of the tridents the game has already given out.
  The rest get Impaling, Loyalty, Riptide or Unbreaking. 0 = only these enchantments. 1 = always Channeling.
DROP_CHANCE — 0.085 = 8.5% helmet drop. 0 = does not drop.
REGEN_HP — how much health at a time. 2 = one heart. 0 = no healing.
REGEN_SECONDS — healing pause. 3 = three seconds.

DROWNED WITH A FISHING ROD
==========================

Type: regular drowned that the game has already given a fishing rod (not a new mob).
Whoever has a trident has no rod. Whoever has a rod — it is only decoration in the hand.
Casting code (currently off): drowned/DrownedFishing.java.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. The game itself gives the rod. The mod does not take it away and does not create a new one.
   Whoever has a trident has no rod.
2. The rod just sits in the hand. The hook is not cast, the player is not pulled.
3. It hits with its hands, like a regular drowned.


SETTINGS (DrownedFishing.java)
-------------------------------
ENABLED — false, the rod is only an item in the hand. true turns casting and pulling back on.
The numbers below work only if casting is on.
MELEE_BLOCKS — 3, closer than this it hits instead of pulling.
CAST_BLOCKS — 16, farther than this it only walks.
PULL_PER_TICK — 0.18, strength of the pull per tick.
MISS_COOLDOWN_SECONDS — 1, pause after a miss.
FLIGHT_TICKS — 30, how many ticks the hook flies if it hits no one. 30 = a second and a half.
CAST_SPEED — 1.6, speed of the hook. Lower — it hits the ground more often.

COMMON VARIANTS THAT ZOMBIES CAN ALSO HAVE
==========================================

The full description of these mechanics is in separate files in mobs/common/.
Here only a short summary, so that the list of zombie variants is in one place.

Bastion. 1000 health, hits do not knock back, speed potion does not work, experience 200.
Armor, enchantments and block breaking as for a regular zombie. From day 60, the chance does not grow.
File: bastion.txt

Spiky. One armor piece stings for 7–8 hearts through armor and Protection. The rest of the armor is normal.
From day 40, chance 0.2%. File: spiky.txt

Mirror. A point-blank hit is returned to the player. Chance 0.3% from day 1. File: mirror.txt

Raid anchor. A zombie with a golden hoe, 100 health and 12–15 minions.
While the anchor is alive, the minions hear the player. From day 80. File: raid-anchor.txt

Siege — a rare night against the whole base, not a separate zombie. File: siege.txt

Random health, weapon, armor, shield, totem, potions and kill experience are also files in mobs/common/.
What makes the chances grow: threat-scale.txt
