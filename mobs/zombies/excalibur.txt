EXCALIBUR ZOMBIE
================

Type: rare variant of a regular zombie. The name Excalibur is shown above its head.
It is visible only when your crosshair is on it and it is no farther than about 64 blocks.
Code: zombie/ZombieExcalibur.java (settings, sword, loot, command),
     mixin/ZombieExcaliburMixin.java (flag and experience),
     mixin/ZombieExcaliburLootMixin.java (name tag),
     mixin/ZombieMixin.java (chance on spawn; own sword, armor and shield as for a regular zombie).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn the chance is SPAWN_CHANCE (1%). FIXED: from day 40, does not grow with the threat scale.
   Before day 40 there are none. An Excalibur that is already alive stays.
   Regular adult zombie only.
   husk, drowned, zombie villager, baby, Overseer and raid zombies do not become one.
2. In hand there is always one sword, with equal chances: wood, stone, copper, iron or diamond.
   It has Sharpness 20. At the enchanting table the cap is V.
   No gold and no netherite.
3. Armor as on a regular zombie: each piece separately, sometimes a full set, enchantments on the armor.
   A shield in the second hand is possible, with the same chance as for a regular zombie.
   The sword in the first hand always stays.
   Random weapon, totem, pumpkin and spawner are not given to its head.
   Potions at spawn are possible — as with a regular zombie.
   It does not pick up other items from the ground, otherwise the sword would change.
4. A sword with this sharpness does not drop. Armor and shield drop with their usual chance.
   One name tag named Excalibur always drops.
5. Health is always 50. Excaliburs that are already alive keep their old health.
6. If a player killed it — 30 experience. The global experience multiplier does not increase this number.
   If the zombie burned on its own — no experience, the name tag still drops.


THE HIT
----
It is an ordinary sword hit, only with a high Sharpness. There is no separate crushing move.
The game adds 1 damage for the first level of Sharpness and another 0.5 for each next one.
20 gives 10.5 damage on top of the sword itself (Sharpness V gives only 3).

A naked player, normal difficulty, 10 hearts:
  wooden sword ..... about 8 hearts
  stone and copper .. about 9
  iron .............. about 9 and a quarter
  diamond ........... almost 10, a quarter of a heart is left

On hard difficulty any such sword kills a naked player in one hit.
In armor the hit is noticeably weaker: a full diamond set is not taken down in one hit.


COMMAND
-------
  /etexcalibur
  /evolvingthreats spawn excalibur
The zombie appears 6 blocks away in the direction you look. Each call rerolls the sword material.
The day is not checked: the command works before day 40 as well.
Operator permissions are required.


SETTINGS (ZombieExcalibur.java)
--------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.01 = 1%. FIXED from day 40. 0 = command only
  Activation day ............... ThreatTable.EXCALIBUR_DAY = 40
  SHARPNESS_LEVEL .............. 20. 5 = normal Sharpness V
  MAX_HEALTH ................... 50 = 25 hearts
  XP_REWARD .................... 30. 0 = no experience. Does not touch the experience multiplier
  NAME_TAG_COUNT ............... 1. 0 = do not drop a name tag
  DISPLAY_NAME ................. Excalibur
