ZOMBIE: STEALS AN ITEM FROM YOUR HANDS
======================================

Type: regular zombie, cadaver and zombie villager (not a new mob).
Code: zombie/ZombieTheft.java (chance and drop),
     mixin/ZombieTheftMixin.java (moment of the hit).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. A hit that has already removed health has a 4% chance to take one item from the player's hands.
   Main hand first (sword). If it is empty — the off-hand (shield or totem).
2. The item ends up in the zombie's main hand. It hits with it: a netherite sword hurts more than a bare hand.
3. If the zombie already has something in its main hand, it takes nothing.
   A fishing rod, trident, hoe, TNT and the mod's random weapon all count as occupying the hand.
4. The stolen item always drops when the zombie dies. Durability is not damaged.
   While the zombie holds it, it does not despawn on its own and does not pick up another item from the ground.
5. Drowned and zombified piglins do not steal.
   A hit blocked by a shield, and the invulnerability flash after a hit, do not give a chance to steal.


SETTINGS (ZombieTheft.java)
----------------------------
ENABLED — false turns stealing off.
STEAL_CHANCE — 0.04 = 4%, roughly every twenty-fifth hit that lands. 0 = never.
DROP_CHANCE — 1 = the item always drops and its durability is intact. 0 = it vanishes together with the zombie.
