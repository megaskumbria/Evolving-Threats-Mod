DROWNED: HELMET, WATER, THUNDERSTORM
====================================

Type: regular drowned (not a new mob).
Code: drowned/DrownedThreats.java (chances, healing, enchantments),
     mixin/DrownedThreatMixin.java (spawn),
     mixin/DrownedRegenMixin.java (healing),
     mixin/DrownedTridentMixin.java (lightning when the trident hits).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. About every tenth one spawns in a turtle shell helmet with Protection II.
   That is damage resistance. If there is already an item on the head, the helmet is not put on.
2. In water any drowned heals 2 health (one heart) every 3 seconds,
   until full. On land there is no healing.
3. The trident is given by the game itself, to roughly every sixteenth one. The mod does not create a new one and does not take the fishing rod away.
   The mod picks the enchantments on this trident and replaces those the game had put on.
   In 30% of cases it is Channeling. In the other 70% — one other enchantment, equally likely:
   Impaling, Loyalty, Riptide or Unbreaking. The level is from I up to the maximum of that enchantment.
   Whoever got a fishing rod just holds it in hand. It does not cast the hook. See drowned-fishing-rod.txt.
4. A trident with Channeling hitting a player strikes lightning even without rain and under a roof.
   A trident with another enchantment does not strike lightning.
   In a thunderstorm under open sky the game itself calls lightning, we do not strike a second time.
   Lightning does not strike other mobs. A point-blank trident stab does not call lightning.
   A shield that blocked the throw does not either.
5. The helmet drops with a chance of about 8.5%. The trident drops as in the normal game.


SETTINGS (DrownedThreats.java)
-------------------------------
ENABLED — false returns regular drowned.
HELMET_CHANCE — 0.10 = 10% with a helmet. 0 = none.
HELMET_PROTECTION — Protection level. 2 = Protection II. 0 = helmet without enchantments. 4 = maximum.
CHANNELING_CHANCE — 0.30 = Channeling on 30% of the tridents the game has already given out.
  The rest get Impaling, Loyalty, Riptide or Unbreaking. 0 = only these enchantments. 1 = always Channeling.
DROP_CHANCE — 0.085 = 8.5% helmet drop. 0 = does not drop.
REGEN_HP — how much health at a time. 2 = one heart. 0 = no healing.
REGEN_SECONDS — healing pause. 3 = three seconds.
