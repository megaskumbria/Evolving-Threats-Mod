ROTTEN OVERSEER
===============

Type: rare mini-boss (a regular zombie with a special kit and AI).
Code: ZombieOverseer.java — settings are in the "SETTINGS" block.


HOW TO ENCOUNTER IT
-------------
- Chance on natural zombie spawn (SPAWN_CHANCE, default 0.2%).
- Not a drowned and not a zombified piglin.
- On spawn it can immediately "lock on" to the nearest player within ~128 blocks.


STATS (default)
------------------------------
  Health ................. 100 HP
  Armor .................. full diamond set, Protection II
  Weapon ................. diamond axe, Sharpness III (main hand)
                          + axe, Sharpness II (off-hand, it hits too)
  Chase radius ........... ~112 blocks (FOLLOW_RANGE)


ABILITIES
-----------
1. Hunting the player — it remembers the locked-on target while you are within ~128 blocks
   and in the same dimension.

2. Hunt timeout — if you stay too far away for ~2 minutes (outside the chase zone),
   the hunt is reset.

3. Pack summon — once every ~25 s, if the target is closer than ~96 blocks: 2 regular zombies,
   no more than 5 "servants" at a time (zombies near the same target).
   It places them only on free floor nearby, not inside a wall. If there is no room —
   it waits 5 seconds and tries again. Summoned zombies do not become Overseers themselves.


DIFFERENCES FROM A REGULAR ZOMBIE
-------------------------
- Does not get random loot from RandomWeapons / RandomArmor on spawn.
- Name above the head: Rotten Overseer. Visible only when your crosshair is on it and it is no farther than about 64 blocks.


TEST IN GAME
-----------
Commands (cheats / OP required, permission level 2):
  /etoverseer
  /evolvingthreats spawn overseer
  /etoverseer_egg
  /evolvingthreats give overseer_egg
  /evolvingthreats give overseer_egg 16

Spawn egg:
  Creative → the "Spawn Eggs" tab (next to the zombie egg).
  Or /etoverseer_egg, /give @s evolving-threats:rotten_overseer_spawn_egg
  Right-click on a block or in the air — spawns an Overseer.
  Texture: assets/.../textures/item/rotten_overseer_spawn_egg.png

Random spawn: ZombieOverseer.SPAWN_CHANCE (default 0.2%).
