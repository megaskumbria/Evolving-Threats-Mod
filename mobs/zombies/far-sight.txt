FAR-SIGHT ZOMBIE
================

Type: zombie behavior (not a separate mob).
Code: zombie/FarSightZombie.java (the roll and the distance),
     grudge/PlayerGrudge.java (keeps walking after the lucky look),
     mixin/MobGrudgeAiMixin.java (asks each zombie to roll).

Scale: FIXED. 5% on each roll, from day 0. The moon does not change it.


WHAT HAPPENS
------------
Any ordinary zombie can notice the player from 112 blocks
(a bit past 100; a normal zombie still notices from 35).
It is not decided at spawn.
Every 30 seconds he rolls a number from 1 to 100.
If it is 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5, he notices — that is 5%.
The roll only matters when you are farther than his normal sight and the view is clear.
A wall, a hill, or the ground blocks that first look. Closer than 35 blocks he does not roll:
he already notices the usual way.
A miss just waits another 30 seconds.

After the lucky look he keeps walking even behind walls and underground,
until the player dies or gets farther than 112 blocks.
He does not forget after 5 minutes the way an ordinary zombie does.
When the hunt ends, his sight goes back to 35.

Each zombie rolls on his own timer, so a crowd does not all look on the same tick.

Not these: drowned, zombified piglin, Rotten Overseer, White Eyes,
raid zombies, ritual band. They already hunt their own way.


SETTINGS (FarSightZombie.java)
------------------------------
  ENABLED ................ false — nobody notices from the long distance
  ROLL_EVERY_TICKS ....... 600 — how often he rolls. 20 ticks = 1 second, so 600 = 30 seconds
  ROLL_OUT_OF ............ 100 — the roll is 1 through this
  NOTICE_ON_OR_BELOW ..... 5 — notice on 1–5, which is 5%. 100 = always, on the half-minute
  SEE_BLOCKS ............. 112 — the lucky look, and how far he still chases. 35 = normal zombie
  NORMAL_SIGHT ........... 35 — sight restored when the hunt ends


TEST
----
1. Stand in an open field. Summon a few zombies about 80 blocks away, where they can see you.
   Wait 30 seconds. About 1 in 20 rolls should start one walking. The rest ignore you.
   To force it, set NOTICE_ON_OR_BELOW to 100 and restart.
2. Duck behind a hill after one has started walking. He should keep coming.
3. Get more than 112 blocks away. He should give up.
