ZOMBIES GO TOWARD LIGHT (Light Seeking)
=======================================

Type: behavior of regular zombies (not a separate mob).
Code: light/LightSeeking.java (settings + light search),
     entity/ai/LightSeekGoal.java (walk / stand / walk on),
     mixin/ZombieLightSeekGoalsMixin.java (hooks it into the zombie).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. A zombie WITHOUT a target searches for light about every 2 seconds in an area: ±60 blocks in X/Z, ±16 in height
   (in a straight line up to ~60 blocks, diagonally up to ~85). It picks the nearest suitable block.
2. It finds the nearest bright light source (brightness 12+) that it can see in a straight line
   (not through walls) — and walks to it.
3. When it arrives (closer than ~2.5 blocks) it BREAKS it at the same speed as ordinary blocks
   (cracks grow on the block; a torch breaks at once, glowstone in a couple of seconds) (see block-breaking.txt). If the light cannot be broken (bedrock/obsidian)
   or breaking is turned off — it stands for ~4 seconds and leaves.
4. If the light is still there, it counts as "visited" for 2 minutes, and the zombie looks for another one or wanders as usual.
5. As soon as the zombie notices a player — it attacks the player. While it remembers the player
   (PlayerGrudge, up to 32 blocks), it does not go to light even if the player hid underground.
6. VILLAGERS MATTER MORE THAN LIGHT: if there is a villager within 40 blocks (±16 in height, through walls),
   the zombie targets it and goes to kill/infect it. This is checked both before heading to a light
   and every half second on the way / at the light — the zombie abandons the torch halfway.


WHO TAKES PART
-------------
  Yes:  regular zombie, husk, zombie villager.
  No:   drowned (in water), zombified piglin, Rotten Overseer, raid minions.


WHAT COUNTS AS LIGHT
--------------------
Any block with brightness >= MIN_LIGHT_LEVEL (default 12):
  torch, glowstone, lantern, sea lantern, campfire, fire, etc.
LAVA (and a lava cauldron) and GLOW BERRIES (cave vines) are always excluded.

Important: the game does not remember who placed a block. Village torches attract zombies too.
If that is a problem — raise MIN_LIGHT_LEVEL to 15.


SETTINGS (LightSeeking.java)
-----------------------------
  ENABLED ................... true / false — turn the whole behavior on/off
  MIN_LIGHT_LEVEL ........... 12 — minimum brightness of the light source
  SEARCH_RADIUS_HORIZONTAL .. 60 — "vision" radius around the zombie (blocks)
  SEARCH_RADIUS_VERTICAL .... 16 — up/down (blocks)
  REQUIRE_LINE_OF_SIGHT ..... true — light behind a wall is not noticed
  SCAN_INTERVAL_TICKS ....... 40 — pause between searches (20 ticks = 1 sec)
  MOVE_SPEED ................ 1.0 — walking speed toward the light
  ARRIVE_DISTANCE ........... 2.5 — counts as "arrived" when closer than this (blocks)
  WAIT_AT_LIGHT_TICKS ....... 80 — how long to stand at the light
  IGNORE_VISITED_LIGHT_TICKS  2400 — how long before returning to the same light
  MAX_WALK_TICKS ............ 600 — maximum for one trip (30 sec)
  VILLAGER_BEATS_LIGHT ...... true — a villager nearby matters more than light (false — as before)
  VILLAGER_SENSE_RADIUS ..... 40 — distance at which the zombie senses a villager (blocks)
  VILLAGER_SENSE_VERTICAL ... 16 — up/down (in vanilla only ±4)
  VILLAGER_RECHECK_TICKS .... 10 — how often to check for a villager on the way to the light


TEST IN GAME
-----------
1. Create a cheats-enabled world, night: /time set night, /gamerule doMobSpawning true (default).
2. Move ~15 blocks away from the zombie so it cannot see you (behind a hill/house).
3. Place a torch or glowstone in the open, in the zombie's field of view.
4. A zombie without a target should slowly walk up to the torch, stand, and move on.
5. Quick test: /summon minecraft:zombie ~ ~ ~ near the torch at a distance of 10 blocks.
