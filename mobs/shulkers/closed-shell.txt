SCHALKER: CLOSED PANZIRE POWERS
========================================

Type: A regular shalker (not a new mob).
Code: shulker/ShulkerShellDurability.java (chance and proportion of strength)
mixin/ShulkerShellMixin.java (hitting a closed shell)


What's going on?
--------------
1. A blow to a closed shell with a 5 percent chance removes half the strength of that shell.
than hit: sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel, hoe, bow, crossbow.
2. An arrow on a closed shalker still doesn't hurt him.
Onions can still lose half the scale.
3. The charm of “strength” does not reduce this write-off. If less than half is left, the thing breaks down.
At the time of writing off, you can hear the crack of a broken instrument, even if it is still intact.
4. Open or open shalker weapons do not spoil. Fist punch, too.
5. The chance for each stroke is separate. The shalkers don't share a counter.
The player in creative mode does not lose strength.


SETTINGS (ShulkerShellDurability.java)
---------------------------------------
ENABLED - False shell does not spoil anything again.
HIT CHANCE is 0.05 = 5% per impact. 0 = never. 1 = each impact on the closed shell.
DURABILITY FRACTION - 0.5 = half of the scale. 1 = the thing breaks right away.
