SHALKERS: Snacks and Transplantations
================================

Type: A regular shalker (not a new mob). They all look the same.
Code: shulker/ShulkerThreats.java (chances, seconds, range)
mixin/ShulkerThreatMixin.java (appearance and transfer)
mixin/ShulkerAttackGoalMixin.java (shot),
mixin/ShulkerBulletMixin.java.


What's going on?
--------------
1. Approximately one in eight shalker shoots slowing and weakness for 20 seconds.
The levitation remains. After landing, the player is still slow and the punches are weaker.
Schulker, who has already lived in the world before this mechanics, throws up a chance when the chunk loads.
2. Approximately one in three fires a second round in half a second at the same target.
3. In battle, any shalker is transplanted to a neighboring block every 8 seconds.
The target does not throw and does not stop shooting. The usual long-distance jump, when he's wounded, remains, too.
4. About one in four projectiles levitate for 18 seconds instead of the usual 10.
5. Approximately every twelfth shalker teleports a player when hit.
3-5 blocks to the side, on the floor next to it. Other mobs don't teleport.
If there is no free sex, the player stays in place.

The properties are independent: one shalker can slow down, shoot twice, and teleport.
The shield that repulsed the projectile and the blinking after the impact do not give any of this.
Milk relieves levitation, slowdown and weakness.


SETTINGS (ShulkerThreats.java)
-------------------------------
ENABLED - False returns the usual shalkers.
SLOWNESS CHANCE - 0.12 = 12% deceleration shalkers. 0 = no.
SLOWNESS SECONDS: How many seconds does it slow down? 20 = twenty.
SLOWNESS AMPLIFIER - 0 = Deceleration I. 1 = Deceleration II.
WEAKNESS SECONDS - how many seconds the same shalker has a weakness. 20 = twenty. 0 = no.
WEAKNESS AMPLIFIER - 0 = Weakness I. 1 = Weakness II.
DOUBLE SHOT CHANCE - 0.30 = 30% shalkers with two shells.
SECOND SHOT TICKS is a pause between shells. 10 = half a second. 0 = second no.
NEIGHBOR HOP SECONDS - How often do you switch in combat? 8 = once every eight seconds. 0 = off.
NEIGHBOR RANGE - 1 = only the adjacent block.
LONG LEVITATION CHANCE - 0.25 = every fourth round is long.
LONG LEVITATION SECONDS - 18 = eighteen seconds of levitation.
TELEPORT CHANCE - 0.08 = 8% of shalkers teleport the player.
TELEPORT MIN BLOCKS / TELEPORT MAX BLOCKS - range, 3 and 5 blocks.

SCHALKER: CLOSED PANZIRE POWERS
========================================

Type: A regular shalker (not a new mob).
Code: shulker/ShulkerShellDurability.java (chance and proportion of strength)
mixin/ShulkerShellMixin.java (hitting a closed shell)


What's going on?
--------------
1. A blow to a closed shell with a 5 percent chance removes half the strength of that shell.
than hit: sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel, hoe, bow, crossbow.
2. An arrow on a closed shalker still doesn't hurt him.
Onions can still lose half the scale.
3. The charm of “strength” does not reduce this write-off. If less than half is left, the thing breaks down.
At the time of writing off, you can hear the crack of a broken instrument, even if it is still intact.
4. Open or open shalker weapons do not spoil. Fist punch, too.
5. The chance for each stroke is separate. The shalkers don't share a counter.
The player in creative mode does not lose strength.


SETTINGS (ShulkerShellDurability.java)
---------------------------------------
ENABLED - False shell does not spoil anything again.
HIT CHANCE is 0.05 = 5% per impact. 0 = never. 1 = each impact on the closed shell.
DURABILITY FRACTION - 0.5 = half of the scale. 1 = the thing breaks right away.
