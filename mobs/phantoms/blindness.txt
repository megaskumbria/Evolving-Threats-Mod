PHANTOMS: BLINDNESS ON BITE
========================

Type: part of normal phantoms (not a new mob or separate species).
Code: phantom/PhantomBlindness.java (chance and duration),
     mixin/PhantomBlindnessMixin.java (mark on phantom),
     mixin/PhantomBlindnessMobMixin.java (roll on spawn, save, on bite).

Who can apply blindness: about every fifth phantom.
Roll is separate from weakness: one phantom may have both, only one, or neither.
Night flock, spawn egg, and spawn command use this roll.
A marked phantom stays marked after rejoining the world.
Chance does not grow with days (FIXED).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. On spawn, 20% chance the phantom can apply blindness.
   Other phantoms never apply blindness.
2. A bite that dealt damage gives random blindness for 7–12 seconds.
   Screen darkens; the phantom is hard to see.
3. Shield block and post-hit invulnerability do not apply a new effect.
4. A repeat bite refreshes duration if blindness remaining is shorter.
   Longer blindness is not shortened by this bite.
5. Milk removes blindness immediately.
6. Hitting another mob does not apply blindness. Players only.
7. Bite damage unchanged (still from “bite and flock”).
   Weakness, if the phantom has it, still applies as before.


SETTINGS (PhantomBlindness.java)
---------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — toggle blindness
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.20 — share of phantoms that can (20%)
  BLINDNESS_MIN_SECONDS ........ 7 — shortest blindness, seconds
  BLINDNESS_MAX_SECONDS ........ 12 — longest blindness, seconds
  Both 0 disables the effect. If min/max are swapped, the game still uses
  the lower and higher values.


TEST
----
1. To test quickly, set SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0 temporarily.
   Every phantom applies blindness. Restore 0.20 after testing.
2. Summon a phantom and let it bite without a shield. Blindness 7–12 seconds.
3. Block with a shield. Blocked bite does not apply blindness.
4. Set SPAWN_CHANCE = 0.20 and summon several phantoms.
   Blindness on about every fifth. Weakness still about every third.
