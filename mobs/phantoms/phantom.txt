PHANTOMS: BITE AND FLOCK
====================

Type: vanilla insomnia phantoms (not a new mob).
Code: phantom/PhantomThreats.java (damage, extra days without sleep, flock cap),
     mixin/PhantomBiteMixin.java (damage at bite),
     mixin/PhantomSpawnerMixin.java (wave size).

Who bites harder: any phantom, including from spawn egg and commands.
Who arrives in a bigger flock: only night spawn from insomnia.
Spawn egg still gives one phantom.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. Unarmored bite: Easy 3 HP (1.5 hearts), Normal 6 HP (3 hearts),
   Hard 9 HP (4.5 hearts). In vanilla that is 2 / 2 / 3.
   Armor and shield reduce damage as usual. Strength potion on the phantom adds on top.
2. For the first three in-game days without a bed, flock size is normal:
   Easy 1–2, Normal 1–3, Hard 1–4.
3. After that, each 2 extra days without sleep adds 1 phantom to the wave.
   Day 5 +1, day 7 +2, day 9 +3.
4. Wave cap: Easy 3, Normal 5, Hard 6.
   On Hard the cap is reached by day 13 without sleep: then every wave is 6.
5. Wave still every 1–2 minutes, per player who has not slept.
   Bed or death resets the counter and flock size returns to normal.


SETTINGS (PhantomThreats.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — toggles bite and flock growth
  EASY_BITE_HP ................. 3 — Easy bite in health units (2 = 1 heart)
  NORMAL_BITE_HP ............... 6 — Normal bite
  HARD_BITE_HP ................. 9 — Hard bite
  CALM_DAYS .................... 3 — days without sleep before flock grows
  DAYS_PER_EXTRA_PHANTOM ....... 2 — extra days per +1 phantom
  EASY_FLOCK_CAP ............... 3 — Easy wave cap
  NORMAL_FLOCK_CAP ............. 5 — Normal cap
  HARD_FLOCK_CAP ............... 6 — Hard cap


TEST
----
1. Normal difficulty. Do not sleep for 3 in-game days, go outside at night above sea level.
   Wave 1–3 phantoms; bite removes 3 hearts with no armor.
2. Wait until day 5 without a bed. Wave has one extra phantom.
3. Sleep in a bed. Next night flock is normal again until insomnia builds up.

PHANTOMS: WEAKNESS ON BITE
=========================

Type: part of normal phantoms (not a new mob or separate species).
Code: phantom/PhantomWeakness.java (chance, duration, amplifier),
     mixin/PhantomWeaknessMixin.java (mark on phantom),
     mixin/PhantomWeaknessMobMixin.java (roll on spawn, save, on bite).

Who can apply weakness: about every third phantom.
Night flock, spawn egg, and spawn command use this roll.
A marked phantom stays marked after rejoining the world.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. On spawn, 30% chance the phantom can apply weakness.
   Other phantoms never apply weakness.
2. A bite that dealt damage gives the player Weakness I for 10 seconds.
   Melee hits deal 4 less damage (2 hearts).
3. Shield block and post-hit invulnerability do not apply a new effect.
4. A repeat bite refreshes duration if weakness remaining is shorter.
   A longer weakness is not shortened by this bite.
5. Milk removes weakness immediately.
6. Hitting another mob does not apply weakness. Players only.
7. Bite damage is unchanged by this mechanic (still from “bite and flock”).


SETTINGS (PhantomWeakness.java)
--------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — toggle weakness
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.30 — share of phantoms that can (30%)
  WEAKNESS_SECONDS ............. 10 — duration in seconds
  AMPLIFIER .................... 0 — Weakness I (1 = Weakness II)


TEST
----
1. To test quickly, set SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0 temporarily.
   Every phantom applies weakness. Restore 0.30 after testing.
2. Summon a phantom and let it bite without a shield. Weakness I for 10 seconds.
3. Block with a shield. Blocked bite does not apply weakness.
4. Set SPAWN_CHANCE = 0.30 and summon several phantoms.
   Weakness on about every third.

PHANTOMS: BLINDNESS ON BITE
========================

Type: part of normal phantoms (not a new mob or separate species).
Code: phantom/PhantomBlindness.java (chance and duration),
     mixin/PhantomBlindnessMixin.java (mark on phantom),
     mixin/PhantomBlindnessMobMixin.java (roll on spawn, save, on bite).

Who can apply blindness: about every fifth phantom.
Roll is separate from weakness: one phantom may have both, only one, or neither.
Night flock, spawn egg, and spawn command use this roll.
A marked phantom stays marked after rejoining the world.
Chance does not grow with days (FIXED).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. On spawn, 20% chance the phantom can apply blindness.
   Other phantoms never apply blindness.
2. A bite that dealt damage gives random blindness for 7–12 seconds.
   Screen darkens; the phantom is hard to see.
3. Shield block and post-hit invulnerability do not apply a new effect.
4. A repeat bite refreshes duration if blindness remaining is shorter.
   Longer blindness is not shortened by this bite.
5. Milk removes blindness immediately.
6. Hitting another mob does not apply blindness. Players only.
7. Bite damage unchanged (still from “bite and flock”).
   Weakness, if the phantom has it, still applies as before.


SETTINGS (PhantomBlindness.java)
---------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — toggle blindness
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.20 — share of phantoms that can (20%)
  BLINDNESS_MIN_SECONDS ........ 7 — shortest blindness, seconds
  BLINDNESS_MAX_SECONDS ........ 12 — longest blindness, seconds
  Both 0 disables the effect. If min/max are swapped, the game still uses
  the lower and higher values.


TEST
----
1. To test quickly, set SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0 temporarily.
   Every phantom applies blindness. Restore 0.20 after testing.
2. Summon a phantom and let it bite without a shield. Blindness 7–12 seconds.
3. Block with a shield. Blocked bite does not apply blindness.
4. Set SPAWN_CHANCE = 0.20 and summon several phantoms.
   Blindness on about every fifth. Weakness still about every third.
