PHANTOMS: WEAKNESS ON BITE
=========================

Type: part of normal phantoms (not a new mob or separate species).
Code: phantom/PhantomWeakness.java (chance, duration, amplifier),
     mixin/PhantomWeaknessMixin.java (mark on phantom),
     mixin/PhantomWeaknessMobMixin.java (roll on spawn, save, on bite).

Who can apply weakness: about every third phantom.
Night flock, spawn egg, and spawn command use this roll.
A marked phantom stays marked after rejoining the world.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. On spawn, 30% chance the phantom can apply weakness.
   Other phantoms never apply weakness.
2. A bite that dealt damage gives the player Weakness I for 10 seconds.
   Melee hits deal 4 less damage (2 hearts).
3. Shield block and post-hit invulnerability do not apply a new effect.
4. A repeat bite refreshes duration if weakness remaining is shorter.
   A longer weakness is not shortened by this bite.
5. Milk removes weakness immediately.
6. Hitting another mob does not apply weakness. Players only.
7. Bite damage is unchanged by this mechanic (still from “bite and flock”).


SETTINGS (PhantomWeakness.java)
--------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — toggle weakness
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.30 — share of phantoms that can (30%)
  WEAKNESS_SECONDS ............. 10 — duration in seconds
  AMPLIFIER .................... 0 — Weakness I (1 = Weakness II)


TEST
----
1. To test quickly, set SPAWN_CHANCE = 1.0 temporarily.
   Every phantom applies weakness. Restore 0.30 after testing.
2. Summon a phantom and let it bite without a shield. Weakness I for 10 seconds.
3. Block with a shield. Blocked bite does not apply weakness.
4. Set SPAWN_CHANCE = 0.30 and summon several phantoms.
   Weakness on about every third.
