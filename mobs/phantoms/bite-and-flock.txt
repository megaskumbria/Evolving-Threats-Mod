PHANTOMS: BITE AND FLOCK
====================

Type: vanilla insomnia phantoms (not a new mob).
Code: phantom/PhantomThreats.java (damage, extra days without sleep, flock cap),
     mixin/PhantomBiteMixin.java (damage at bite),
     mixin/PhantomSpawnerMixin.java (wave size).

Who bites harder: any phantom, including from spawn egg and commands.
Who arrives in a bigger flock: only night spawn from insomnia.
Spawn egg still gives one phantom.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. Unarmored bite: Easy 3 HP (1.5 hearts), Normal 6 HP (3 hearts),
   Hard 9 HP (4.5 hearts). In vanilla that is 2 / 2 / 3.
   Armor and shield reduce damage as usual. Strength potion on the phantom adds on top.
2. For the first three in-game days without a bed, flock size is normal:
   Easy 1–2, Normal 1–3, Hard 1–4.
3. After that, each 2 extra days without sleep adds 1 phantom to the wave.
   Day 5 +1, day 7 +2, day 9 +3.
4. Wave cap: Easy 3, Normal 5, Hard 6.
   On Hard the cap is reached by day 13 without sleep: then every wave is 6.
5. Wave still every 1–2 minutes, per player who has not slept.
   Bed or death resets the counter and flock size returns to normal.


SETTINGS (PhantomThreats.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — toggles bite and flock growth
  EASY_BITE_HP ................. 3 — Easy bite in health units (2 = 1 heart)
  NORMAL_BITE_HP ............... 6 — Normal bite
  HARD_BITE_HP ................. 9 — Hard bite
  CALM_DAYS .................... 3 — days without sleep before flock grows
  DAYS_PER_EXTRA_PHANTOM ....... 2 — extra days per +1 phantom
  EASY_FLOCK_CAP ............... 3 — Easy wave cap
  NORMAL_FLOCK_CAP ............. 5 — Normal cap
  HARD_FLOCK_CAP ............... 6 — Hard cap


TEST
----
1. Normal difficulty. Do not sleep for 3 in-game days, go outside at night above sea level.
   Wave 1–3 phantoms; bite removes 3 hearts with no armor.
2. Wait until day 5 without a bed. Wave has one extra phantom.
3. Sleep in a bed. Next night flock is normal again until insomnia builds up.
