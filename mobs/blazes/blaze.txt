Blaze chemist
==============

Type: a rare variant of an ordinary blaze. It looks like vanilla.
Code: blaze/BlazeAlchemist.java (settings, resistance slice, command),
     mixin/BlazeAlchemistMixin.java (flag and pause between special balls),
     mixin/BlazeAlchemistMobMixin.java (spawn chance and save),
     mixin/SmallFireballAlchemyMixin.java (which ball cuts resistance).


How it works
------------
1. On spawn, chance SPAWN_CHANCE (4%). Fortress spawns can also roll alchemist.
2. Blaze still spins rods for 3 seconds and fires a volley of three balls.
   All three are ordinary fireballs: same kind, speed, damage, and ignition.
   First volley ball, if pause has passed, further trims fire resistance.
3. If fire resistance on target is longer than 20 seconds, 20 seconds remain.
   Potion level unchanged (II stays II).
   If none or already shorter than 20 seconds, timer untouched.
   Infinite resistance from commands is also cut to 20 seconds.
   Ball still hits and burns like normal blaze ball.
4. Shield facing the ball catches resistance slice. Shield ball damage not cancelled.
5. In a block, ball places fire as usual.
6. Next slicing ball not sooner than 20 seconds apart.
   Normal volleys during pause behave like ordinary blaze.
   Up close (closer than 2 blocks) blaze melee hits and does not shoot — step back to see volley.


COMMAND
-------
  /etalchemist
  /evolvingthreats spawn alchemist
Blaze appears 10 blocks ahead and immediately targets you.
Operator rights required, like other mod commands.


SETTINGS (BlazeAlchemist.java)
-------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true — false turns off spawn and resistance slice
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.04 — world chance (4%). Command ignores chance
  FIRE_RESISTANCE_LEFT_SECONDS . 20 — seconds of resistance left after hit
  SHOT_COOLDOWN_SECONDS ........ 20 — pause between resistance-cutting balls


TEST
----
1. Drink long fire resistance. Enter /etalchemist and step back if standing close.
2. Wait for rods to finish. Balls look like normal blaze fire.
3. Let first hit land. Effect lasts ~20 seconds. Ball still burns.
4. Block next such ball with shield. Resistance timer must not reset.
