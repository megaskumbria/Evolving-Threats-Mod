MAGNET CREEPER
==============

Type: rare variant of the normal creeper. Looks vanilla, but electric sparks show while it pulls.
Code: creeper/MagnetCreeper.java (settings, pull, command),
     mixin/CreeperMagnetMixin.java (flag, timer, tick),
     mixin/MobDynamiteCreeperSpawnMixin.java (chance on spawn, after feint and super-fast).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn, SPAWN_CHANCE (1%). FIXED: from day one, does not scale with threat level or moon.
   Never sapper, dynamite thrower, feint, or super-fast.
   Can be charged and a shield-breaker. Charged magnet still pulls the same; the blast is bigger.
2. Until it notices a player, it is a normal creeper.
   Creative and spectator are not pulled.
3. Once it has you as a target, an invisible leash appears just inside the edge of its sight.
   Sight is the creeper's follow range: 32 blocks with the sight mod, 16 if sight is off.
   The leash starts 1 block inside that, so the creeper does not forget you on the rim.
   You can run out to that leash and no farther. Walking and sprinting both reach it at first,
   because the tug starts weaker than walking.
4. Over RAMP_SECONDS (8) the edge shrinks to 2 blocks and the tug grows past sprint.
   Hanging at the edge does not last: you are reeled in even if you keep sprinting.
   Speed potions and a short elytra burst do not open a lasting gap.
   An ender pearl far outside the leash snaps you back onto it, unless that spot is inside a block.
5. Closer than 1.5 blocks the tug stops, so you are not pushed through the body.
   By then the hiss has started. Fuse and blast are a normal creeper's (1.5 seconds),
   unless it is also charged.
6. If it loses you for more than 1 second, the next meeting starts weak again.
   A brief corner does not reset the pull.
7. A horse or boat is dragged with you. Flint and steel still ignites it.


COMMAND
-------
  /etmagnet
  /evolvingthreats spawn magnet
Creeper spawns 16 blocks in front of where you look and immediately pulls.
Requires operator permissions. This spawn is not charged; lightning still charges it.


SETTINGS (MagnetCreeper.java)
-----------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.01 = 1%. 0 = command only
  RAMP_SECONDS ................. 8 — how long you can hold the edge of sight before the reel
  ARRIVE_BLOCKS ................ 2 — leash shrinks to this
  EDGE_MARGIN .................. 1 — leash starts this far inside sight, so it keeps the target
  START_PULL_PER_TICK .......... 0.10 — weaker than walking (0.22). Sprint is about 0.28
  END_PULL_PER_TICK ............ 0.60 — stronger than sprint, you are dragged in
  MAX_PULL_PER_TICK ............ 1.5 — yank when already outside the leash
  VERTICAL_PULL ................ 0.5 — 0 = ground only, 1 = straight at the creeper
  STOP_BLOCKS .................. 1.5 — tug stops this close
  SNAP_BLOCKS .................. 6 — pearl / long dive snaps back onto the leash
  LOSE_GRACE_TICKS ............. 20 — 1 second without a target resets the ramp
  SHOW_PARTICLES ............... true — electric sparks while pulling
  PARTICLE_EVERY_TICKS ......... 2
