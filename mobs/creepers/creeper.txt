DYNAMITE-THROWER CREEPER
========================

Type: rare variant of the normal creeper. Looks vanilla — no separate texture.
Code: creeper/DynamiteCreeper.java (settings, throw, spawn command),
     entity/ai/DynamiteThrowGoal.java (distance and retreat),
     mixin/CreeperDynamiteMixin.java (goal and persistence),
     mixin/MobDynamiteCreeperSpawnMixin.java (chance on spawn),
     mixin/SwellGoalMixin.java (does not swell or explode on its own).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On normal spawn, SPAWN_CHANCE (default 4%) to become a thrower.
2. Does not melee the player and does not explode itself. If you get closer than 8 blocks — backs away.
3. At 8–16 blocks it strafes sideways around the player (does not stand still) and throws dynamite.
   Dynamite flies toward the feet and lands shortly before detonation. Fuse 2–3 seconds. Pause between throws 8 seconds.
4. Beyond 16 blocks — moves closer to throw. Beyond 28 — gives up the chase.
5. Flint and steel can still ignite it, like a normal creeper.
6. Dynamite is vanilla TNT: breaks blocks unless the tntExplodes gamerule is off.


COMMAND (needs gamemaster permissions, like other mod commands)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  /etdynamite
  /evolvingthreats spawn dynamite
Creeper spawns 10 blocks in front of where you look and immediately targets you.


SETTINGS (DynamiteCreeper.java)
--------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.04 — world spawn chance (0.04 = 4%)
  RETREAT_DISTANCE ............. 8 — backs away closer than this
  THROW_MAX_DISTANCE ........... 16 — moves closer beyond this
  HOLD_DISTANCE ................ 12 — distance at which it circles
  STRAFE_SIDE_BLOCKS ........... 6 — how far sideways it runs
  THROW_BLOCKS_PER_TICK ........ 0.70 — dynamite speed (higher = flatter arc)
  LAND_BEFORE_EXPLODE_TICKS .... 8 — lands at feet this many ticks before explosion
  THROW_COOLDOWN_SECONDS ....... 8
  FUSE_MIN_SECONDS / MAX ....... 2 / 3 — time until dynamite explodes

CHARGED CREEPER
==================

Type: normal creeper, already charged like after lightning. Aura around it is visible.
Code: creeper/ChargedCreeper.java (chance),
     mixin/CreeperPowerAccessor.java (charged flag),
     mixin/MobDynamiteCreeperSpawnMixin.java (roll on spawn, before thrower).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. On world spawn, 1% chance: roughly one creeper in a hundred is already charged.
   Lightning still charges any creeper.
2. Explosion twice as strong as normal. Head may drop from it.
3. Does not throw dynamite.
4. Separate chance it may be a sapper or shield-breaker.
   Then that skill works as usual, but the explosion is still charged.
5. Commands /etsapper, /etshield, and /ettnt do not roll this chance.


SETTINGS (ChargedCreeper.java)
-------------------------------
ENABLED — false: does not spawn charged on its own. Lightning still charges.
SPAWN_CHANCE — 0.01 = 1%. 0 = never. 1 = every creeper charged.

SHIELD-BREAK CREEPER
==========================

Type: rare variant of the normal creeper. Looks vanilla.
Code: creeper/ShieldBreakCreeper.java (settings, shield destruction, command),
     mixin/CreeperShieldBreakMixin.java (flag and persistence),
     mixin/LivingEntityShieldBreakMixin.java (moment shield blocked the blast),
     mixin/MobDynamiteCreeperSpawnMixin.java (chance on spawn, after sapper and thrower).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn, SPAWN_CHANCE (5%). If sapper or thrower already rolled — this creeper is normal.
2. Runs and explodes like a normal creeper. Fuse 1.5 sec, normal explosion power.
3. If the player blocked with a shield and the blast hit the shield (facing the creeper) —
   the shield is destroyed entirely, no damage to the player.
   Durability and Unbreaking do not help.
4. If the shield is not raised or the player is not facing the blast — shield intact, damage as normal creeper.
   A charged creeper can also be a shield-breaker: shield breaks in one blast, bigger hole.
5. Flint and steel still ignites it.


COMMAND
-------
  /etshield
  /evolvingthreats spawn shield
Creeper spawns 8 blocks in front of where you look.


SETTINGS (ShieldBreakCreeper.java)
-----------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.05 — world chance (5%)

SAPPER CREEPER
=================

Type: rare variant of the normal creeper. Looks vanilla.
Code: creeper/SapperCreeper.java (settings, blast, command),
     entity/ai/SapperBreachGoal.java (where it goes and when it detonates),
     mixin/CreeperSapperMixin.java (flag and persistence),
     mixin/MobDynamiteCreeperSpawnMixin.java (chance on spawn, before thrower),
     mixin/SwellGoalMixin.java (does not swell up close vanilla-style on its own).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn, SPAWN_CHANCE (5%, like jack-o-lantern). If sapper rolled,
   dynamite thrower is no longer rolled for it. Charged creeper can be a sapper:
   then the explosion is still twice as strong.
2. Explosion power and hiss like a normal creeper (1.5 sec). Nothing reduced.
3. Walks to the block under the player's feet (floor, tower top, step).
   Reaches it — hisses 1.5 sec and explodes.
   If you walk away while it hisses, fuse resets like a normal creeper.
   Hit and knockback alone in open ground do not trigger the blast.
4. Tower with no path up: approaches the base (within 2.2 blocks horizontally) and hisses.
   Walk away from the tower — hissing stops.
5. No path and player not visible (house, wall, closed door): gets as close as possible,
   presses against the wall, stands 8–10 seconds and explodes. This blast cannot be cancelled.
   Remembers the player for 90 seconds (MEMORY_SECONDS), even if they went deep inside the house.
   If the door opens or the player is visible again before the wait ends — timer resets, creeper runs again.
6. Flint and steel still ignites it. Fuse from flint and steel does not reset.


COMMAND
-------
  /etsapper
  /evolvingthreats spawn sapper
Creeper spawns 8 blocks in front of where you look.


SETTINGS (SapperCreeper.java)
------------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.05 — world chance (5%)
  DETONATE_HORIZONTAL .......... 2.2 — flush against support
  ELEVATED_BLOCKS .............. 2 — blocks higher to count as a tower
  PATH_REACH_BLOCKS ............ 2 — "can reach" if path gets this close
  WALL_DELAY_MIN / MAX ......... 8 / 10 seconds until blast at wall
  STUCK_MOVE_BLOCKS / TICKS .... 0.6 blocks in 20 ticks — "stuck and standing"
  MEMORY_SECONDS ............... 90 — how long it remembers player without line of sight
  CHASE_MAX_DISTANCE ........... 40 — gives up beyond this

SUPER-FAST CREEPER
====================

Type: rare variant of the normal creeper. Looks vanilla.
Code: creeper/FastCreeper.java (settings, speed, command),
     mixin/CreeperFastMixin.java (flag and persistence),
     mixin/MobDynamiteCreeperSpawnMixin.java (chance on spawn).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn, SPAWN_CHANCE (3%). FIXED: from day one, does not scale with threat level.
   Does not become sapper, dynamite thrower, or feint.
   Charged creeper and shield-breaker can be fast.
2. Runs twice as fast as a normal creeper. Sprint and sprint-jumps do not outrun it:
   on a straight line it catches up.
3. Remembers the player for 48 blocks. Normal creeper range is about 16:
   run farther — chase ends. That does not work here.
4. Hisses 0.5 seconds and explodes. Normal creeper hiss is 1.5 seconds.
   Same explosion power. Hard to run away in half a second.
   Once hissing stops without exploding, it runs and catches up again.
5. Speed potion on spawn can make it even faster.


COMMAND
-------
  /etfast
  /evolvingthreats spawn fast_creeper
Creeper spawns 12 blocks in front of where you look and immediately runs at you.
Requires operator permissions.


SETTINGS (FastCreeper.java)
----------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.03 = 3%. 0 = command only
  CHASE_SPEED .................. 0.50. Normal creeper 0.25. 0.35 you can still escape
  FOLLOW_RANGE ................. 48 blocks. 16 = like normal
  FUSE_SECONDS ................. 0.5 = half a second of hiss. Normal creeper 1.5

FEINT CREEPER
===============

Type: rare variant of the normal creeper. Body like vanilla, no name tag.
Speed potion particles always around it — that's how you spot it early.
Code: creeper/FeintCreeper.java (settings, burst, command),
     mixin/CreeperFeintMixin.java (flag, distance and tick),
     mixin/MobDynamiteCreeperSpawnMixin.java (chance on spawn).


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. On spawn, SPAWN_CHANCE (0.3%). FIXED: from day one, does not scale with threat level or moon.
   Never sapper, dynamite thrower, or super-fast.
   Can be charged and shield-breaker.
2. While the player is far, acts like a normal creeper: speed 0.25, hiss 1.5 seconds.
   Sprint gets away from it. Remembers player like normal, about 16 blocks, not 48.
   Speed potion particles swirl constantly even while it still moves slowly.
   Visual only: particles do not make it faster. Visible from about 32 blocks.
3. Each feint creeper picks one distance between 6 and 7 blocks once.
   When the player it chases enters that zone — disguise drops.
   Iron golem and other mobs do not trigger the burst: player only.
4. After reveal, runs like super-fast (speed 0.50) and hisses 0.5 seconds.
   Same explosion power. Hard to run away in half a second.
   Hiss still starts up close, about 3 blocks, like normal:
   from 7 blocks the blast does not reach the player, so only the sprint there.
   It is already charging before hiss, and hiss is three times shorter.
5. By default, once revealed — no pretending for the rest of its life.
   Step back and make it slow again — not possible.
   If you run so far it gives up chase, next time it is fast immediately.
   Constant KEEP_BURST = false restores disguise when the player left the zone
   and hiss stopped without exploding.
6. Speed potion on spawn can make the burst even faster.


COMMAND
-------
  /etfeint
  /evolvingthreats spawn feint_creeper
Creeper spawns 12 blocks in front of where you look and walks toward you like normal,
until it enters the 6–7 block zone.
Requires operator permissions.


SETTINGS (FeintCreeper.java)
-----------------------------
  ENABLED ...................... true
  SPAWN_CHANCE ................. 0.003 = 0.3%. 0 = command only
  TRIGGER_MIN / TRIGGER_MAX .... 6 and 7 blocks. Each creeper picks its number between them
  CHASE_SPEED .................. 0.50 after reveal. Like super-fast. Normal 0.25
  CALM_SPEED ................... 0.25 while pretending
  FUSE_SECONDS ................. 0.5 = half a second hiss after reveal
  CALM_FUSE_SECONDS ............ 1.5 = normal hiss while disguise holds
  KEEP_BURST ................... true = after reveal, fast forever
  SPEED_PARTICLES .............. true = speed potion particles for feint's whole life
  SPEED_PARTICLE_EVERY_TICKS ... how often one particle. 2 = slightly denser than drunk potion.
                                 0 = do not spawn
