CHARGED CREEPER
==================

Type: normal creeper, already charged like after lightning. Aura around it is visible.
Code: creeper/ChargedCreeper.java (chance),
     mixin/CreeperPowerAccessor.java (charged flag),
     mixin/MobDynamiteCreeperSpawnMixin.java (roll on spawn, before thrower).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. On world spawn, 1% chance: roughly one creeper in a hundred is already charged.
   Lightning still charges any creeper.
2. Explosion twice as strong as normal. Head may drop from it.
3. Does not throw dynamite.
4. Separate chance it may be a sapper, shield-breaker, or magnet.
   Then that skill works as usual, but the explosion is still charged.
5. Commands /etsapper, /etshield, and /ettnt do not roll this chance.


SETTINGS (ChargedCreeper.java)
-------------------------------
ENABLED — false: does not spawn charged on its own. Lightning still charges.
SPAWN_CHANCE — 0.01 = 1%. 0 = never. 1 = every creeper charged.
