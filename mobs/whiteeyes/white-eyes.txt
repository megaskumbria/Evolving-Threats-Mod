WHITE EYES
==========

Type: one boss in the upper world. Looks like Steve with solid white eyes, iron armor, diamond sword.
The eyes stay white in the dark. The player drawing is not used: it puts Steve's purple pupils back.
Code: whiteeyes/WhiteEyes.java (the fight),
     whiteeyes/WhiteEyesNight.java (which night, the bed),
     whiteeyes/WhiteEyesData.java (the world remembers the night).
Picture: textures/entity/white_eyes.png, drawn by whiteeyes/WhiteEyesRenderer.java.
Test: /etwhiteeyes


WHEN
----
Scale night 100, then 200, 300, and so on (ThreatTable.WHITE_EYES_DAY).
The bar on screen still stops at 100. This counter keeps going.
Not a chance. The moon does not change it.
Peaceful: he does not come, and the bed works.
If nobody is in the upper world that night, that visit is missed until the next hundred.
One at a time. If he is already alive, that night does not add another.
He appears on the surface, 40 blocks from the player closest to world spawn.
That night, while it is dark, the bed will not take you. No bell, no warning line.
The only text is the player's own short reason, in chat, when they try to lie down.
A siege scheduled for the same night is pushed off.


WHAT HE DOES
------------
1. 600 health (300 hearts). Iron armor is Protection II. The sword shows Sharpness V.
   Sun, lava, and water do not kill him. In water he swims at the surface.
   Slowness, Weakness, and poison from you still work. Hurt sound is a blaze.
   Armor and the sword never drop. He drops a name tag "White Eyes", one enchanted golden apple, and 500 experience.
   The usual double experience does not multiply the 500.
   The killer also gets the challenge Unblinking once: one netherite block. No extra experience.
2. Sword before armor: 5 hearts on a naked player, about 1 heart in full diamond with Protection IV.
   Same hit on easy, normal, and hard. A little faster than once a second, and only when the blade can touch you.
   The sword does not knock you back, and hits do not knock him back.
   A raised shield blocks the sword. He does not knock the shield down.
3. He hunts the closest living player in this world, including behind a hill or in a cave, until one of you dies.
   A real wall in front of him is broken. Dirt is almost instant, stone about 1 second, obsidian about 8 seconds.
   He does not dig the floor under his own feet, bedrock, a nether portal, or the end portal and its frame.
   A chest on the path breaks and the items fall. One block at foot level is a step, not a wall.
4. If you are 3 or more blocks above him and the path never reaches that floor, or there is no route
   (no path, a path that goes nowhere, or you are more than 96 blocks away), he waits 5 seconds, then appears 2 blocks behind you.
   In the second phase that wait is 3 seconds. He does not teleport through a wall he is already digging.
   If there is no path and you are more than 8 blocks away, he teleports instead of digging a tunnel.
   While you glide he keeps walking and does not teleport. Enderman sound and particles when he moves.
   He does not swing for half a second after that, and he does not throw for 6 seconds. He will not teleport again for 8 seconds.
5. Bow: every 14 to 16 seconds, if he can see you. He draws for 1 second, shoots, and the sword is back.
   He keeps walking while he aims. Punch 5 throws you. About 2 hearts through full netherite with no Protection.
   Protection makes it less. A shield stops the arrow. The bow never drops.
   In water or lava the wait is gone: he shoots again as soon as the arrow leaves, while he can still see you.
6. Throw: within 5 blocks, every 30 seconds, 50 to 60 blocks up. He does not stop to do it.
   No Slow Falling. The landing is a normal fall. A shield does not stop it.
7. Second phase at 150 health (75 hearts): one blaze shot and smoke. He walks faster, 0.40 instead of 0.30.
   The sword does not hit harder. From then on he also lights fire and throws the wither bottle.
   Fire: a 3×3 under you if you are farther than 4 blocks and standing on the ground. It stays lit 5 seconds even on stone.
   A punch puts that flame out. Wood catches and the fire spreads. It does not start while you are in water.
   Next fire after 14 seconds, or 9 in the second phase. A shield does not stop it.
   Wither: a splash bottle, Wither I for 8 seconds, when you are 6–18 blocks away.
   About 2 hearts through armor if you stay in it. Milk clears it. Every 30 seconds. A shield does not stop it.
8. Bow, crossbow, or trident: he counts your hits. The cycle is randomly 2 or 4.
   The last hit of that cycle is returned to you, then he rolls 2 or 4 again.
   Your armor reduces the returned hit. A miss does not count.
9. Another dimension, or you die: he waits 15 seconds, walks home, and heals to full over 20 seconds.
   He does not wait by the bed.

Not in the fight: the speed burst, cave spiders, sword effects, and knocking the shield down.


SETTINGS (WhiteEyes.java)
--------------------------
MAX_HEALTH — 600 = 300 hearts.
PHASE_HEALTH — 150 = second phase. Fire, the wither bottle, and the faster walk start here.
MOVE_SPEED — 0.30 before that. PHASE2_MOVE_SPEED — 0.40 after.
SWORD_DAMAGE — 10 before armor. SWORD_REACH — 2 blocks.
BOW_DAMAGE — 12 before armor. BOW_GAP_MIN_SECONDS / BOW_GAP_MAX_SECONDS — 14 and 16.
FLUID_BOW_GAP_SECONDS — 0 = no wait between shots in water or lava. BOW_DRAW_SECONDS — 1. BOW_PUNCH — 5.
THROW_RANGE — 5 blocks. THROW_BLOCKS_MIN / THROW_BLOCKS_MAX — 50 and 60. THROW_GAP_SECONDS — 30.
THROW_AFTER_TELEPORT_SECONDS — 6.
FIRE_MIN — 4 blocks. FIRE_PATCH — 3. FIRE_HOLD_SECONDS — 5.
FIRE_GAP_SECONDS — 14. FIRE_GAP_PHASE2_SECONDS — 9.
POTION_MIN / POTION_MAX — 6 and 18. WITHER_SECONDS — 8. POTION_GAP_SECONDS — 30.
TELEPORT_WAIT_SECONDS — 5. TELEPORT_WAIT_PHASE2_SECONDS — 3. TELEPORT_GAP_SECONDS — 8. LEASH_BLOCKS — 96.
SPAWN_DISTANCE — 40. GIVE_UP_SECONDS — 15. HEAL_SECONDS — 20. EXPERIENCE_REWARD — 500.
