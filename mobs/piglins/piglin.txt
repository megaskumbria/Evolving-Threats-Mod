PIGLIN BRUTE: HEALTH
=============================

Type: vanilla piglin brute (not a new mob).
Code: piglin/PiglinBruteHealth.java (health value),
     mixin/PiglinBruteHealthMixin.java (on spawn).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. A newly spawned brute has 60 health (30 hearts). In vanilla it is 50 (25 hearts).
2. Regular piglins and zombified piglins are unchanged.
3. A brute already in the world keeps its old health until it despawns and respawns.


SETTINGS (PiglinBruteHealth.java)
----------------------------------
ENABLED — false restores vanilla 50.
MAX_HEALTH — 60 = 30 hearts.

PIGLIN BRUTE: ARMOR DAMAGE
=================================

Type: vanilla piglin brute (not a new mob). Regular piglin and zombified piglin do not do this.
Code: piglin/PiglinBruteArmorHit.java (chance, durability fraction, nausea),
     mixin/PiglinBruteArmorMixin.java (on hit).


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. About every twentieth hit that already dealt health damage also damages one armor piece.
2. Half of that piece’s maximum durability is removed. Netherite chestplates lose half the bar
   even with Unbreaking: the enchant does not reduce this hit.
3. Which piece is random among equipped slots: helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots.
   Turtle shell counts. Carved pumpkin and elytra do not.
4. If less than half durability remains, the item breaks and is destroyed.
5. At the same moment you hear armor crack and get 10 seconds of nausea.
   The screen wobbles so the special hit is obvious. Crack plays even if the item did not break yet.
6. With no armor, nothing happens. Creative players do not lose durability either.
   A shield block and post-hit invulnerability do not roll the chance.


SETTINGS (PiglinBruteArmorHit.java)
-------------------------------------
ENABLED — false restores normal hits.
HIT_CHANCE — 0.05 = about every twentieth damaging hit. 0 = never. 1 = every hit.
DURABILITY_FRACTION — 0.5 = half the bar. 1 = item breaks immediately.
NAUSEA_SECONDS — 10 = ten seconds of nausea. 0 = no effect.
