HELL BERSERK
=============

Type: his own mob. Not a piglin brute. He does not hunt piglins, and piglins do not hunt him.
Spawn: anywhere in the Nether, in place of a hostile mob that has ground under it. A ghast in the air stays a ghast.
Scale: FIXED. The chance does not grow with the day.
Code: piglin/HellBerserk.java (health, axes, rage, spawn),
      entity/ai/HellBerserkDigGoal.java (breaking blocks),
      spawn/NaturalMonsterSwap.java (the Nether roll),
      client/piglin/HellBerserkModel.java (the body),
      client/piglin/HellBerserkRenderer.java and HellBerserkAxeLayer.java (axes in the hands).
Test: /etberserk or /evolvingthreats spawn berserk. Spawns 6 blocks in front of you and looks at you.


WHAT HAPPENS
--------------
1. About 4 of every 100 Nether hostile spawns are a Hell Berserk, after the floating netherite tool has had its roll.
   The chance is the same from the first day. 0 turns natural spawns off. The test command ignores the chance.
2. He has 80 health (40 hearts). Fire and lava do not hurt him.
3. A netherite axe in each hand. Both hit. Neither axe drops.
   About one hit in twenty that already took health also ruins one armor piece, the same as a piglin brute:
   half that piece's bar, and 10 seconds of nausea. Unbreaking does not soften it.
4. He chases players, and anyone who hit him. Follow range is 24 blocks.
5. At half health (40) he rages, and he stays enraged even if healed. You hear the brute's angry sound.
6. While enraged he has Speed II, and hits do not shove him.
7. While enraged, if a wall blocks the way to the player, he breaks it. He does not dig before rage.
   Bedrock, obsidian, and portals stay. Broken blocks do not drop. Turn off mob griefing and he stops digging.
   An axe is slow on stone and fast on netherrack. 0.45 is a bit under half a player's speed.
8. Killing him gives 60 experience. The mod's double for other monsters does not add on top.
9. If a player landed the kill, a wither skeleton skull drops with the same chance as from a wither skeleton:
   2.5%, and Looting adds 1% per level (I is 3.5%, II is 4.5%, III is 5.5%).
   Another mob, lava, or a fall drops no skull.

The body is the Piglin Brute Remake by LaserFont0103 (Sketchfab, CC BY). The lantern was removed.
The axes are real items, drawn on the hands.


SETTINGS (HellBerserk.java)
----------------------------
NATURAL_SPAWN_CHANCE — 0.04 = 4%, about one in twenty-five. 0 = never on his own. 0.1 = one in ten.
MAX_HEALTH — 80 = 40 hearts.
ATTACK_DAMAGE — 7 from the body, before the axes. Each axe adds its own damage.
MOVE_SPEED — 0.35 before rage. Same as a brute.
FOLLOW_RANGE — 24 blocks.
KNOCKBACK_RESISTANCE — 0.5 before rage. A hit moves him half as far. 0 = a normal shove. 1 = no shove.
RAGE_HEALTH_FRACTION — 0.5 = rage at half health.
RAGE_SPEED_AMPLIFIER — 1 = Speed II. 0 = Speed I.
RAGE_KNOCKBACK_RESISTANCE — 1.0 = hits do not shove him during rage.
DIG_SPEED_MULTIPLIER — 0.45 = how fast he breaks a block in rage, compared with a player holding that axe.
EXPERIENCE_REWARD — 60 for the kill. The usual monster double does not add on top.
SKULL_DROP_CHANCE — 0.025 = 2.5%, a wither skeleton skull when a player kills him. 0 = never.
SKULL_LOOTING_BONUS — 0.01 = 1% more per Looting level. 0 = Looting does not help.

Armor crack uses the brute's numbers in PiglinBruteArmorHit.java: 5% of damaging hits, half the bar, 10 seconds of nausea.

Hand position of the axes: HellBerserkAxeLayer.java, the OFFSET and ROT numbers.
