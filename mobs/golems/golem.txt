DAMAGED GOLEM
==================

Type: iron golem hostile to the player from spawn.
Code: golem/DamagedGolem.java (health, cracks, hit, command),
     spawn/NaturalMonsterSwap.java (shared roll with floating tool),
     mixin/NaturalSpawnerFloatingToolMixin.java (night spawn),
     client/golem/DamagedGolemRenderer.java (texture with black eyes).
Texture: assets/evolving-threats/textures/entity/damaged_golem.png


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Overworld at night, same places as zombies and skeletons. Not closer than 24 blocks to player.
   Chance NATURAL_SPAWN_CHANCE = 0.002 (0.2%, one in five hundred). FIXED from day one, not scale.
   Shared roll with floating tool: first 5% tool, next 0.2% golem.
2. If ceiling too low for 2.7-block golem, normal mob spawns there instead.
3. Sees player — attacks. Does not protect village or give flowers.
   Iron ingot does not repair it.
   Zombies, skeletons, etc. do not aggro: not a target for them.
   Village iron golem still aggro’d as before.
4. Hit like normal iron golem.
5. Health 100 (50 hearts) even while looking broken.
6. Eyes fully black. Cracks from CRACK_LEVEL: 3 = heaviest.
7. Does not spawn in Nether or End. Despawns in peaceful.
8. No iron drop on death — otherwise night would be an iron farm.


COMMAND
-------
  /etgolem
  /evolvingthreats spawn golem
Test spawn, not natural. Appears 6 blocks ahead on ground and attacks.
Does not roll 0.2% chance. Operator rights required.


SETTINGS (DamagedGolem.java)
-----------------------------
  NATURAL_SPAWN_CHANCE ..... 0.002 — 0.2%, one in 500. 0 = command only
  MAX_HEALTH ............... 100 — 50 hearts
  ATTACK_DAMAGE ............ 15 — like normal golem
  FOLLOW_RANGE ............. 24 blocks
  CRACK_LEVEL .............. 3 heavy, 2 medium, 1 slight, 0 intact
  EXPERIENCE_REWARD ........ 10 before multiplier, 20 from mob
